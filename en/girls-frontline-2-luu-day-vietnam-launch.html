Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium Officially Launches in Vietnam

THS Game officially launches the Vietnamese server for Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium featuring full Vietnamese localization, dynamic 3D cover mechanics, and generous launch rewards.

Home › Gaming › Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium Launches in Vietnam
Mobile / PC · Tactical RPG · News 2026-08-18 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
THS Game officially launches the Vietnamese server for Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium featuring full Vietnamese localization, dynamic 3D cover mechanics, and generous launch rewards.
Vietnam launch confirmed for August 27, 2026
THS Game released Girls' Frontline 2: Lưu Đày in Vietnam at 10:00 a.m. (GMT+7), with cross-platform support for Android, iOS and PC.

Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium now has a dedicated Vietnamese release. Local publisher THS Game confirmed that Girls' Frontline 2: Lưu Đày opened on August 27, 2026, giving players in Vietnam an officially supported version of MICA Team's 3D tactical role-playing game.

The launch matters because the international version had already built a sizable audience, while Vietnamese players were waiting for a locally operated release. The official announcement confirms access through Android, iOS and a Windows PC client, as well as cross-platform play between those versions.

What the Vietnamese version includes

The Vietnamese release keeps the core structure of Exilium: players command a squad of T-Dolls on grid-based battlefields, choose firing positions and use cover, elevation and character abilities to control each encounter. The sequel moves the series into fully 3D environments while retaining the squad-building and character-collection systems associated with the franchise.

THS Game's launch notice specifically confirms cross-platform access. Players can select the version appropriate for their device from the official Vietnamese website, then continue playing on supported mobile and PC platforms. Availability of account linking or progress synchronization should be checked in the publisher's current account guidance, because those details can change after launch.

How the tactical combat works

Combat is turn-based rather than real-time. Each unit has its own movement range, weapon role and skills, so positioning is as important as raw damage. Cover can reduce incoming pressure, while flanking an enemy or breaking its stability can create a safer opening for the rest of the squad.

The terrain also affects decisions. High ground, destructible objects and environmental hazards can change which route is safest. That makes the game closer to a compact tactical puzzle than a traditional automatic mobile RPG: a strong roster helps, but poor positioning can still cost a mission.

Where to download and get support

Players should use the official Vietnamese website or the verified mobile store listings rather than third-party APK sites. THS Game also provides a customer-support center and the email address listed in its launch announcement for installation, account or payment problems.

Before downloading the PC client, check the current storage and system requirements on the official page. Mobile users should also confirm that the store listing is published by THS Game Joint Stock Company. These checks reduce the risk of installing an unofficial package that may contain modified files.

What new players should know

Girls' Frontline 2 is set after the original game, but the early chapters introduce the Commander and the new situation gradually. Newcomers can follow the main plot without memorizing every event from the first title, while returning players will recognize organizations, terminology and recurring characters.

The official Vietnamese launch gives local players a clearer route to downloads, localized communication and regional support. For the latest maintenance notices, event schedules and reward details, use THS Game's news page because time-limited campaigns may differ from older international-server guides.

Sources: THS Game launch announcement and the verified Vietnamese Google Play listing.
