Tokyo Game Show 2026: The Biggest Announcements, 257,642 Attendees

Tokyo Game Show 2026 ran September 17–20 at Makuhari Messe with 257,642 attendees, headlined by Ace Combat 8, Crazy Taxi: World Tour, Gachiakuta: Breakout and STRANGER THAN HEAVEN.

Gaming 2026-09-23 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Tokyo Game Show 2026 ran September 17–20 at Makuhari Messe with 257,642 attendees, headlined by Ace Combat 8, Crazy Taxi: World Tour, Gachiakuta: Breakout and STRANGER THAN HEAVEN.

Tokyo Game Show 2026, one of Asia’s biggest game expos, closed after four days from September 17 to 20, 2026 at Makuhari Messe in Japan. This year’s show drew 257,642 in-person attendees, slightly down from 263,101 last year but still showing the event’s pull.

Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve — launches October 2, 2026

Bandai Namco made a splash by announcing Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve, playable throughout the event, with an official release date of October 2, 2026 — just after TGS closed.

SEGA shows Crazy Taxi: World Tour and Gachiakuta: Breakout

SEGA brought two notable announcements. Crazy Taxi: World Tour revives the classic arcade brand with an open Japan map, due in 2027 on current platforms. Gachiakuta: Breakout turns the hit anime about trash and hidden value into an action RPG with survival elements, also due in 2027.

STRANGER THAN HEAVEN set for January 15, 2027

SEGA and Ryu Ga Gotoku Studio (the team behind Like a Dragon/Yakuza) announced the release date for STRANGER THAN HEAVEN: January 15, 2027. The game had a playable demo, stage presentations and a dedicated content area throughout the show.

  • Dates: September 17–20, 2026
  • Venue: Makuhari Messe, Japan
  • Attendance: 257,642 (last year: 263,101)
  • Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve: launches October 2, 2026
  • Crazy Taxi: World Tour & Gachiakuta: Breakout: due 2027
  • STRANGER THAN HEAVEN: launches January 15, 2027

Fifth day canceled by a typhoon

TGS 2026 was originally scheduled to run five days for the first time, but the fifth day (September 21) was canceled due to Typhoon No. 25. The 257,642 figure is the total for the four days from September 17–20.

Playable games and showcases

  • Playable: Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance, Final Fantasy VII Revelation, Marvel’s Wolverine and Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve
  • GTA VI: not playable, but PlayStation dedicated a theater to extended presentations of the game
  • Koei Tecmo: made Attack on Titan 3 its centerpiece, due December 10, 2026 on PS5, Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S and PC

Sources: Anime News Network, GameDaily, Shacknews, Nikkei.
