Honkai: Star Rail 3.0: HoYoverse Unveils New World Amphoreus & Memory Resonance System

HoYoverse officially reveals Honkai: Star Rail 3.0, introducing the Greco-Roman world Amphoreus (Eternal Land), the brand-new Remembrance combat mechanic, and 5-star character lineups.

Key Highlights
HoYoverse has officially detailed the next major chapter of Honkai: Star Rail Version 3.0, bringing the Astral Express crew to Amphoreus: The Eternal Land. The update introduces the novel Path of Remembrance combat path, extensive graphical enhancements, and an ambitious lineup of new 5-star characters.

During a worldwide special broadcast, developer HoYoverse revealed comprehensive details for Honkai: Star Rail 3.0, commencing the Astral Express's most monumental interstellar expedition yet into the mythical civilization of Amphoreus: The Eternal Land.

1. Amphoreus: The Eternal Realm & The Tri-Realm Conflict

Departing the eventful dreamscape of Penacony, the Astral Express navigates uncharted cosmic currents to arrive at Amphoreus — an ancient civilization steeped in Greco-Roman classical mythology. Long isolated by impenetrable mists of memory, Amphoreus features three prominent factions: the Celestial Sanctuary of Okhema, the Ashen Valley of Castrum Kremnos, and the Abyssal Memory Core.

Players will traverse grand marble acropolises, decipher celestial astrolabes, and uncover centuries-old secrets buried beneath divine temples.

2. The Path of Remembrance & Spirit Summoning Mechanics

Version 3.0 introduces the playable Path of Remembrance to the combat roster for both the Trailblazer and newly featured allies:

  • Action Bar Spirit Summons: Remembrance combatants summon independent tactical spirits onto the Action Bar, executing supplemental attacks and crowd-control effects.
  • True Memory & Frost Synergy: Skills trigger "Glacial Memory" debuffs, bypassing conventional elemental resistances on elite bosses.
  • New Light Cones & Relic Sets: Specialized gear tailored for high effect hit rates and rapid ultimate energy regeneration.

3. 5-Star Character Banners & Version 3.0 Roadmap

The initial 3.0 banners introduce key heroic figures central to the Amphoreus chronicle:

  • Aglaea (5-Star - Lightning / Remembrance): Okhema's master weaver who weaves golden fate threads to bind foes and execute area-wide electro strikes.
  • Cyrene / The Maiden (5-Star - Ice / Remembrance): A mystic maiden granting potent critical damage amplifications and team-wide energy restoration.
  • Trailblazer (Remembrance Form): A completely new combat stance featuring stunning cinematic ultimate animations.
