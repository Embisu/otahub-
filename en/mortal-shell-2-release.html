Mortal Shell II Set Release Date August 20, 2026

Cold Symmetry announced that Mortal Shell II will launch globally on August 20, 2026 on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, bringing a completely new "posture" combat system and 8 transformable Shells.

OtaHub › Gaming › Mortal Shell II Set Release Date August 20, 2026
Gaming 2026-08-17 5 minutes reading OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Mortal Shell II, the standalone sequel to the popular action-RPG Soulslike, will launch globally on 20/8/2026 above PC (Steam), PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, starting price 49.99 USD. The game introduces a "posture" combat system that does not depend on physical strength, allowing players to transform into themselves 8 Shells different, explore more 60 dungeons in a tightly interconnected open world.

Developer Cold Symmetry same publisher Playstack officially confirmed Mortal Shell II will launch globally on the day 20/8/2026, present at the same time on PC (Steam), PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This is a standalone sequel to the Soulslike game that is highly appreciated by hardcore gamers thanks to its condensed gameplay and characteristic dark atmosphere.

Completely new "posture" combat system

The biggest highlight of Mortal Shell II is the combat-based system "posture" (Standing/Position), completely replacing the familiar stamina limitation mechanism of Soulslike games. The new mechanism allows players to freely combine many different fighting styles to break enemy stances and deliver fatal blows, instead of having to calculate every breath as before. In addition, the game adds an element stealth and options Ranged weapons/sidearms, along with a series of brutal finishing moves designed specifically for each weapon type.

8 Shells, more than 60 dungeons

Players will be transformed into 8 Shells different, each Shell has its own unique fighting style and stats, explore a compact open world but closely linked to More than 60 dungeons hide secrets. Familiar creatures such as Wormfish will also return, continuing the brand's signature dark world.

Price and special editions

Standard version priced 49.99 USD / 49.99 EUR / 39.99 GBP. In addition, Devout Edition Comes with Obsidian skin set for all 8 Shells and early access 72 hours before the official release date. On PS5 there is also a limited physical version Revered Edition, including an artbook, steelcase, premium art set, additional digital content, and two pre-order exclusive Harbinger skins.

  • Launch date: 20/8/2026
  • Platform: PC (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X|S
  • Developer: Cold Symmetry
  • Publisher: Playstack
  • Standard price: 49.99 USD
  • Content: 8 playable Shells, 60+ dungeons
