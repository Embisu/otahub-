1. The Cursed Prince Loki Unchained

One Piece Chapter 1193, titled "A Vow Beneath the Yggdrasil," finally unravels the biggest mystery surrounding Prince Loki — a man held captive by massive sea-stone chains for years. The chapter continues the flashback into Loki's trauma and King Harald's tragic love story, revealing why the kingdom of Elbaf once stood on the brink of collapse.

2. Luffy, Loki, And Hajrudin Against Imu

In the present timeline, Luffy, Loki, and the giant Hajrudin are confronted by a menacing new form of Imu, the shadow ruler of the Ancient World. For the first time, readers witness Imu's true power on the page directly, rather than through rumor and implication.

3. The Beginning Of The Final War For One Piece

With new clues surfacing about Uranus — the last of the Ancient Weapons yet to be revealed — Eiichiro Oda is pushing the pace at Elbaf to its most intense point yet, signaling this arc as the decisive turning point for the rest of the series.
