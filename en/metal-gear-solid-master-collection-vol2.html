Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 Set Launch Date August 27

Konami confirms Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 launches August 27, 2026, bringing MGS4: Guns of the Patriots to modern platforms for the first time with Peace Walker HD and Ghost Babel.

OtaHub › Gaming › Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 Set Launch Date August 27
Gaming 2026-08-12 6 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2 will be released on August 27, 2026, on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, Switch, and Steam for $49.99. The collection includes MGS4: Guns of the Patriots (playable on modern consoles for the first time), Peace Walker HD with 6-player online co-op, and the spin-off game Ghost Babel and a digital soundtrack.

Almost two decades. It's about time Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots was previously a PS3 exclusive, forcing anyone not owning that console to watch YouTube videos to see how Solid Snake, now an aging character nicknamed Old Snake, ends his journey. That's about to change. Konami has just officially confirmed the release date for Metal Gear Solid: Master Collection Vol. 2, the sequel to the effort to revive the legacy created by Hideo Kojima, and this time the collection is aimed squarely at the period many consider the emotional and technological peak of the series.

Launch date and price

The collection will be available on shelves 27/8/2026 for $49.99, released simultaneously on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, original Switch and Steam. Pre-orders were available for sale right from the moment of publication, for both physical and digital versions.

MGS4 finally exits the PS3

Without exaggeration, this is the reason why Vol. 2 became the center of attention. Metal Gear Solid 4: Guns of the Patriots, the grand finale of the Solid Snake arc, which caused a sensation in 2008 thanks to its cinematic quality, a rarity at the time, is making its first appearance outside of the PlayStation platform. For those who played the original on PS3 more than ten years ago, this is an opportunity to relive one of the series' most memorable moments, now running more smoothly on modern hardware. And for the generation of players who have never touched a PS3, this is ultimately their first chance to understand why MGS4 is still spoken of with such reverence.

Peace Walker returns, bringing with him the legacy of Mother Base

Metal Gear Solid: Peace Walker is also included in the familiar HD Collection, and more importantly, the online co-op feature for up to 6 players remains. Many long-time fans will surely remember the original PSP version that created a sensation thanks to its Mother Base recruitment and base building mechanism, an idea that seemed small but was actually the direct foundation for the massive Mother Base system later in Metal Gear Solid V.

  • MGS4: Guns of the Patriots, the original, for the first time on a modern platform
  • Peace Walker (HD Collection), online co-op for up to 6 players
  • Metal Gear: Ghost Babel, bonus content
  • Digital Soundtrack Vol. 2 along with digitized screenplay and master book

Right time to pay attention

The timing of its release is also noteworthy. Master Collection Vol. 2 appeared right when the community was still endlessly discussing the future of the series after Metal Gear Solid Delta: Snake Eater, the warmly received remake from last year. The fact that MGS4 finally escaped the shadow of its old console exclusivity shows that Konami is serious about bringing the entire Metal Gear Solid legacy closer to players across all platforms, instead of leaving the most important chapters of the series forever stuck on discontinued hardware.

49.99 USD for two games that shaped an entire generation of gamers, plus accompanying bonus content, is an acceptable price for fans of this classic stealth game series. As for MGS4 specifically, August 27th will mark the moment it officially breaks free from the exclusivity that has haunted it for nearly two decades.
