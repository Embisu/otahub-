Honkai: Star Rail Announces Version 3.1: Amphoreus Realm Expansion & 5-Star Cyrene Banner

HoYoverse announces Honkai: Star Rail Version 3.1 on Mobile and PC, adding the Eternal Pantheon on Amphoreus and 5-Star Nihility Imaginary character Cyrene.

Home › Gaming › Honkai: Star Rail Announces Version 3.1: Amphoreus Realm Expansion & 5-Star Cyrene Banner
Gaming 2026-08-24 8 min read OtaHub Editorial
Version 3.1 Overview
HoYoverse has hosted its Special Program for Honkai: Star Rail 3.1: The Undying Song of the Stars. Expanding the narrative on mythical planet Amphoreus, introducing 5-Star character Cyrene (Imaginary · Nihility), and gifting 10 Star Rail Special Passes to all mobile players.

Following the Astral Express's arrival on the mythical planet Amphoreus, HoYoverse elevates the storyline in Version 3.1 with dedicated mobile optimization for iOS and Android.

1. 5-Star Character: Cyrene (Imaginary · Nihility)

As the high priestess guarding the Temporal Shrine on Amphoreus, Cyrene serves as a cornerstone for DoT and Super Break teams, triggering immediate 120% DoT detonation and area-of-effect Imaginary weakness implants.

2. New Biome: Eternal Pantheon on Amphoreus

Explore Greek-mythological architectural marvels, solve light reflection mirrors, and challenge weekly boss Khronos the Revelation Beast.
