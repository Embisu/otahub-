Chainsaw Man Movie: Reze Arc – MAPPA Unveils New Trailer, Theme Song & Global Theatrical Release Date

MAPPA officially releases a breathtaking trailer for the upcoming Chainsaw Man: Reze Arc movie, revealing the new theme song and global theatrical release schedule.

🕒 Sep 07, 2026 ✍️ Tik (Khanh Quynh) ⏱️ 6 min read
⚡ Key Movie Highlights
  • Official Trailer: MAPPA reveals explosive Sakuga animation showcasing Denji and Reze's tragic yet exhilarating romance.
  • Theme Song Preview: Teaser features an emotional new original theme song tailored specifically for the Reze Arc.
  • Cinematic Quality: Mastered in native 4K HDR and custom formatted for IMAX and Dolby Cinema screenings.
  • Release Schedule: Premiering in Japanese theaters followed by global theatrical distribution across North America, Europe, and Asia.

1. The Explosive Entrance of the Bomb Devil (Reze)

Following months of eager anticipation, premier Japanese animation studio MAPPA has officially unveiled the full two-minute theatrical trailer for Chainsaw Man: The Movie – Reze Arc. Adapting one of the most celebrated story arcs by creator Tatsuki Fujimoto, the feature film follows Denji's fateful encounter with Reze, an enigmatic cafe worker who captures his heart.

The trailer contrasts tender, atmospheric moments of nighttime romance with breathtaking, visceral Sakuga action as Reze's true identity as the lethal Bomb Devil is unleashed across Tokyo.

2. Elite Production Team & 4K IMAX Visual Mastery

Crafted exclusively for the cinematic screen, Reze Arc features upgraded fluid hand-drawn animation that surpasses Season 1. MAPPA's veteran animators have integrated dynamic volumetric lighting and advanced particle FX to deliver earth-shattering pyrotechnic clashes.

3. Original Soundtrack & Global Box-Office Projections

Coupled with jaw-dropping animation, the teaser offers a sneak peek into the hauntingly beautiful theme song. Industry analysts project Reze Arc to easily cross $100M at the worldwide box office, solidifying Chainsaw Man as an undisputed global titan alongside Jujutsu Kaisen 0 and Mugen Train.
