Chainsaw Man Manga Review: The Complete Journey From Denji To Asa Mitaka

From the poor young man who merges with a chainsaw demon to the Demon Academy and Demon War arcs, Chainsaw Man concludes a journey unlike any other manga.

Home › Manga › Chainsaw Man Manga Review: From Denji To Asa Mitaka
Reviews · Manga 2026-08-16 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Chainsaw Man Tatsuki Fujimoto's has officially concluded after Part 2 was completed, with confirmation that no Part 3 is planned. From Denji's beginning journey with the demon Pochita in Part 1, to the arc of Asa Mitaka and the War Devil in Part 2, the manga has brought to a close one of the boldest and most unpredictable action-horror stories in modern shounen/seinen.

Rarely does any manga maintain such absolute unpredictability Chainsaw Man by Tatsuki Fujimoto throughout its long journey, and now, when the series has officially ended with confirmation that there will be no Part 3, this is the right time to look back at the entire work.

Part 1: foundation with blood and chainsaw

Part 1 introduces Denji, a poor young man forced to pay off his debts by hunting demons with his companion Pochita, a chainsaw demon. After being betrayed and killed, Denji merges with Pochita to be resurrected as Chainsaw Man, opening up a world where any character can die unexpectedly, and readers' expectations are constantly being overturned.

Part 2: Asa Mitaka and the War Demon

Part 2 shifts the story to Asa Mitaka, a high school girl who inadvertently merges with War Devil, one of the most dangerous demons in the world of Chainsaw Man. The Academy Saga arc and what follows take the story to darker lands, while revealing new layers of meaning for the universe that Fujimoto built from Part 1.

End, no Part 3

After Part 2 was completed, Tatsuki Fujimoto and the publisher officially confirmed that there would be no Part 3, concluding the series as a complete work instead of extending indefinitely-a rare decision, but one that aligns with the uncompromising, fearless spirit that Chainsaw Man has pursued throughout its journey.

  • Author: Tatsuki Fujimoto
  • Part 1: Denji & Pochita, starting Chainsaw Man
  • Part 2: Asa Mitaka & War Devil, Demon Academy
  • Condition: Completed, confirmed no Part 3

Score: 9.4/10, Masterpiece

Chainsaw Man concludes as one of the boldest and most unique action-horror manga of its generation, not compromising on reader expectations until the very last page. Tatsuki Fujimoto's decision not to extend it to Part 3 only further confirms the skill of an author who knows when to stop at the right time.
