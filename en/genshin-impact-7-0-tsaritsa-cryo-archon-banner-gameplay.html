Genshin Impact 7.0 Snezhnaya: Tsaritsa Kit Revealed, Cryo Archon Banner & Frostbite Reactions

HoYoverse reveals Tsaritsa Cryo Archon kit, new Frostbite elemental temperature reactions, and Version 7.0 Snezhnaya banner schedules.

Home › Gaming › Genshin Impact 7.0 Snezhnaya: Tsaritsa Kit Revealed, Cryo Archon Banner & Frostbite Reactions
Gaming 2026-09-07 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
⚡ Genshin Impact 7.0 & Tsaritsa Key Highlights
  • Cryo Archon Tsaritsa Kit: 5-Star Cryo Catalyst wielder creating Eternal Glacial Domains that amplify elemental Critical Damage by 100%.
  • New Frostbite Temperature Reactions: Revolutionary reaction mechanics interacting with Hydro, Pyro, and Electro under sub-zero conditions.
  • Expansive Snezhnaya Map: Explore the royal Zapolyarny Palace, subterranean Fatui industrial hubs, and dynamic blizzard survival zones.
  • Version 7.0 Banner Schedule: Phase 1 features Tsaritsa alongside Sandrone, followed by Capitano and Columbina in Phase 2.

Following the blazing triumphs in Natlan, developer HoYoverse has officially unveiled the landmark update Genshin Impact Version 7.0: Snezhnaya – The Realm of Frost, alongside full gameplay details for the final sovereign of Teyvat: The Cryo Archon, Tsaritsa.

Tsaritsa Skill Kit Breakdown – The Ultimate Cryo Catalyst Archon

Wielding a Cryo Catalyst with regal poise, Tsaritsa introduces a versatile playstyle that seamlessly transitions between off-field elemental application and hyper-scaling team amplification:

  • Elemental Skill (E) – Scepter of Permafrost: Summons three orbital ice crystals that unleash automated Cryo volleys while continuously applying strong Cryo aura even when off-field.
  • Elemental Burst (Q) – Realm of Zapolyarny: Expands an absolute glacial domain that suspends enemies in temporal frost for 3 seconds, granting a 100% Critical Damage bonus to all party members against Cryo-afflicted foes.
  • Ascension Passives: Reduces all elemental resistances of enemies within the domain by 30% and generates regenerative Cryo shields upon triggering reactions.

For fellow tactical gacha enthusiasts, explore our in-depth Girls Frontline 2 Beginner & Squad Guide and check out Honkai Star Rail 3.0 Amphoreus Update.

Revolutionary Thermal Mechanics: Frostbite & Sublimation

Version 7.0 enhances Teyvat's elemental combat with new Thermal Reaction Mechanics tailored to sub-zero environments:

  • Frostbite (Cryo + Electro/Hydro under sub-zero temp): Shatters enemy defenses, imposing a 40% DEF shred for 12 seconds.
  • Sublimation (Flash Cryo + Pyro): Converts standard melt into pressurized superheated steam bursts dealing cascading AoE True Damage.

Version 7.0 Banner Roadmap

HoYoverse confirmed an unprecedented 6-week banner schedule for the launch of Snezhnaya:

  • Phase 1: Tsaritsa (5-Star Cryo) debuting alongside Sandrone / Marionette (5-Star Geo).
  • Phase 2: The highly anticipated return of Capitano paired with Columbina / Damselette.
