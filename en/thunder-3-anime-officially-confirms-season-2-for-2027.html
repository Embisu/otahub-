Thunder 3 Season 2 Set for 2027: Gantz Creator’s Anime

Thunder 3 is getting a second season in 2027. The news landed on October 1, hours after episode 12 closed out Season 1, and exactly a week after Hiroya Oku admitted he was the real author.

Summary
The Thunder 3 (サンダー3) anime will return for Season 2 in 2027. The announcement came on October 1, 2026, right after episode 12 ended Season 1 on Fuji TV’s +Ultra block and Netflix. The manga was drawn by Gantz creator Hiroya Oku under the pen name Yuki Ikeda, which he only confirmed on September 24. No exact premiere date or new cast has been announced.

Thunder 3 is getting a second season in 2027. The anime staff announced it on October 1, just hours after episode 12 closed out Season 1 on Fuji TV and Netflix. The timing is neat: exactly a week earlier, Gantz creator Hiroya Oku admitted that the “Yuki Ikeda” on the manga’s cover was him.

Thunder 3 Season 2: what’s been announced

The announcement was short: a visual reading “SEASON2 2027年 放送決定” (broadcast confirmed for 2027) and a celebration illustration drawn by the author. Season 1 premiered at 24:45 on July 8 Japan time (effectively 12:45 a.m. on July 9) on Fuji TV’s +Ultra block, and streamed worldwide exclusively on Netflix. Episode 12, the finale, aired in the late-night slot on September 30.

What hasn’t been announced: an exact date, the broadcaster, whether Netflix keeps exclusive rights, and whether the cast or staff will change. There is no new trailer for Season 2 yet, only the announcement visual below.

The pen name twist: Yuki Ikeda is Hiroya Oku

For four years Thunder 3 carried the name of an unknown author, Yuki Ikeda. On September 24, 2026, Hiroya Oku confirmed on X that it was his pen name, opening with something along the lines of “as everyone knows.” That phrasing suggests it was a secret plenty of readers had already worked out.

His reason is very Oku: the first chapter has a twist, and if readers saw Hiroya Oku on the cover they’d see it coming. He wanted first-time readers to be genuinely surprised. Coming from the creator of Gantz, Inuyashiki and GIGANT, that says a lot about how much he cares about the reading experience.

There’s also a slightly bittersweet note. In an August 2026 interview, Oku said he plans to retire after finishing his latest manga, The Sweet Sixty Princess. That makes the Thunder 3 anime one of the last adaptations we’re likely to get from a new Oku series.

What Thunder 3 is about

Three ordinary schoolboys, Pyontaro Tezuka, Hiroshi Ochanomizu and Tsubame Azuma, get hold of a DVD that supposedly opens a gateway to a parallel world. While their backs are turned, Pyontaro’s little sister Futaba slips through and is kidnapped by the aliens who have taken over that version of Earth.

What makes Thunder 3 instantly recognisable is the clash of art styles. The three boys are drawn in a round, big-eyed style straight out of Showa-era children’s manga, while the other world is drawn in the cold, realistic style Gantz fans will know. The anime keeps that contrast, and it’s a big reason the show doesn’t look like anything else this season. To keep things fresh for newcomers, we won’t spoil the first-chapter twist.

Voice cast and staff

  • Pyontaro Tezuka: Sayumi Suzushiro
  • Hiroshi Ochanomizu: Eri Akiyama
  • Tsubame Azuma: Natsumi Kawaida
  • Futaba Tezuka: Honoka Mitsubachi
  • Doc: Shogo Sakata

The anime is produced by studio Unend. Hiroyuki Seshita, co-director of Netflix’s anime Godzilla trilogy, is chief director, with Keisuke Ide directing. Series composition and scripts are by Hiroshi Seko, a familiar name to Chainsaw Man fans. Character designs are by Junko Yamanaka and Yūki Moriyama, based on original concepts by Naoyuki Onda. The music is by Frédéric Chateau and Akiyuki Tateyama. The opening theme is “Thunderbolt” by Yuuri and the ending theme is “Shururerira” by Otoha.

Why the manga caught on

Thunder 3 ran in Kodansha’s Monthly Shonen Magazine from May 6, 2022 to June 5, 2026 and was collected in 10 volumes, the last released on August 17, 2026. Kodansha USA has published it in English since November 2024.

Even before anyone knew Oku was behind it, the series got noticed. It was nominated for the 2022 Next Manga Award (print category) and the 2023 eBook Initiative Japan Manga Award, and placed fourth on Kono Manga ga Sugoi! 2024’s list for male readers.

Where to watch Thunder 3

All 12 episodes of Season 1 are on Netflix as a worldwide exclusive. If you’d rather read ahead, the manga is complete at 10 volumes.

OtaHub’s take

Thunder 3 has an advantage few multi-season anime get: the source material is finished. Season 2 doesn’t have to chase the manga, doesn’t need an anime-original ending, and the staff know exactly where the story lands. Add Hiroshi Seko, who has plenty of experience adapting violent, twist-heavy manga, and Season 2 is on solid ground.

What we’re most curious about is whether the anime can hold on to that art-style contrast as the story gets darker. It’s what makes Thunder 3 different, and it’s also the first thing to get flattened under a tight production schedule. 2027 is a while away, so if you haven’t started, now is a good time to catch up on Season 1.

Thunder 3 Season 2 FAQ

When does Thunder 3 Season 2 come out?

Thunder 3 Season 2 is confirmed for 2027. The exact premiere date, broadcaster and streaming platform have not been announced yet.

Where can I watch Thunder 3 Season 1?

Season 1 has 12 episodes and streams exclusively worldwide on Netflix, starting July 8, 2026. In Japan it aired on Fuji TV’s +Ultra block.

Who is the author of Thunder 3?

Thunder 3 is credited to Yuki Ikeda, a pen name of Hiroya Oku, the creator of Gantz, Inuyashiki and GIGANT. He confirmed it on X on September 24, 2026.

Has the Thunder 3 manga ended?

Yes. It ran in Kodansha’s Monthly Shonen Magazine from May 6, 2022 to June 5, 2026 and spans 10 volumes. The final volume came out on August 17, 2026.

Is Thunder 3 like Gantz?

It shares the feeling of ordinary people thrown into a brutal world where they have to survive. But Thunder 3 is built around a parallel Earth overrun by aliens, with three schoolboys trying to rescue the hero’s little sister.
