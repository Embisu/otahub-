Speed & Accuracy
OtaHub is committed to reporting news as quickly as possible, but not at the expense of accuracy. Every news story is verified from multiple sources before publication. We update and correct publicly when there are errors.
Asian perspective
We don't just translate news from the West. OtaHub focuses on the Asian perspective and context, where gaming, anime and manga are not just entertainment but a profound part of culture. This is our core difference.
Value core
01
Independent & Non-biased
Does not receive sponsorship from the publisher to write reviews. No hidden ads. Our views are the views of the community.
02
In-depth
We don't write to get views. Every review, every analysis is based on many hours of actual gaming or watching anime.
03
Community first
OtaHub is built by fans for fans. All content decisions are based on actual reader feedback and needs.
04
Continuously updated
Asian game and anime news moves very quickly. The OtaHub team is available 16/7 to ensure you never miss a thing.
05
Multilingual
