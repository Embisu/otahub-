Chainsaw Man Season 2 “Assassins Arc”: MAPPA Begins Full Production

MAPPA officially launches production on Chainsaw Man Season 2 adapting the International Assassins arc following Reze Arc's $191.4M global box office surge.

Home › Anime › Chainsaw Man Season 2 “Assassins Arc”
Anime 2026-09-26 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Following the monumental global box office success of the Reze Arc feature film, MAPPA has officially commenced production on Chainsaw Man Season 2, adapting the chaotic and visceral International Assassins arc.

At the annual Jump Festa expo, animation studio MAPPA officially confirmed that television project Chainsaw Man Season 2, subtitled the Assassins Arc, has entered intensive pre-production and active animation scheduling. Serving as a direct continuation of the theatrical feature Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc, the upcoming television run plunges viewers into the most destabilizing, darkly comedic, and brutal conflict across the entirety of Part 1 in Tatsuki Fujimoto's original manga serialization.

1. The Strategic $191M Reze Arc Catalyst and Transition into Season 2

The decision to adapt the Bomb Girl arc (Reze Arc) into a theatrical feature film prior to advancing television broadcast seasons has proven to be an exceptional commercial triumph for MAPPA. Surpassing $191.4 million in worldwide box office receipts—bolstered by a staggering 1.25 billion yen domestic opening weekend in Japan—the cinematic release reinforced Denji's cross-demographic cultural footprint while generating substantial capital reserves earmarked for reinvestment into Season 2.

Clarifying the naming hierarchy as "Season 2" rectifies widespread online speculation incorrectly cataloging the production as "Season 3" (a colloquial misinterpretation counting the movie as an entire season). The official narrative lineage remains strictly structured: Season 1 (episodes 1–12), the Reze Arc theatrical bridge, and Season 2 adapting the International Assassins saga.

2. The International Assassins Arc: Global Bounties on Denji's Heart

In the aftermath of harrowing skirmishes across Tokyo that publicly exposed the Chainsaw Devil's presence to international intelligence agencies, foreign governments recognize the existential threat and geopolitical leverage concealed within Denji's hybrid biology. A covert global bounty is placed upon Denji's physical heart (Pochita), drawing elite mercenary squads into Japanese sovereign territory.

Metropolitan Tokyo rapidly devolves into an urban war zone. Public Safety devil hunters led by Makima are tasked with fortifying 24-hour perimeter security around Denji as specialized operatives arrive simultaneously from the Soviet Union, the United States, Germany, and China. Layering psychological subterfuge, urban ambushes, and cosmic dread stemming from Primal Fears residing in Hell, the arc escalates suspense to unprecedented heights.

3. Elite Mercenaries Enter the Fray: Quanxi, Santa Claus, and Hirofumi Yoshida

The defining appeal of the Assassins Arc centers around its eclectic roster of morally ambiguous antagonists and specialized combatants:

  • Quanxi (The First Devil Hunter): Renowned as humanity's earliest devil hunter hailing from China, Quanxi wields near-instantaneous blinding sword draw speed alongside a fiercely loyal collective of fiend companions. Her hand-to-hand clashes against Special Division agents remain among the most revered dynamic sequences in modern shonen manga.
  • Santa Claus: A shadowy operative representing German intelligence orchestrating grotesque puppet-flesh mechanics, ultimately contracting with the terrifying Darkness Devil in the depths of the Netherworld.
  • Hirofumi Yoshida: A calm high-school private devil hunter contracted with the Octopus Devil, drafted as an elite tactical bodyguard to neutralize rogue infiltrators.

4. MAPPA's 100% Self-Financed Production Model and Cinematic Anime Ambition

What sets the Chainsaw Man adaptation apart historically is its unique financial structure: MAPPA self-funded 100% of the production budget without relying on a conventional multi-corporate production committee. This bold operational autonomy affords director and animation heads complete artistic leeway to honor Fujimoto's uncompromising visual grotesque comedy, uncensored visceral combat, and idiosyncratic cinematic framing.

According to creative leads at MAPPA, Season 2 preserves the live-action aesthetic and atmospheric acoustic design that established Season 1 while incorporating fan feedback to sharpen kinetic pacing during full-speed animation cuts. While formal broadcast premiere windows remain unannounced—with industry analysts anticipating an early-to-mid 2027 arrival—Chainsaw Man Season 2 is undeniably positioned to establish an artistic benchmark for premier television animation.

Sources: Shueisha Jump Festa Official Announcements, MAPPA Corporate Releases, Anime News Network, Oricon News.
