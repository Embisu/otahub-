Remedy Expands the Control Universe: FBC: Firebreak and Control 2 In Development

Remedy Entertainment officially details FBC: Firebreak and full production on Control 2, expanding the Remedy Connected Universe with Annapurna.

Home › Gaming › Remedy Expands Control Universe
Gaming 2026-09-25 5 min read OtaHub Deep Dive
Key Takeaway
Finnish developer Remedy Entertainment has unveiled its comprehensive expansion roadmap for the Remedy Connected Universe (RCU), highlighting 3-player co-op shooter FBC: Firebreak and the full production status of Control 2. A strategic partnership with Annapurna Pictures co-finances the sequel while unlocking film and television adaptations for Alan Wake and Control.

Since its critically acclaimed release in 2019, Control redefined supernatural action-adventure and laid the cornerstone for the Remedy Connected Universe (RCU). Riding the momentum of Alan Wake 2's commercial and critical triumphs, Remedy Entertainment has outlined the future of the Federal Bureau of Control (FBC) with two distinct flagship titles: the cooperative shooter FBC: Firebreak and full-scale development of Control 2.

1. FBC: Firebreak – Cooperative Tactical Action Inside The Oldest House

Previously known under the internal codename Project Condor, FBC: Firebreak marks Remedy's first self-published multiplayer endeavor. Instead of a single-player journey, the game is built from the ground up as a 3-player cooperative first-person PvE shooter.

Set within the ever-shifting concrete corridors of The Oldest House, players suit up as First Responder agents of the FBC. Armed with specialized weaponry and bizarre paranatural equipment, teams must neutralize interdimensional breaches, quell hostile Altered World Events, and push back supernatural threats.

"FBC: Firebreak is not a watered-down spin-off or Control 2 lite. It is an independent, pick-up-and-play tactical experience engineered to let friends delve together into Remedy's weird fiction while maintaining intense team-based gameplay." — Thomas Puha, Communications Director at Remedy Entertainment

The game will release simultaneously across PC (Steam, Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S, launching Day One on PC Game Pass and PlayStation Plus Game Catalog.

2. Control 2 Enters Full Production as a Major Action-RPG

While FBC: Firebreak targets multiplayer fans, Remedy's flagship sequel Control 2 (codename Heron) has successfully completed its pre-production milestone and transitioned into full-scale active production.

According to Remedy's financial updates to shareholders, Control 2 is designed as a sprawling AAA Action-RPG. The game leverages the proprietary Northlight engine, pushing dynamic environmental destruction, telekinetic combat physics, and psychological atmosphere beyond anything seen in previous studio projects.

Game Director Mikael Kasurinen confirmed that Jesse Faden's story will delve deeper into the enigmatic origins of the Board, the Former entity, and unmapped thresholds of the paranormal realm.

3. Historic Deal with Annapurna Pictures Brings Control to Hollywood

A crucial catalyst behind Remedy's expansive ambition is a strategic co-financing and adaptation agreement with Annapurna Pictures. Under the agreement:

  • Production Co-Financing: Annapurna finances 50% of the production budget for Control 2, insulating Remedy against escalating development expenses.
  • Film & TV Extensions: Annapurna secures options to bring both Control and Alan Wake franchises to film, television, and cross-media entertainment.
  • Creative Ownership Retained: Remedy preserves 100% of the game IP rights and creative control over their interactive software.

4. The Expanding Horizon of the Remedy Connected Universe

The original Control achieved over 4.5 million copies sold and engaged more than 19 million players across subscription platforms, earning over 20 Game of the Year honors. Alongside the *Max Payne 1&2* remake in partnership with Rockstar Games, Remedy has solidified its reputation as one of the most distinctive independent auteurs in the global gaming industry.
