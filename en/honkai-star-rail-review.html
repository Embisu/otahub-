Honkai: Star Rail Review: A Turn-Based Symphony (9.2/10)

Honkai: Star Rail review: HoYoverse's free turn-based JRPG launched April 26, 2023, with deep Weakness Break combat and the Astral Express story; IGN 9/10, with gacha monetization.

Home › Reviews › Honkai: Star Rail Review: A Turn-Based Symphony (9.2/10)
Mobile / Turn-Based RPG · Review 2026-08-18 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Honkai: Star Rail review: HoYoverse's free turn-based JRPG launched April 26, 2023, with deep Weakness Break combat and the Astral Express story; IGN 9/10, with gacha monetization.

Honkai: Star Rail takes HoYoverse's gacha formula and gives it something the genre rarely offers: genuinely deep turn-based combat paired with a layered science-fiction story. The game is free, runs on mobile, PC and PS5, and IGN called it the best free-to-play game of 2023.

It launched on April 26, 2023 on Windows, iOS and Android, then reached PS5 on October 11, 2023.

Quick Facts

  • Release: April 26, 2023 (Windows, iOS, Android); October 11, 2023 (PS5)
  • Developer: miHoYo / HoYoverse; Unity engine; free-to-play gacha model
  • Genre: Turn-based JRPG with four-character parties
  • Critic scores: Metacritic 80 (PC), 82 (PS5); OpenCritic 90%; IGN 9/10; PC Gamer 90/100
  • Sales: Over 20 million downloads in the first week, over 100 million by February 2024

Story

Players take the role of the Trailblazer, an amnesiac carrying a Stellaron, who travels the universe aboard the Astral Express with March 7th, Dan Heng and Himeko. The game's world revolves around seven Paths, universal concepts presided over by powerful Aeons, with antagonists such as the Stellaron Hunters.

Each area has its own identity: the frozen planet Jarilo-VI draws on Russian architecture, the starship Xianzhou Luofu is East Asian silkpunk, Penacony blends the Roaring Twenties with classic Hollywood, and Amphoreus leans on Greco-Roman mythology.

Behind The Scenes

Honkai: Star Rail is developed by miHoYo (HoYoverse internationally) on Unity. Before launch it had 22.98 million pre-registrations in mainland China and 10 million globally, and passed 20 million downloads in its first week. By February 2024, total downloads exceeded 100 million.

The game regularly features major collaborations such as Fate/stay night (July 2025), Crunchyroll (October 2025, version 3.7) and Fortnite (February 2026).

Experience

Turn-based combat

Parties hold up to four characters, each with a combat Path and elemental type, plus Traces and Light Cone and Relic gear. The system revolves around exploiting enemy weaknesses and Weakness Break. Eurogamer praised the combat as magnificent, and Rock Paper Shotgun described it as deep yet approachable. Auto-battle and fast-forward help with repeated content.

Gacha, exploration and music

Characters and Light Cones are earned through Warps, using Stellar Jade that can be earned in-game or bought. Exploration areas are self-contained maps with treasure and puzzles rather than one large open world. Music is by HOYO-MiX with seven credited composers, alongside a multilingual voice cast.

Strengths

  • Deep yet approachable turn-based combat
  • Varied worlds and story, each planet with its own identity
  • Free to play across mobile, PC and PS5
  • Regular updates and major collaborations

Weaknesses

  • Still gacha, with progression tied to spending and luck
  • Some criticism that character designs lack creativity, especially female characters
  • Lengthy dialogue and exposition sometimes runs against a concise plot

What Critics Say

IGN scored it 9/10 and called it the best free-to-play game of 2023, PC Gamer gave 90/100 for its visual style and strategic depth, and Eurogamer praised the humor, voice cast and combat. Metacritic lists 80 on PC and 82 on PS5, with OpenCritic at 90%.

The game won Best Mobile Game at The Game Awards 2023, iPhone Game of the Year at the 2023 App Store Awards, and Best Game and Best Story at the 2023 Google Play Awards. In its first ten days, global revenue passed $100 million.

Who Is It For

For fans of turn-based JRPGs, science-fiction stories and long-term games. It suits both patient free-to-play players and those willing to spend on favorite characters. If you dislike gacha, think carefully since it sits at the core of progression.

Verdict

Honkai: Star Rail is one of the deepest gacha games around thanks to its turn-based combat and distinctive worlds. Gacha remains something you must accept. OtaHub scores it 9.2/10.

FAQ

Is Honkai: Star Rail free?

Yes, it is free-to-play with a gacha model.

Which platforms is it on?

Windows, iOS, Android and PS5 (since October 11, 2023).

Is it turn-based?

Yes, combat is turn-based with parties of up to four characters and an auto-battle option.
