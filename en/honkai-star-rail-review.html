Honkai: Star Rail Review: A Turn-Based Symphony (9.2/10)

Honkai: Star Rail review: HoYoverse's turn-based RPG launched April 26, 2023, Metacritic PC 80 and PS5 82; gameplay, the Penacony and Amphoreus stories, weaknesses.

Mobile / Turn-Based RPG · Review 2026-08-18 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Honkai: Star Rail review: HoYoverse's turn-based RPG launched April 26, 2023, Metacritic PC 80 and PS5 82; gameplay, the Penacony and Amphoreus stories, weaknesses.

This OtaHub review is compiled from official information and critics' reviews; it is not a personal playthrough diary. The score is an editorial judgment.

Quick Facts

  • Released: April 26, 2023 on Windows, iOS and Android; PS5 version on October 11, 2023
  • Developer: miHoYo (published as HoYoverse outside mainland China); built on Unity
  • Metacritic: PC 80, PS5 82; OpenCritic: 90%
  • Commercial: 22.98 million pre-registrations in mainland China; over $100 million global mobile revenue in the first 10 days; over 100 million downloads by February 2024

Gameplay

The game is a turn-based RPG with teams of up to four characters. Each character follows a Path that defines their combat role, plus a combat type and Traces (abilities). Equipment consists of Light Cones and Relics, and characters and weapons are obtained through a gacha "warp" system using the premium currency Stellar Jade.

Story

Penacony tells of a shared dream world with conspiracy and reality manipulation. Amphoreus, starting with version 3.0 (January 2025), takes players to an isolated planet where the mortal heroes known as the Chrysos Heirs struggle over the Coreflame; characters such as Phainon, Cyrene and Castorice feature prominently.

What Critics Say

Reviews praised the story, characters, combat system and visual style. Criticism targets the character progression system and the lack of variety in female character design.

Awards

The game won Best Mobile Game at The Game Awards 2023, iPhone Game of the Year at the App Store Awards 2023 and Best Game, Best Story and Best for Tablets at the Google Play Awards 2023.

Verdict: 9.2/10

Honkai: Star Rail shows that turn-based RPGs still have plenty of room to grow when given real investment in writing, music and tactical depth. Note that it is a gacha game with a long-running progression system.
