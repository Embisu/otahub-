Silent Hill: Townfall Details Scotland Setting And CRTV Retro Tech

Developer Screen Burn has shared new details on Silent Hill: Townfall, a first-person game set in the Scottish town of St. Amelia in 1996, launching September 24 exclusively on PS5.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-18 · 6 min read

In short: Silent Hill: Townfall's developers detail the St. Amelia, Scotland setting from 1996, the retro CRTV device, and first-person combat. The game launches September 24, exclusively on PS5.

The developers of Silent Hill: Townfall have shared new details about the game's Scottish setting, retro technology and first-person combat system, in an interview published on PlayStation Blog on July 30.

The game is being developed by Scotland-based studio Screen Burn, formerly known as No Code, and published by Annapurna Interactive, set in St. Amelia, a fog-shrouded island town, in 1996. Protagonist Simon Ordell finds himself trapped in a time loop within the town, using a device called the CRTV, a handheld television that tunes into unstable signals using real analog and VHS technology, to detect threats, see through obstacles, and decode clues.

On combat, the developers describe a pace faster and more frenetic than longtime fans might expect, blending melee and gunplay: players can block while holding a melee weapon, while weapons degrade over time and ammunition stays scarce, so every trigger pull becomes a choice. The first-person perspective was chosen because it is the only viewpoint that lets the CRTV mechanic function as intended.

Silent Hill: Townfall launches September 24, 2026, exclusively on PlayStation 5 at launch, with a PC version coming to Steam and the Epic Games Store. You can find more on our latest news and Gaming pages.

Source: PlayStation Blog.
