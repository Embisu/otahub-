VIZ's August 18 manga slate: Vagabond, Undead Unluck and Kingdom

August 18 is one of VIZ's busiest release dates of the month, spanning action shonen, historical seinen and premium collected editions.

OtaHub Editorial · August 18, 2026 · 6 min read
Highlights: Vagabond Definitive Edition Vol. 6, Undead Unluck Vol. 26, Kingdom Vol. 10, Centuria Vol. 3, Dogsred Vol. 6 and Sakamoto Holidays Vol. 2.

The August 18 slate

VIZ's official calendar lists more than ten books for the date, including Hunter x Hunter 3-in-1 Edition Vol. 7, Mission: Yozakura Family Vol. 24, Magilumiere Magical Girls Inc. Vol. 14, Show-ha Shoten! Vol. 11, Snowball Earth Vol. 9 and Skip・Beat! 3-in-1 Edition Vol. 17.

What should readers prioritize?

Vagabond Definitive Edition is aimed at premium collectors, Kingdom remains the historical-war pick, and Undead Unluck Vol. 26 is best for established readers approaching the series' final stretch. Centuria and Dogsred are useful catch-up choices for newer series.
