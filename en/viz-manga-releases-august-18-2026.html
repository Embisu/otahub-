VIZ's August 18 Slate: Vagabond, Undead Unluck, Kingdom

August 18 is one of VIZ's busiest release dates of the month, spanning action shonen, historical seinen and premium collected editions.

Home › Manga › VIZ Manga Releases for August 18, 2026: Vagabond & More
Manga 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
August 18 is one of VIZ's busiest release dates of the month, spanning action shonen, historical seinen and premium collected editions.

VIZ's official calendar lists more than ten books for August 18, 2026. Besides the headline titles, Vagabond Definitive Edition Vol. 6, Undead Unluck Vol. 26 and Kingdom Vol. 10, the slate includes Hunter x Hunter 3-in-1 Edition Vol. 7, Mission: Yozakura Family Vol. 24, Magilumiere Magical Girls Inc. Vol. 14, Show-ha Shoten! Vol. 11, Snowball Earth Vol. 9 and Skip・Beat! 3-in-1 Edition Vol. 17.

The main August 18 releases

VIZ's official calendar lists more than ten books for the date. Besides the headline titles above, there are Hunter x Hunter 3-in-1 Edition Vol. 7, Mission: Yozakura Family Vol. 24, Magilumiere Magical Girls Inc. Vol. 14, Show-ha Shoten! Vol. 11, Snowball Earth Vol. 9 and Skip・Beat! 3-in-1 Edition Vol. 17.

Which book should you prioritize?

Vagabond Definitive Edition suits collectors who want a premium print; Kingdom is the pick for readers who enjoy long-running historical war stories; and Undead Unluck Vol. 26 sits close to the end of the series, so it fits readers who have followed it from earlier volumes. Centuria and Dogsred are two worthwhile options if you want to catch up with newer series.

A note on regional availability

This is VIZ's English-language calendar for the markets the publisher supports. Availability in Vietnam may come later, depending on the importer and distributor. OtaHub does not treat a third-party store's import schedule as an official release date.

The OtaHub view

VIZ releasing a classic that finished long ago (Vagabond) alongside a series that is nearing its final stretch (Undead Unluck) in the same batch points to a balanced catalog strategy: it serves collectors who want to complete a landmark series while keeping the readers who follow current series closely. For newcomers to English-language manga, it is also a good example of why international release calendars run ahead of local ones, and why readers in Vietnam should check their import source instead of assuming that VIZ's official release date is also the date books reach local bookstores.
