Phantom Blade Zero: State of Play Gameplay Deep Dive August 17, Open Pre-Order

Phantom Blade Zero officially opened pre-order and prepared a separate State of Play on August 17, revealing nearly 20 minutes of new gameplay before the game launches in October.

OtaHub › Gaming › Phantom Blade Zero: State of Play Gameplay Deep Dive August 17, Open Pre-Order
Gaming 2026-08-17 6 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Phantom Blade Zero, the wuxia action game from studio S-Game, officially opened pre-order and announced a State of Play separately broadcast on August 17, 2026, revealing nearly 20 minutes of new gameplay. The game is expected to launch on PS5 on October 29, 2026, with Donnie Yen taking on the role of creative consultant and motion capture actor for the father character of main character Soul.

Sony just confirmed a broadcast State of Play specially reserved for Phantom Blade Zero, the ambitious wuxia action game from studio S-Game. The broadcast took place today 17/8/2026 at 7:00 p.m. Pacific time (10:00 a.m. August 18 Vietnam time), revealing nearly 20 minutes of completely new gameplay on PlayStation's official Twitch and YouTube channels.

Pre-order officially opened, attractive early gifts

Alongside the announcement of State of Play, Phantom Blade Zero has also officially gone on pre-order on PlayStation 5. Pre-orderers will receive the Treasure Basin accessory and the Legacy outfit, both of which can also be unlocked through regular gameplay.

Donnie Yen: from creative consultant to motion capture actor

A notable highlight of the project is the participation of a martial arts star Donnie Yen, who has served as a creative consultant for Phantom Blade Zero since 2023, brings his experience in martial arts choreography and cinematic action to the game's combat design. He also contributed to the facial and movement motion capture for Mó Yuan, the father of the main character Soul.

Phantom World is full of mystery

The latest pre-order trailer only reveals the beginning of Soul's journey, from battles on his speeding chariot to confrontations with former teammate The Hunt. The upcoming State of Play promises to introduce entirely new characters who will play a central role in the storyline, while also showcasing the level of gameplay customization players can build for themselves in the dark wuxia land known as Phantom World.

  • State of Play air date: August 17, 2026, 7:00 p.m. PT
  • Development studio: S-Game
  • Launch date: October 29, 2026, on PS5
  • Creative advisor: Donnie Yen (from 2023)
  • Pre-order offers: Treasure Basin accessories, Legacy apparel
