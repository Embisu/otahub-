Spy x Family Review: A Fake Family Full of Secrets

Spy x Family anime review: A heartwarming, hilarious espionage masterpiece powered by the endearing dynamics of the counterfeit Forger family.

Home › Anime › Spy x Family Review: A Fake Family Full of Secrets
Anime 2026-09-27 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Spy x Family delivers an exquisite balance between cold-war espionage action and heartwarming domestic comedy among three undercover strangers: master spy Twilight, lethal assassin Yor, and telepathic toddler Anya. An in-depth review of Wit Studio and CloverWorks' hit anime adaptation.

Spy x Family takes a simple idea, a spy who must build a fake family to complete his mission, and turns it into one of the best-selling family comedy manga of the decade. Tatsuya Endo's manga has run on Shonen Jump+ since March 25, 2019, and by April 2026 had passed 42 million copies in print across 17 volumes.

The anime is co-produced by Wit Studio and CloverWorks: season 1 aired April–December 2022, season 2 from October–December 2023, and season 3 from October–December 2025. The film Spy x Family Code: White opened in Japanese theaters in December 2023 and in North America in April 2024.

Quick Facts

  • Author: Tatsuya Endo
  • Publisher: Shueisha, Shonen Jump+ (since March 25, 2019, biweekly)
  • Scale: 17 volumes; over 42 million copies by April 2026
  • Studio: Wit Studio & CloverWorks; season 1 (April–December 2022), season 2 (October–December 2023), season 3 (October–December 2025)
  • Awards: 52nd Japan Cartoonists Association Award Grand Prize (2023)

Story

Veteran spy Twilight takes on a mission to approach a political target by building a fake family. He adopts Anya, a girl with the power to read minds, and marries Yor, a professional assassin, with none of the three aware of the others' true identities.

The trio each keep their own secrets while trying to live as a normal family, creating comedic situations layered over espionage and assassination plots.

Behind The Scenes

The franchise's success expanded into musicals, video games and theme park attractions, showing a scale beyond an ordinary manga. The Code: White film was also a milestone, bringing the series to theaters in both Japan and North America.

Experience

Family comedy and spy action

The series' biggest strength is balancing light family comedy with tense spy scenarios. Viewers know the secrets the characters hide from each other, creating dramatic irony that fuels much of the comedy.

The three leads

Loid/Twilight, Yor and Anya each have their own arc with enough depth to stand alone, but the chemistry between the three is what keeps viewers coming back across multiple seasons.

Strengths

  • Family comedy well balanced with spy action
  • Three leads with depth and strong chemistry
  • Franchise expanded successfully into film, musicals, games
  • Manga sales among the highest ever

Weaknesses

  • The comedy formula can feel repetitive in some situations
  • The main spy plot sometimes gets overshadowed by comedy
  • Those who dislike slice-of-life may find the pacing slow between seasons

What Critics Say

Spy x Family won the Grand Prize at the 52nd Japan Cartoonists Association Award in 2023 and maintained strong sales in international markets. The three leads and the comedy-meets-spy-action formula are seen as the main reasons for its commercial success.

Who Is It For

For fans of light family comedy mixed with action, suitable for family viewing given its gentle tone. If you want a serious spy thriller, that is not this anime's main focus.

OtaHub Score

9.0

Verdict

Spy x Family is one of the most successful family comedy anime of the decade, thanks to three charming leads and a well-balanced comedy-action formula. OtaHub scores it 9.0/10.

FAQ

How many seasons does Spy x Family have?

Three seasons: 2022, 2023 and 2025, plus the film Code: White in 2023.

Who are the leads?

The three leads are Twilight (Loid Forger), Yor Forger and Anya Forger.

Has the manga ended?

No, the manga is still serialized on Shonen Jump+.
