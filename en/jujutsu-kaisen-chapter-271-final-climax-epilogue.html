Jujutsu Kaisen Nears Final Climax: Gege Akutami Confirms Series Finale & 5-Chapter Epilogue

Weekly Shonen Jump and Gege Akutami confirm the exact release date for Jujutsu Kaisen's climactic final chapter and special 5-part epilogue arc.

Manga · Shonen Jump · News 2026-08-18 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Weekly Shonen Jump and Gege Akutami confirm the exact release date for Jujutsu Kaisen's climactic final chapter and special 5-part epilogue arc.
The End of an Era
Gege Akutami concludes six years of Jujutsu Kaisen with Chapter 271, reuniting Yuji, Megumi, and Nobara.

After six historic years serializing in Weekly Shonen Jump and smashing global circulation records, Gege Akutami's modern dark fantasy phenomenon Jujutsu Kaisen (Chú Thuật Hồi Chiến) has officially published Chapter 271 — the poignant final chapter concluding the monumental sorcery epic.

1. A Peaceful Epilogue for the Tokyo Jujutsu High Trio

Following the apocalyptic devastation of the Shinjuku Showdown and the decisive victory over the King of Curses, Ryomen Sukuna, Chapter 271 shifts tone toward earned serenity. The beloved core trio of Itadori Yuji, Fushiguro Megumi, and Kugisaki Nobara embark on a modest, everyday curse exorcism mission, demonstrating their maturity, lingering camaraderie, and optimistic outlook for the future.

2. The Enduring Legacy of Gojo Satoru

Though the legendary Gojo Satoru fell in battle during his duel with Sukuna, his radical vision for the jujutsu world lives on. Rather than reproducing the oppressive conservatism of the deceased higher-ups, the surviving generation unites with mutual trust, reshaping jujutsu society into a collaborative, protective community.

3. Gege Akutami's Parting Words & Series Milestone

In the author's commentary section, Gege Akutami extended heartfelt gratitude to the millions of passionate fans across the globe whose unwavering support fueled the series from its humble 2018 debut to global cultural ubiquity.
