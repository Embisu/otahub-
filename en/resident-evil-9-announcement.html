Resident Evil Requiem: Everything You Need to Know

Capcom officially unveils Resident Evil 9 with state-of-the-art RE Engine visuals, setting a new horror milestone on PC and PS5.

Home › Gaming › Resident Evil Requiem: Everything You Need to Know
Gaming 2026-06-10 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Resident Evil Requiem (Capcom, RE Engine) released February 27, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Switch 2. The game follows Grace Ashcroft (FBI technical analyst) and Leon Kennedy at the Wrenwood Hotel, 30 years after the Raccoon City disaster, with the series' first switchable first-/third-person perspective.

Capcom officially released Resident Evil Requiem, the official title for the ninth mainline installment, on February 27, 2026, for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2 (running natively on Switch 2, unlike the cloud-streamed RE7/RE8 ports before it).

The story: Grace Ashcroft and the ghosts of Raccoon City

Requiem is set roughly 30 years after the missile strike that destroyed Raccoon City. The protagonist is Grace Ashcroft, an FBI technical analyst and the daughter of Alyssa Ashcroft, an investigative journalist and Raccoon City survivor. Grace is pulled into an investigation of a string of deaths at the Wrenwood Hotel, forcing her to confront her mother's unsolved death and the secrets of the Umbrella Corporation. She soon catches the attention of former Umbrella scientist Dr. Victor Gideon, who believes Grace's natural immunity to the T-virus and the entire Progenitor Virus family is the key to unlocking a secret project called Elpis.

Leon Kennedy returns with secrets of his own

Along the way, Grace crosses paths with Leon S. Kennedy, the series' longtime anti-bioterrorism agent, who is hiding secrets of his own. This marks the first time Resident Evil has built its structure around two protagonists with distinctly different experiences: Grace's sections lean into investigative horror, while Leon's lean into fast-paced action.

Gameplay: dual perspective, a first for the series

Requiem's biggest departure is letting players freely switch between first- and third-person perspective across the roughly 12-hour campaign. Grace's sections default to first-person to maximize the sense of claustrophobia, while Leon's action-heavy sections lean toward a classic third-person over-the-shoulder view, but players can toggle either perspective on both characters at will. According to the director, the feature is aimed largely at players who found RE7's pure first-person perspective difficult to get through.

Umbrella, the Progenitor Virus and Project Elpis

Requiem digs further into Umbrella lore by placing Grace's unusual immunity to the Progenitor Virus at the center of its plot. Dr. Gideon believes Grace is the key to "unlocking Elpis", a detail that ties directly back to Raccoon City's history and her mother's legacy, forming one of the most-discussed lore twists in recent RE entries.

Sources: capcom.co.jp, deadline.com.
