One Piece Arc Egghead: Vegapunk, Prophecy and Secrets of the World

Vegapunk, the world's greatest scientist, reveals a shocking secret about the true history of the One Piece world, and changes everything readers thought they knew.

Manga2026-06-2310 minutes to readOtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
The Egghead Arc (manga chapters 1058-1120+) is the longest and perhaps most important arc in the Final Saga One Piece. Vegapunk reveals Void Century. The appearance of Gorosei. Twist about Joy Boy and Nika.

In the more than 25 years One Piece has existed, no arc has ever thrown out as many important revelations in such a short period of time Egghead. This is not simply an action arc, this is where Oda Eiichiro begins to gather the threads he has spun since the first chapters.

Egghead Island, The Island 500 Years Ahead

Dr.'s Island Vegapunk is called “a future 500 years ahead of its time,” a place where technology is so advanced that it's completely alien. Oda built Egghead as a sci-fi playground before turning it into the most dangerous battlefield the Straw Hats had ever entered.

Vegapunk And Its Shocking Message

The climax of the Egghead Arc was the moment Vegapunk broadcast a mysterious message globally, and revealed that he had discovered scientific evidence of Void Century. The leaked pieces of information are enough to overturn all fan theories so far about the origin of the world.

Gorosei Revealed, And They Are Not Human

Egghead is the first time readers see the Gorosei actually fight, and the results are absolutely shocking. Saturn, Warcury, Marcus Mars are not just old officials, they are ancient entities with monster forms and the ability to regenerate.

Why Egghead Is The Most Important Arc Final Saga

Egghead stands out because narrative density, the density of information and revelation in each chapter is higher than any previous arc. This is where Oda begins to "pay off" the storytelling contracts he signed with readers 25 years ago, and he does it with the sophistication of a writer at the peak of his career.
