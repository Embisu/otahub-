Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree Review (9.7/10)

Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree review (June 21, 2024): Metacritic 92–95, 5 million sold in 3 days; Scadutree Fragments, bosses, new weapons and why OtaHub scores it 9.7.

PC / Console Game · Review 2026-08-18 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree review (June 21, 2024): Metacritic 92–95, 5 million sold in 3 days; Scadutree Fragments, bosses, new weapons and why OtaHub scores it 9.7.

This OtaHub review is compiled from official information and critics' reviews; it is not a personal playthrough diary. The score is an editorial judgment.

Quick Facts

  • Released: June 21, 2024 on PS4, PS5, Windows, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S
  • Director: Hidetaka Miyazaki (FromSoftware); published by Bandai Namco
  • Metacritic: PS5 94, Windows 92, Xbox Series X 95
  • Sales: 5 million in 3 days; 10 million as of July 2025
  • DLC requirement: defeat Starscourge Radahn and Mohg, Lord of Blood in the base game

Story And Setting

Shadow of the Erdtree sends the player after the demigod Miquella into the Land of Shadow (Realm of Shadow), a land hidden from the Lands Between, after discovering that Miquella's spirit has abandoned his body. The expansion digs into unresolved threads of the base game, including Queen Marika's past and the hidden history of the Golden Order.

It is described as the largest downloadable expansion FromSoftware has made, with a world density comparable to or exceeding Limgrave, the base game's opening region.

Gameplay And What Is New

Scadutree Fragments boost attack and defense within the DLC, while Revered Spirit Ash strengthens summoned spirits and Torrent. Rather than relying on level alone, players are encouraged to explore to find these upgrades.

The DLC adds several new weapon types, including hand-to-hand arts, light greatswords, great katanas and thrusting shields, along with memorable opponents such as Messmer the Impaler, Rellana and Consort Radahn.

What Critics Say

Critics praised the level design, the additions to combat, enemy variety and exploration; reactions to the difficulty were mixed, with many noting that boss difficulty and the power curve demand patience.

Awards

Shadow of the Erdtree won Best Game Expansion at the Golden Joystick Awards 2024 and was nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2024.

Verdict: 9.7/10

Shadow of the Erdtree is an expansion with the scale of a full game, offering dense world design, meaningfully expanded combat and exploration that rewards curiosity. It is close to essential for anyone who has played Elden Ring, but note that the DLC expects a character who has progressed far in the base game and its difficulty is not for newcomers.
