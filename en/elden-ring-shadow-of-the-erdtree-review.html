Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree Review (9.7/10)

Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree review: an expansion as big as a standalone game, with the Land of Shadow, new weapons and the Scadutree system; Metacritic 94, though the difficulty is a barrier.

Home › Gaming › Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree Review (9.7/10)
PC / Console Game · Review 2026-08-18 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Elden Ring: Shadow of the Erdtree review: an expansion as big as a standalone game, with the Land of Shadow, new weapons and the Scadutree system; Metacritic 94, though the difficulty is a barrier.

Shadow of the Erdtree is not an expansion in the usual sense. FromSoftware sends players to the Land of Shadow, a new region big enough and dense enough with dungeons and bosses to stand as a full game. It is also the highest-rated DLC Metacritic has recorded, surpassing The Witcher 3: Blood and Wine.

The expansion launched on June 20, 2024 on PS4, PS5, Windows, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, and requires the base game.

Quick Facts

  • Release: June 20, 2024
  • Platforms: PS4, PS5, Windows, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S; requires the Elden Ring base game
  • Developer: FromSoftware; published by Bandai Namco Entertainment
  • Critic scores: Metacritic 94 (PS5, 64 reviews), 92 (PC), 95 (Xbox Series X)
  • Awards: Best Game Expansion (Golden Joystick 2024); Best DLC/Expansion and Best Original Score (GEM Awards 2025)
  • Sales: Over 10 million copies after release

Story

Players follow their character into the Land of Shadow, a realm tied to Miquella, a figure from the base game. The expansion digs into the history of the Lands Between and lore that the base game only hinted at through item descriptions. One point some critics debated is that the DLC tells its story more explicitly than FromSoftware's usual oblique style.

Entering the new land requires having defeated Starscourge Radahn and Mohg, Lord of Blood in the base game, so this is content for players who have come a long way.

Behind The Scenes

Shadow of the Erdtree is Elden Ring's only major expansion, released more than two years after the base game launched on February 25, 2022. FromSoftware chose to expand with an entirely new land rather than small additions, and requires players to have progressed well into the base game to enter.

The expansion sold over 10 million copies and is recorded by Metacritic as the highest-rated DLC of all time.

Experience

The Land of Shadow and world design

The new region is designed with dense vertical and horizontal layering: interlocking dungeons, shortcuts back to earlier spots and many hidden nooks. Critics singled out its scale, level design and sense of exploration. Game Informer scored it 9.75/10, highlighting the Realm of Shadow, the dungeons, new bosses and weapons, and the Scadutree progression system.

Combat and progression

Rather than traditional leveling, power in the Land of Shadow rises through Scadutree Fragments and Revered Spirit Ash, pushing players to explore to grow stronger. The expansion adds many new weapon types, including Backhand Blades, Great Katanas, Light Greatswords, Beast Claws and Throwing Blades, along with enemies with unpredictable move sets. Bosses such as Messmer the Impaler and Promised Consort Radahn are the fights most often mentioned in reviews.

Strengths

  • Scale on par with a full game, with exploration well rewarded
  • Dense level design with shortcuts and hidden corners
  • Many new weapons and playstyles widen build options
  • Standout boss encounters

Weaknesses

  • High difficulty, best suited to players already skilled in the base game
  • Eurogamer (3/5) argued the more explicit storytelling loses the series' inherent ambiguity
  • Access requires substantial progress in the base game

What Critics Say

Shadow of the Erdtree scored 94 on PS5, 92 on PC and 95 on Xbox Series X on Metacritic, making it the highest-rated DLC. IGN and GameSpot both gave 10/10; GameSpot described an expansion with enough content to resemble a game of its own, praising the setting, combat options and additions to lore. Eurogamer was the outlier at 3/5.

It won Best Game Expansion at the 2024 Golden Joystick Awards and Best DLC/Expansion and Best Original Score at the 2025 GEM Awards, and sold over 10 million copies.

Who Is It For

For players who have spent serious time with Elden Ring, enjoy challenge and are willing to die many times to learn each boss. If you have not finished the base game, do so first because the DLC has no early-access path. Those seeking a relaxed, low-pressure experience will be better served elsewhere.

Verdict

Shadow of the Erdtree is a worthy closing chapter for Elden Ring and a new benchmark for expansions: large in scale, sharp in world design, with memorable combat and bosses. The difficulty and more explicit storytelling are the two things to weigh. OtaHub scores it 9.7/10.

FAQ

You need the Elden Ring base game and must have defeated Starscourge Radahn and Mohg, Lord of Blood to enter the Land of Shadow.

Which platforms is the DLC on?

PS4, PS5, Windows, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S.
