Key Strengths (Why It Scored High)
  • The Realm of Shadow offers unparalleled vertical complexity and breathtaking architectural vistas.
  • Over 10 monumental Remembrance Bosses offering intense, exhilarating challenges.
  • 8 completely new weapon classes providing limitless character build variety.
Points to Consider
  • Early-game difficulty spike can be unforgiving before collecting Scadutree Fragments.
  • Occasional camera tracking issues during fast-moving colossal boss encounters.

1. Premise & The Realm of Shadow

Shadow of the Erdtree is not just a standard expansion; it is effectively a full-scale game in its own right. Stepping beyond the withered arm of Miquella at the Cocoon of the Empyrean, players enter the Realm of Shadow.

From the towering ramparts of Belurat to the eerie depths of the Abyssal Woods, the vertical world design stands as a masterclass in environmental exploration.

2. Why It Earned a 9.7 Score on OtaHub

OtaHub awarded Shadow of the Erdtree a 9.7/10 based on four central strengths: innovative Scadutree scaling, world-class boss choreography (Messmer, Rellana, Midra), stellar orchestral score, and deep combat customization.

3. Verdict

A triumphant capstone to Elden Ring that cements FromSoftware position at the pinnacle of open-world action RPG design.
