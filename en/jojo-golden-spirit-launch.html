JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Spirit Releases Worldwide August 13

The first JoJo mobile game officially released outside of Japan, launching globally tomorrow August 13, 2026 on iOS and Android, free, tactical RPG genre with 50 characters/Stand from 6 story parts.

OtaHub › Gaming › JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Spirit Releases Worldwide August 13
Gaming 2026-08-12 6 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Spirit, the first JoJo mobile game officially released outside of Japan, launches globally on iOS and Android tomorrow, August 13, 2026. This is a free-to-play, turn-based strategy game with a multi-grid board system and gacha system, featuring approximately 50 characters and Stands ranging from Phantom Blood to Stone Ocean.

After many years of existing only as a Chinese-exclusive mobile game (once called Golden Wind/Golden Hymn), the JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Finally there is an official mobile game for the global market. JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Golden Spirit will open on both iOS and Android tomorrow, August 13, 2026, marking the first time the series composed by Hirohiko Araki has an official mobile game product launched outside of Japan and China.

Tactical RPG with multi-tile chessboard and weather elements

In terms of gameplay, Golden Spirit is a game turn-based tactical role-playing (tactical RPG) This is an auto-battler game where players arrange their team on a grid of multiple squares instead of a simple single row like many other gacha games. Weather and terrain effects also play a role in tactical planning; for example, Guido Mista's accuracy decreases from the fourth shot onwards, reflecting the original Stand mechanics quite accurately in the gameplay.

50 characters, spanning 6 story parts

At the time of launch, the game gathered about 50 Characters and Stands Different characters, ranging from Phantom Blood (Jonathan Joestar) to Stone Ocean (Jolyne Cujoh), cover almost the entire history of the series to date. Each character is rendered in high-detail 3D models combined with Live2D for interactive scenes, and the game recreates many classic confrontations from the anime in Story Mode.

Character progression system

Golden Spirit follows the standard free-to-play model of the genre, with progression classes including: Stand Awakening, skill upgrades, gear forging, and character ascension. In addition to the story mode, the game also features world-boss raids and ranked PvP arenas for players to test their team compositions.

  • Global launch date: 13/8/2026
  • Platform: iOS, Android
  • Business model: Free (free-to-play), gacha
  • Initial Character/Stand Number: ~50, from Phantom Blood to Stone Ocean
  • Category: Tactical RPG, multi-cell grid formation
  • Play mode: Story Mode, world-boss raid, ranked PvP

Meaningful to the global JoJo fan community

With a brand boasting a large and loyal fanbase but previously almost absent from the international mobile game market, Golden Spirit's official "export" is a noteworthy milestone. If the game maintains a faithful adaptation of the original work, which is always a challenge for detailed IPs like JoJo, it could very well become one of the most worthwhile anime mobile games to play in the latter half of 2026.
