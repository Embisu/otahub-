How the rankings work
  1. Only titles with a published OtaHub review are ranked; the score shown is the score at the end of that review.
  2. One spot per work; live-service games (Genshin Impact, Wuthering Waves…) use their most recent review.
  3. Sorted from highest to lowest score; on a tie, the more recent review ranks first.
  4. Every list always has exactly 10 titles and updates when a new review scores high enough.
  5. Hardware (consoles) and unscored news or analysis pieces are not ranked.
