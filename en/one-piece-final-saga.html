One Piece Final Saga: Elbaph Through Chapter 1194

A summary of One Piece's Final Saga in Elbaph through chapter 1194: Imu, Loki, the Dawn Thor Bullet and Zoro vs Sommers, plus the God Valley film in summer 2027.

Home › Manga › One Piece Final Saga: Oda Reveals Laugh Tale Map
Manga 2026-08-09 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
A summary of One Piece's Final Saga in Elbaph through chapter 1194: Imu, Loki, the Dawn Thor Bullet and Zoro vs Sommers, plus the God Valley film in summer 2027.

One Piece is in its Final Saga, but “Final Saga” does not mean the series will end after a single arc. It is the large phase that began after Wano, covering Egghead and the story on Elbaph, where many long-running threads about the world's history, the World Government and the Straw Hat Pirates' journey are gradually converging.

Where is the Final Saga now?

Egghead widened the story from an adventure on a single island into a conflict that affects the whole world. Elbaph continues that thread while taking the Straw Hats to the land of the giants, which has been mentioned since Little Garden. That is why the value of this arc is not only in its battles: it is also a chance to pick up Usopp's promise, the history of the giants and the mysteries tied to the Void Century.

New chapter schedule

As of September 30, 2026, MANGA Plus lists chapter 1194 as released on September 27, with the next chapter scheduled for October 11 at 15:00 UTC. The schedule can change with breaks for the author or the magazine, so readers should check the official platform directly instead of relying on screenshots or leaks on social media.

  • MANGA Plus: carries the new-chapter schedule and the official reader, by region.
  • VIZ Shonen Jump: the official English-language release in supported markets.
  • ONE PIECE.com: updates on the anime, the volumes and announcements from the brand.

How do the Elbaph anime and the manga differ?

The official ONE PIECE site confirms that the Elbaph anime began on April 5, 2026, after Egghead ended and the series took a three-month production break. The anime currently adapts the early part of the Elbaph adventure, while the manga is considerably ahead. Anime-only viewers should avoid “new chapter spoiler” posts; manga readers should still wait for the official translation to avoid errors in attack names, characters or context.

Storylines worth following

The three most important threads are the Straw Hats' goal on Elbaph, the giants' role in the world's history, and how the major powers react as the race to One Piece enters its decisive stage. This is a more useful way to read than treating every page as a “confirmation” of the ending, because Eiichiro Oda has not announced a chapter count or a specific end date.

The OtaHub view: Elbaph stands out because it restores the feel of classic adventure while also carrying the answers to many long-standing mysteries. This article will be updated only when official chapters are released; leaked content is not treated as confirmation.

Sources: MANGA Plus, VIZ and ONE PIECE.com. Updated September 30, 2026.
