Facing the Rain: A Heartfelt Indie RPG Now Free Forever on Steam

Facing the Rain is an indie RPG that is now permanently free on Steam, with a heartfelt story, combat-free gameplay and a runtime of about 2–3 hours.

Home › Gaming › Facing the Rain: A Heartfelt Indie RPG Now Free Forever on Steam
Gaming 2026-09-30 2 min read Anna
Key Takeaway
Facing the Rain, an indie RPG with a heartfelt story, is now permanently free on Steam. With a runtime of only about 2–3 hours and no combat, it’s a notable pick for anyone who loves story-driven indie games.

Quick Facts

  • Title: Facing the Rain
  • Genres: Indie RPG, Pixel Art, Story-Rich, Puzzle, Adventure
  • Platform: PC (Steam)
  • Model: Permanently free (Free-to-Play)
  • Playtime: 2–3 hours

From Paid Game to Permanently Free-to-Play

Facing the Rain first launched in 2024 at around $5, before the price was lowered to about $3.

The developer has now officially made the game permanently free on Steam. This isn’t a giveaway or a time-limited trial. Players can add the game to their library and play it without paying anything.

What Makes Facing the Rain’s Story Special?

Players take on the role of Billy, a young woman who starts working as a housekeeper at a hotel in the town of Drumberg. As she works, Billy gradually uncovers the stories surrounding the hotel and its owner, Irma.

Through Billy’s journey, Facing the Rain explores themes such as loss, depression and compassion. The story is told mainly through the environment, the characters and the events players discover along the way.

Combat-Free Gameplay Focused on Exploration

Unlike many traditional RPGs, Facing the Rain doesn’t focus on combat but guides players toward exploring and understanding the story. Gameplay mainly revolves around interacting with the environment, talking to characters, collecting items and solving small puzzles.

Players’ choices can also affect how the story unfolds and lead to multiple endings. This gives players more reason to come back and discover content they didn’t unlock in previous playthroughs.

Nostalgic Pixel Art and a Short Runtime

Facing the Rain uses a 2D top-down Pixel Art style, combined with an original soundtrack to create a distinctive atmosphere for its world.

Although each playthrough lasts only about 2–3 hours, the game also includes random elements, hidden secrets and 15 Steam Achievements to discover.

As a result, Facing the Rain isn’t just about finishing the story; it also encourages players to look for content they may have missed the first time around.

Who Is Facing the Rain For?

With a runtime of about 2–3 hours, gameplay that doesn’t revolve around combat and its new permanently free status on Steam, Facing the Rain is a good fit for players who love indie games focused on story, exploration and light puzzle-solving.

If you’re looking for a short game to play over the weekend, this may be one worth adding to your Steam library.
