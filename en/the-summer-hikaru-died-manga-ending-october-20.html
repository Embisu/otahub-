The Summer Hikaru Died Ends in 2 Chapters, October 20

Mokumokuren’s horror manga The Summer Hikaru Died ends with two final chapters: chapter 50 on October 13 and chapter 51 (the finale) on October 20, 2026.

Home › Manga › The Summer Hikaru Died Ends in 2 Chapters, October 20
Manga 2026-09-23 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Mokumokuren’s horror manga The Summer Hikaru Died ends with two final chapters: chapter 50 on October 13 and chapter 51 (the finale) on October 20, 2026.

The official X account for the supernatural horror manga The Summer Hikaru Died (Hikaru ga Shinda Natsu) announced that Mokumokuren’s series will end after just two more chapters.

Release schedule for the final two chapters

Per the official announcement, chapter 50 releases on October 13, 2026, and chapter 51, the series finale, arrives exactly one week later on October 20, 2026.

Five years on Young Ace Up

The Summer Hikaru Died began on Kadokawa Shoten’s Young Ace Up platform in August 2021. As of June 2026 it has been collected into 9 tankobon volumes, building a loyal following through the suffocating rural-Japan horror atmosphere and the story of the friendship between Yoshiki and the “thing” that replaced his dead best friend Hikaru.

Anime season 2 confirmed, no air date

On the anime side, season 2 was confirmed on September 27, 2025, right after episode 12 of the first season aired, with CygamesPictures continuing production. As of now neither an official air date nor the episode count for season 2 has been announced.

  • Chapter 50: October 13, 2026
  • Chapter 51 (finale): October 20, 2026
  • Author: Mokumokuren
  • Serialization: Young Ace Up (Kadokawa Shoten)
  • Volumes released: 9 tankobon (as of June 2026)
  • Anime season 2: confirmed, CygamesPictures, no air date
