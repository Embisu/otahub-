Wuthering Waves 3.6: Complete Ren Realm Exploration Guide & Sword Flight Traversal Mechanics

Comprehensive exploration guide for Ren Realm in Wuthering Waves 3.6, Sword Flight mastery, and 5-star chest puzzles.

Home›Guides›Wuthering Waves 3.6: Complete Ren Realm Exploration Guide & Sword Flight Traversal Mechanics
Guides Aug 24, 2026 9 min read OtaHub Editorial
Guide Summary
Version 3.6 of Wuthering Waves introduces the majestic Ren Realm and Sword Flight mechanics. This guide walks you through unlocking flight, solving 15 Bagua puzzle matrices, and fast-farming boss Echoes.

Version 3.6 of Wuthering Waves expands the world of Solaris-3 with the majestic, mist-veiled mountain region of Ren Realm. With sheer cliffs soaring thousands of meters into the sky, the revolutionary Sword Flight (Ngự Kiếm) mechanic becomes your primary tool to achieve 100% exploration completion.

1. How to Unlock & Master Sword Flight (Ngự Kiếm)

To unlock aerial flight, Rovers must complete Chapter 4 of the main story quest: Echoes of the Mortal Peak:

  • Activate Ancient Flight Arrays: Interact with the 3 Sword Beacons at Thundercloud Peak, Azure Bamboo Valley, and Wisteria Abyss.
  • Flight Controls: Hold the Jump key (Space on PC / Jump icon on Mobile) while airborne or following a plunging strike.
  • Sword Qi Stamina: Flight consumes dedicated Qi stamina rather than standard swimming/climbing stamina. Glide through Aero Drafts along mountain ridges to instantly replenish Qi and gain maximum speed bursts.

2. Solving the 15 Bagua Puzzle Arrays in Ren Realm

The Ren Realm features 15 Bagua puzzle matrices that must be activated in specific sequences from aerial vantage points:

  1. Five-Element Array: Fire ranged sword beams to light up the Qian, Kun, Kan, and Li pillars matching the central beacon's resonant color.
  2. Aerial Ring Trials: Glide through 8 glowing rings within 45 seconds without touching the ground to claim Golden Chests (50 Astrite each).

3. Best Echo Farming Routes & Team Synergies for 3.6

Equip Qingxiao and Jingran with the newly introduced 5-star Aero Echo sets in Bamboo Valley. Pair with Verina for optimal healing and ATK buffs to conquer the weekly White Tiger Sword Immortal boss trial.
