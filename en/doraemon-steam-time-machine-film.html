Doraemon Announces 46th Film: Steam-Powered Time Machine

Doraemon: Nobita's Steam-Powered Time Machine, the franchise's 46th film, sends Nobita and friends to 19th-century London, directed by Rushio Moriyama.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-19 · 6 min read

In short: Doraemon's 46th film, Nobita's Steam-Powered Time Machine, sends Nobita and friends to Victorian-era London using a steam-powered version of the time machine. Directed by Rushio Moriyama and written by Makoto Ueda, it opens in Japanese theaters in spring 2027.

Doraemon has officially announced the franchise's 46th theatrical film, titled Nobita's Steam-Powered Time Machine, set to open in Japanese theaters in spring 2027.

The film is directed by Rushio Moriyama and written by Makoto Ueda. According to the official announcement, Nobita and his friends travel through time using a steam-powered version of the familiar time machine, landing in Victorian-era 19th-century London.

The concept blends the film's two core themes, "steam" and "time," building directly on the gadget most tied to the franchise: the time machine kept in Nobita's desk drawer. This is the next entry in Doraemon's annual theatrical tradition, which dates back to 1980 and remains a major box-office event in Japan every spring.
