Zenless Zone Zero 2.0: Outer Ring Expansion, Sons of Calydon & 4-Player Co-op Raid

HoYoverse unveils Zenless Zone Zero Version 2.0 featuring the vast Outer Ring desert wasteland, Sons of Calydon faction, and 4-player real-time co-op Hollow raids.

A New Era Beyond New Eridu
HoYoverse details Zenless Zone Zero Version 2.0 "Tour de Inferno", bringing the lawless Outer Ring wastelands and 4-player co-op combat.

1. The Outer Ring Wasteland & Motorcycle Traversal

Explore the arid frontiers beyond Sixth Street, navigating treacherous canyons aboard customizable high-speed motorbikes.

2. Sons of Calydon: Caesar King & Burnice White

Unlock S-Rank Agents Caesar King (Physical Defense/Stun) and Burnice White (Fire Anomaly dual-flamethrower specialist).
