Hollow Knight: Silksong Announces Free Sea of Sorrow DLC

Team Cherry announced Sea of Sorrow, the first free expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, scheduled to launch in 2026 with new areas, bosses and tools, after the game has reached more than 7 million copies sold.

OtaHub › Gaming › Hollow Knight: Silksong Announces Free Sea of Sorrow DLC
Gaming 2026-08-13 5 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Team Cherry has just announced Sea of Sorrow, the first free expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong, scheduled for release in 2026 with new areas, new bosses, and new combat tools. This announcement comes after Silksong sold over 7 million copies since its launch, not including millions more who experienced it through Xbox Game Pass.

Nearly a year after the community's long wait ended, Hollow Knight: Silksong continues to surprise Team Cherry officially announced Sea of Sorrow, the first major content expansion for the title, and most importantly: absolutely free for everyone who owns the game.

New content: areas, bosses and tools

According to the official announcement, Sea of Sorrow will bring these Completely new exploration area, adding more bosses to challenge player skills, along with new combat tools that expand gameplay options for Hornet. Team Cherry hasn't revealed specific details about the storyline or setting of the new area, but the name "Sea of Sorrow" is enough to suggest a gloomy space, true to the mysterious underworld that the Hollow Knight series always pursues.

Milestone of 7 million copies after many years of waiting

The news about Sea of Sorrow comes just as Silksong reaches an impressive milestone: over 7 million copies have been sold, not including the massive number of players experiencing it for free via Xbox Game Pass. This is an impressive number for an indie game, especially considering Silksong went through years of delays before its official release, one of the most closely watched game development stories in the global gaming community.

  • DLC name: Sea of Sorrow
  • Price: Free for Silksong owners
  • Content: New areas, new bosses, new fighting tools
  • Expected launch: In 2026
  • Current Silksong sales: More than 7 million copies
  • Platform: PC, PlayStation, Xbox (including Game Pass), Nintendo Switch/Switch 2

Why is it worth noting?

Releasing DLC for free instead of selling it separately is a familiar strategy for Team Cherry, having done the same with the original Hollow Knight through a series of free content updates over the years. With Sea of Sorrow, the studio once again demonstrates its long-term commitment to Silksong, while keeping the flame alive for a fanbase accustomed to waiting, but this time, at least, they have a name and a concrete promise to hold onto.
