Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 3 Returns in October 2027

Frieren Season 3 returns in October 2027 with the Golden Land Arc. Here is the latest on the broadcast window, the Macht teaser and what we know about the next anime season.

Home › Anime › Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 3 Returns in October 2027
Anime 2026-10-01 1 min read Yu
Key Takeaway
Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 3 has been confirmed to return in October 2027 with the Golden Land Arc. The new season picks up after Season 2 and will focus on events surrounding Macht. A new teaser visual has been released, but no exact premiere date has been announced yet.

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 3 has been confirmed to air in October 2027 on the Nippon TV network in Japan. The new season, titled the Golden Land Arc, continues the story after Season 2 and takes Frieren and her companions into a new chapter. The announcement came right after Season 2 ended on March 27, 2026.

New Teaser Focuses on Macht

Alongside the Season 3 announcement, the anime released a teaser visual introducing Macht of the Golden Land. The character’s image has continued to feature in Season 3’s promotional campaign, including new artwork unveiled at Anime Expo 2026.

The official anime website confirmed that the new season is called the “Golden Land Arc” and will begin airing in October 2027. No exact premiere date has been announced yet.
