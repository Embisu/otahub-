One Piece Chapter 1194 Spoilers: Prince Loki’s Devil Fruit, Yggdrasil & Ancient Weapon Uranus

One Piece Chapter 1194 spoilers reveal Prince Loki’s legendary Devil Fruit in Elbaf, the ancient secrets of the Yggdrasil World Tree, and key clues to Ancient Weapon Uranus.

⚡ One Piece Chapter 1194 Key Highlights
  • Prince Loki’s Legendary Fruit: The ancient royal Devil Fruit of Elbaf granting spatial distortion and frost illusion control.
  • Yggdrasil World Tree Revelations: The sanctuary hiding the 4th Road Poneglyph and Void Century relics.
  • Ancient Weapon Uranus Connections: High-stakes negotiation between Luffy and Loki revealing the weapon able to counter Imu.
  • Official Release Date: Chapter 1194 releases worldwide on MANGA Plus this Sunday.

The monumental Elbaf Arc reaches a fever pitch as verified spoiler details for One Piece Chapter 1194 surface online. Master storyteller Eiichiro Oda delivers astonishing revelations surrounding the enigmatic giant monarch: Prince Loki.

The Secret of Prince Loki’s Forbidden Devil Fruit

Chained beneath the roots of the colossal Yggdrasil World Tree, Loki's forbidden power is finally unmasked in Chapter 1194. Rather than a standard Ancient Zoan, Loki wields a primordial fruit capable of spatial manipulation and sub-zero frost generation, allowing him to reshape entire battlefields with titan-scale glacial constructs.

Luffy's playful interaction in Sun God Nika form creates an electrifying dynamic, establishing a temporary alliance where Loki promises royal passage through Elbaf's treacherous canopy in exchange for his liberation.

For more deep lore analysis, revisit our coverage on One Piece Chapter 1193 Breakdown and our comprehensive Egghead Arc Review.

Ancient Weapon Uranus & The Final Road Poneglyph

Chapter 1194 dives deep into elder warrior Jarul's historical flashbacks dating back 800 years to the Void Century. High atop the crown of Yggdrasil sits an ancient astronomical observatory linking the sky weapon Uranus to the ancient giants.

Crucially, the long-lost 4th Road Poneglyph is confirmed to be sealed inside the ancient Elbaf sanctuary, unlocking the direct coordinate path to Laugh Tale.
