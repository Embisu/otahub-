Kingdom Come Deliverance 2 Review: Best Historical RPG

Kingdom Come Deliverance 2 is the best historical RPG since The Witcher 3, Henry returns to the new scene

Home › Gaming › Kingdom Come Deliverance 2 Review: Best Historical RPG
Gaming 2026-06-10 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Kingdom Come Deliverance 2 (Warhorse Studios, 2025). 2 land areas: Trosky and Kuttenberg. 100+ hours of content. Combat cash with sword fighting mo style. Metacritic: 94.

The first part of Kingdom Come Deliverance (2018) was the most challenging RPG in history: no magic, no heroic characters, just Henry, a illiterate country blacksmith, in a historically accurate 15th-century Bohemia. And it was so difficult that many gave up. Part 2 retains that DNA but makes everything more accessible, deeper, and bigger.

The Gioi: Hai Vung Dat, 100+ Gio Noi Dung

KCD2 starts immediately after Part 1 ends and is divided into two separate maps. Trosky It's the familiar hot area of KCD1, but it's still very large. Kuttenberg, the historically significant city of Bohemia, is the largest city in an open-world RPG since Novigrad in The Witcher 3. It boasts a military base, night markets, guilds, and a vibrant city economy that allows Henry to rise or fall according to his understanding of politics and society.

Combat: Sword Fighting Mo Phong That Su

KCD1's swordsmanship system was notoriously complex, forcing players to learn proper techniques instead of just spamming attacks. KCD2 retains this philosophy but makes significant improvements: better feedback when blocking, an expanded training mode to help newcomers catch up, and a new dueling system that allows Henry more freedom in his fighting style. For the first time in KCD2, defeating an experienced swordsman according to the rules is an experience beyond words.

Henry: Tu Anh Tho Ren Den Nhat Phuc Tap

What Warhorse Studios did with Henry in KCD2 is one of the best character adaptations in RPG history. Henry in the first game was an outsider, powerless, and ignorant. Henry in the second game still has to learn, but now he has a real agency and makes decisions with real consequences. The dialogue choices reflect Henry's history and his worldview; there's no such thing as a simple "good choice" or "evil choice.

Authenticity: Czech Team Viet Ve Lich Su Cua Chinh Ho

Warhorse Studios is a Czech studio, and they write about Bohemia 15 with invaluable understanding and love. Every architectural detail, custom, music, and dialogue is researched from real historical documents. KCD2 is not just a great RPG; it's an important cultural work of the Czech Republic, recognized by the gaming world.

Rating: 9.4/10, RPG Simulation Thap Nien 2020s

Kingdom Come Deliverance 2 proves that an RPG doesn't need magic or holographic graphics to be impressive. It sets a new standard for authenticity in storytelling and depth in gameplay. Rated 9.4/10, it places itself on the list of must-play RPGs before you die, alongside iconic titles like Planescape Torment and Baldur's Gate 2.
