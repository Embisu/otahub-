Vinland Saga Manga Review: A Worthy Ending After 20 Years

Review of Makoto Yukimura's Vinland Saga manga: serialized from 2005 to July 25, 2025, 29 volumes, Thorfinn's journey across four arcs; anime by Wit Studio (2019) and MAPPA (2023).

Summary
Review of Makoto Yukimura's Vinland Saga manga: serialized from 2005 to July 25, 2025, 29 volumes, Thorfinn's journey across four arcs; anime by Wit Studio (2019) and MAPPA (2023).

This OtaHub review is compiled from official information and critics' reviews; it is not a personal playthrough diary. The score is an editorial judgment.

Quick Facts

  • Author: Makoto Yukimura; publisher Kodansha
  • Serialization: Weekly Shonen Magazine (April 13–October 5, 2005), then Monthly Afternoon (December 24, 2005–July 25, 2025)
  • Scale: 29 volumes; final chapter published July 25, 2025 (chapter 220), final volume released September 22, 2025
  • Awards: the 36th Kodansha Manga Award (Best General Manga, 2012) and the Grand Prize at the 13th Japan Media Arts Festival (2009)
  • Anime: season 1 by Wit Studio (24 episodes, July–December 2019), season 2 by MAPPA (24 episodes, January–June 2023)

Story

The story follows Thorfinn's transformation from a revenge-driven Viking teenager working under the mercenary Askeladd to a pacifist seeking Vinland. It spans four arcs: War, Slave, Eastern Expedition and Vinland.

Strengths

Critics praise its fluid action sequences and character development. Ending after two decades with a journey from revenge to peace is seen as the work's defining achievement.

Points To Note

Some critics note that the historical fiction setting asks readers to accept a few hard-to-believe details. The pacing of the Slave arc is slower than the War arc, so newcomers need patience.

9.6
Masterpiece
29 volumes, ended July 25, 2025; two anime seasons (2019, 2023)

Verdict

Vinland Saga is a historical manga about violence, war and the search for peace, now fully completed after 20 years. If you have not read it, you can read all 29 volumes or watch the two anime seasons first.
