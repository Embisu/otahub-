Mushoku Tensei Season 3: Chaos Breaker Arc Officially Starts From Episode 8

Mushoku Tensei Season 3's Chaos Breaker arc officially kicks off, taking Rudeus and company to the mysterious flying fortress of Perugius Dola, where an unexpected tragedy is about to strike.

Anime 2026-08-16 6 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation Season 3 officially enters the arc Chaos Breaker From episode 8 titled "Flying Fortress," airing August 16, 2026 on Crunchyroll. The new arc introduces three additional voice actors: Rikiya Koyama as Perugius Dola, Ayumi Tsunematsu as Sylvaril of the Void, and Nanako Mori as Atoferatofe Rybak, along with Kengo Kawanishi as Almanfi the Bright.

After closing the emotional Eris reunion arc, Mushoku Tensei: Jobless Reincarnation Season 3 officially moves on to the next chapter of the Rudeus Adolescence Arc: Chaos Breaker Arc. The new arc begins with episode 8 "The Flying Fortress", which aired on August 16, 2026 as part of Crunchyroll's summer 2026 lineup.

The mysterious flying fortress of Perugius

A new arc takes Nanahoshi, Rudeus, and their companions to the mysterious flying fortress of Perugius, where they seek a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to learn rare magic. But instead of a smooth adventure, the group faces tragedy when a member of the crew falls critically ill, forcing Rudeus to question how much he is willing to sacrifice to save his closest friend.

New voice cast joined

Three new voice actors officially appear in this arc: Rikiya Koyama as Perugius Dola, Ayumi Tsunematsu as Sylvaril of the Void, Nanako Mori as Atoferatofe Rybak, same Kengo Kawanishi as Almanfi the Bright. Previously, the anime also announced new character designs for the trio Rudeus, Roxy and Sylphiette when entering the Rudeus Adolescence Arc.

Production crew and original source

Season 3 continues to be directed by Ryosuke Shibuya, with character designs by Sanae Shimada and Ryota Furukawa, music by Yoshiaki Fujisawa, and produced by Studio Bind. Chaos Breaker is adapted from Volume 14 of the original light novel written by Rifujin na Magonote and illustrated by Shirotaka, revolving around Rudeus's group facing an unexpected disaster at Perugius's flying fortress.

  • Episode begins: Episode 8, "Flying Fortress", broadcast August 16, 2026
  • Studio: Studio Bind
  • Director: Ryosuke Shibuya
  • New voice acting: Rikiya Koyama, Ayumi Tsunematsu, Nanako Mori, Kengo Kawanishi
  • Broadcast platform: Crunchyroll
