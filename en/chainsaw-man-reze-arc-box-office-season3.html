Chainsaw Man – The Movie Reze Arc: Surpasses $191.4M Worldwide, MAPPA Greenlights Assassins Arc Season 3

A massive global box office triumph has solidified Chainsaw Man as MAPPA's flagship franchise — and at Jump Festa 2026, the studio officially greenlit its next project, the "Assassins Arc."

Author: OtaHub Editorial
Published: 2026-08-25
Reading time: 5 min read
Category: Anime News
Box Office Milestone
Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc has crossed $191.4 million in worldwide box office earnings, becoming one of the highest-grossing anime films ever released and MAPPA's most profitable feature to date.

1. From Japan's Box Office Throne To A North American Upset

Released in Japan on September 19, 2025, Reze Arc quickly dominated the domestic box office before pulling off a genuine surprise overseas — toppling both a Bruce Springsteen biopic and "Black Phone 2" to claim the No. 1 spot at the North American box office in its opening weekend, a rare feat for an anime theatrical release outside the Ghibli or Demon Slayer tier.

2. Peak Animation For A Tragic, Blood-Soaked Romance

Critics widely praised how MAPPA handled the short-lived, deception-laced relationship between Denji and the enigmatic Reze — paired with combat sequences pushed well beyond typical TV-anime production values, making the film one of the standout adaptations of the 2025-2026 season.

3. "Assassins Arc" Officially Confirmed At Jump Festa 2026

On December 21, at the Jump Festa 2026 event, MAPPA officially unveiled its next project, the "Assassins Arc" (Shikaku-hen), a direct continuation of the story following Reze Arc that spans roughly 18 chapters of the manga. The story follows Denji as he becomes the target of elite killers from around the world, all hunting for the Chainsaw Man's heart, pushing the series into its most politically charged and high-stakes confrontations yet.

4. TV Season Or Another Theatrical Release?

MAPPA has not yet officially confirmed the format of the project — whether it will be a television season or another cinematic release — though industry sources lean toward a TV continuation. The studio unveiled new key visuals and a trailer during its 15th anniversary livestream in June 2026, confirming the project is now in active production.
