Key Strengths (Why It Scored High)
  • Unprecedented integration of multi-plane 3D architectural cameras and hand-drawn 2D Sakuga.

1. Premise & The Infinity Castle Spatial Wonder

Ufotable thrusts the Demon Slayer Corps into the disorienting, shifting dimensions of the Infinity Castle, setting the stage for the definitive confrontation against Muzan Kibutsuji.

2. Emotional Depth: The Tragedy of Akaza

The duel between Tanjiro, Water Hashira Giyuu Tomioka, and Upper Rank Three Akaza transcends pure spectacle to become an unforgettable meditation on grief, martial pride, and redemption.
