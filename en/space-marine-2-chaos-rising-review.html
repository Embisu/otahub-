Space Marine 2: Confrontation Update Review, A Strong Close To Year 2

Saber Interactive closes out Year 2 of post-launch support for Space Marine 2 with the Confrontation update: 2 new PvP maps, a new Thousand Sons enemy, and Season Pass 2 cosmetics, ahead of Year 3 kicking off in September.

Reviews 2026-08-15 6 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Confrontation, the update that closes out Year 2 of post-launch support for Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2, adds 2 new PvP maps in Sanctum and Facility, a new Terminus enemy in the Scarab Occult Terminator Sorcerer from the Thousand Sons, an Ordeal system with weekly rewards, and 2 cosmetic packs closing out Season Pass 2. It's a solid stepping stone before Year 3 kicks off in September 2026.

After nearly two years of continuous post-launch support, Saber Interactive closes out Year 2 of Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 with the Confrontation update, released free for all players on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. This is the wrap-up update before Year 3 officially kicks off in September 2026.

Two New PvP Maps: Sanctum And Facility

Confrontation adds two new Eternal War arenas, Sanctum and Facility, bringing the total number of PvP maps to 7. Both are designed with distinct sightlines and engagement routes, helping freshen up the pace of the familiar 6v6 matches after many months.

New Enemy And The Ordeal System

On the PvE side, Confrontation introduces a new Terminus enemy from the Thousand Sons: the Scarab Occult Terminator Sorcerer, a formidable foe that demands tighter coordination in the harder Operation difficulties. The update also brings the Ordeal system along with weekly rewards, letting players earn Accolades and cosmetics through Eternal War matches and Datavault challenges.

Cosmetics Closing Out Season Pass 2

Confrontation also delivers the final two cosmetic packs of Season Pass 2: the Raptors Cosmetic Pack and Salamanders Champion Pack 2, along with new Heroic weapons and armor for existing Chapters.

The Downside: More Of A Wrap-Up Than A Leap Forward

Because it's the update closing out a year of support, Confrontation leans toward polishing and rounding out existing content rather than delivering a major breakthrough. Players hoping for new story content or big gameplay changes will likely have to wait for Year 3 to officially roll out, with its headline features, Chaos Siege Mode and a brand-new playable class, announced for early 2027.

  • Content: 2 new PvP maps, 1 new enemy, Ordeal system, Season Pass 2 cosmetics
  • Platform: PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S
  • Developer: Saber Interactive
  • Based on: the Warhammer 40,000 universe (Games Workshop)
