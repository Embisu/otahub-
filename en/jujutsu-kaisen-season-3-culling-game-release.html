Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Culling Game Part 1 Has Aired

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 (Culling Game Part 1) by MAPPA aired in winter 2026 with 12 episodes; Part 2 is in production with no air date announced.

Home › Anime › Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 Culling Game Part 1 Has Aired
Anime 2026-09-19 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 (Culling Game Part 1) by MAPPA aired in winter 2026 with 12 episodes; Part 2 is in production with no air date announced.

Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 – Culling Game Part 1 by MAPPA aired from January 9 to March 27, 2026 across 12 episodes (a one-hour opener covering the first two episodes and an extended finale), adapting the first part of the Culling Game arc from Gege Akutami’s manga. Scripts are by Hiroshi Seko.

Part 2

Culling Game Part 2 has been announced to follow directly after season 3. Takeru Satō directs, with Shōta Goshozono moving to chief director. A teaser visual of Sukuna with the line “Are you watching?” was shown at Juju Fes 2026 on August 29, 2026. There is no air date yet.

The series’ honors

Jujutsu Kaisen won Anime of the Year at the 8th Crunchyroll Anime Awards, the most-awarded anime in that ceremony’s history with eleven wins. Crunchyroll streams the series globally outside Asia with several dubs.

Articles describing a “first teaser” or an “early 2027 schedule” for season 3 are out of date: the season finished airing in March 2026.
