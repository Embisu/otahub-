Hunter x Hunter Returns With Chapter 419, Hiatus Ahead

Hunter x Hunter chapter 419 was released on August 30, 2026. Togashi’s series will leave Weekly Shonen Jump after chapter 420 for a new break.

Home › Manga › Hunter x Hunter Returns With Chapter 419, Hiatus Ahead
Manga 2026-09-23 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Hunter x Hunter chapter 419 was released on August 30, 2026. Togashi’s series will leave Weekly Shonen Jump after chapter 420 for a new break.

After a hiatus that ran from chapter 410 (December 2024) due to chronic back pain, Yoshihiro Togashi returned to Hunter x Hunter with chapter 411 on June 28, 2026 in Weekly Shonen Jump issue 31. The run continued through chapter 419, released on August 30, 2026, the latest chapter at the time of writing.

A rare 10-chapter streak

It is one of Hunter x Hunter’s longest consecutive runs in years, with 10 new chapters released steadily since June 2026. Togashi even shared an image of the finished chapter 419 manuscript on X, along with a manuscript envelope dated February 2026, showing he completed the chapter long before release.

Leaving Shonen Jump after chapter 420

On September 4, 2026, Shonen Jump confirmed Hunter x Hunter will leave the magazine after chapter 420 (September 6, issue 41), starting a new hiatus. Notably, Togashi has finished manuscripts up to chapters 428–430, a far larger stockpile than in previous breaks, although no official return date has been announced.

  • Latest chapter: Chapter 419 (August 30, 2026)
  • Return streak began: Chapter 411, June 28, 2026
  • Leaves the magazine after: Chapter 420 (September 6, 2026)
  • Stockpiled manuscripts: finished through chapters 428–430
  • Next return date: not announced
