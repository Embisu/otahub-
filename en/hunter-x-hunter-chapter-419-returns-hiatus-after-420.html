Hunter x Hunter Returns With Chapter 419, Hiatus Ahead

Hunter x Hunter chapter 419 was released on August 30, 2026. Togashi’s series will leave Weekly Shonen Jump after chapter 420 for a new break.

Home › Manga › Hunter x Hunter Returns With Chapter 419, Hiatus Ahead
Manga 2026-09-23 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Hunter x Hunter chapter 419 was released on August 30, 2026. Togashi’s series will leave Weekly Shonen Jump after chapter 420 for a new break.

Hunter x Hunter chapter 420 was released on September 6, 2026 on VIZ, closing a ten-chapter run from chapter 411 to 420. Chapter 421 has no release date yet; the notice at the end of the chapter says the next publication schedule will be announced in Weekly Shonen Jump once it has been decided.

Exact release milestones

  • Chapter 411: opened the new run in late June 2026.
  • Chapter 419: released on August 30, 2026 on VIZ.
  • Chapter 420: released on September 6, 2026 on VIZ.
  • Chapter 421: no official schedule as of September 30, 2026.

Should this be called a hiatus?

In everyday usage, having no new chapter after 420 would count as a break. The official notice is more cautious, though: the date of the next release is "not yet decided" and will be announced in the magazine. Because Hunter x Hunter no longer runs as a regular weekly series, the accurate wording is no schedule for chapter 421 yet, rather than claiming a specific length of hiatus.

Shueisha announced back in 2022 that the series would not continue on the traditional weekly model and that its publication would be arranged around the author's condition. That explains why a manuscript being in progress does not mean a chapter will immediately be scheduled in the magazine.

What does the manuscript progress tell us?

Yoshihiro Togashi often shares his progress on his official X account. ORICON reported that by September 1 he had finished the manuscript for chapter 427. That is a positive sign about how much material he has in reserve, but it is not a release announcement. A chapter still goes through editing, revisions, pre-press production and the publisher's scheduling decision.

Where to read the new chapters

VIZ Shonen Jump offers chapters 419 and 420 in supported regions. Readers should check the official chapter page rather than fan-predicted schedules. Articles that attach a specific date to chapter 421 without citing a Jump announcement are only estimates.

OtaHub's take: the important point is not guessing how long the break will last, but separating three states: the author is drawing, the manuscript is finished, and the chapter has been scheduled by the publisher. Only the last one produces a reliable release date.

Sources: VIZ Shonen Jump chapter 420, Shonen Jump and ORICON. Updated September 30, 2026.
