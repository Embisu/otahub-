Honor of Kings Global Launches New Ranked Season: Stealth Assassin Lanling Wang & Jungle Overhaul

Level Infinite and TiMi Studio Group kick off a brand new ranked season for Honor of Kings Global with stealth jungler Lanling Wang and optimized mobile controls.

Home›Mobile›Honor of Kings Global Launches New Ranked Season: Stealth Assassin Lanling Wang & Jungle Overhaul
Mobile Aug 24, 2026 8 min read OtaHub Editorial
New Season Highlights
The world's top mobile MOBA Honor of Kings Global enters Ranked Season 4. Introducing premier stealth jungler Lanling Wang, revamping jungle buffs and river terrain, and enhancing smart target-locking Smite controls.

With over 100 million daily active players, Honor of Kings Global solidifies its mobile esports leadership with a comprehensive jungle rework.

1. New Hero Spotlight: Lanling Wang (Jungle Assassin)

Utilize passive stealth to infiltrate enemy backlines, stun targets with daggers, and execute lethal finishing combos based on missing HP.

2. Jungle & Smart Smite Enhancements

Jungle buff durations are increased to 80 seconds with gesture-guided Smite targeting for decisive Dragon and Dark Tyrant contests.
