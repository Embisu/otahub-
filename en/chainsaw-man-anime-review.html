Chainsaw Man Anime Review: MAPPA Turns Crazy Manga Into Visual Masterpiece

MAPPA transforms the brutal, surreal world of Tatsuki Fujimoto into one of the most impressive visual and musical experiences in modern anime.

OtaHub › Reviews › Chainsaw Man (Anime)
Reviews 2026-08-15 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Chainsaw Man, an anime adaptation of Tatsuki Fujimoto's manga of the same name, produced by MAPPA, tells the story of Denji, a poor young man who merges with the chainsaw demon Pochita to become a demon hunter. Season 1, consisting of 12 episodes, laid a solid foundation for the franchise, paving the way for the Reze Arc (Anime Movie of the Year) film and the currently-produced Assassins Arc anime.

Few manga have such an unpredictable and brutal storytelling style Chainsaw Man by Tatsuki Fujimoto, where any character, including the protagonist, can die at any time in a completely unpredictable way. MAPPA's undertaking to adapt this work into an anime was a major challenge, both visually and in terms of capturing the spirit of the original.

Denji and Pochita: a tragic beginning

The story of Denji, a poor young man who must pay off his father's debts by hunting demons, and then merges with the chainsaw demon Pochita after being betrayed, offers a dark yet captivating beginning. The anime retains the dark tone mixed with dark humor of the original manga, not shying away from the violence and nudity that define the series.

Images and music: cinematic level investment

The most impressive aspect of the anime is MAPPA's investment in image and sound quality, a level rarely seen in a TV series. Each episode features a unique ending theme song performed by a different artist, while the visuals, especially the action scenes, are staged with genuine cinematic detail and creativity, under the direction of Ryu Nakayama and composed by Kensuke Ushio.

The foundation for a great brand

The success of season 1 paved the way Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc, the film adaptation won the title of Anime Movie of the Year, and continues with Assassins Arc is currently in production, showing that MAPPA has successfully built a solid foundation to develop the Chainsaw Man brand in the long term.

  • Number of episodes season 1: 12 episodes
  • Studio: MAPPA
  • Director: Ryu Nakayama
  • Music: Kensuke Ushio
  • Brand extension: Reze Arc movie, Assassins Arc anime

This forms a solid foundation for one of the most important action-horror anime franchises today.
