Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc Review: MAPPA Brings Denji to the Spectacular Big Screen

MAPPA adapted the Reze arc to the big screen with a 96% Rotten Tomatoes rating and more than $191 million in global revenue. OtaHub comprehensively reviews the first Chainsaw Man movie.

OtaHub › Reviews › Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc
Reviews 2026-08-15 7 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc is the first film adaptation of the series, bringing the popular "Reze" arc (chapters 39-52 of the manga) to the big screen. With a 96% Rotten Tomatoes rating from critics and 99% from audiences, the film grossed over $191 million worldwide, surpassing many Hollywood blockbusters released at the same time.

After the resounding success of season 1 of the television anime (broadcast in more than 200 countries), MAPPA launched Chainsaw Man on the big screen for the first time Reze Arc, the most famous milestone in Tatsuki Fujimoto's original manga. The film follows Denji, a boy who becomes "Chainsaw Man" after merging with the demon Pochita, when he accidentally meets Reze, a mysterious girl working at a nearby cafe, while hiding from the rain.

Animation: MAPPA pushes the limits of theatrical film budgets

Visually, the Reze Arc is clearly invested in on a completely different scale compared to the TV version. The large-scale fight sequences between Denji and Reze/Demon Bomb are significantly upgraded in detail and frame rate, making the most of the widescreen format. Director Tatsuya Yoshihara and animation director Kazutaka Sugiyama have created visually stunning explosions and urban destruction, true to the artistic violence characteristic of Chainsaw Man.

Music: Kenshi Yonezu and his historic collaboration with Hikaru Utada

The film's highlight lies in its music. Kenshi Yonezu, who wrote "KICK BACK" (the series' opening theme song, which achieved RIAA Gold certification, the first time for a Japanese song), returns with a new theme song, "IRIS OUT". But even more impactful is the ending theme song, "JANE DOE," marking the first-ever collaboration between Kenshi Yonezu and the legendary Hikaru Utada, a musical event considered just as important as the film itself.

"I didn't have a specific singer in mind when I started writing this song, but the more I did it, the more I felt that only Ms. Utada could sing it."Kenshi Yonezu, musician, composed "JANE DOE"

Revenue: a big boost for the anime wave in theaters

Commercially, Reze Arc opened with $17-18 million from 3,003 theaters in North America, far exceeding initial expectations, and became the world's #1 box office hit at its opening, less than two months after Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle's debut. Its total global revenue exceeded $191 million, making it one of the most notable box office successes of anime in 2025, further solidifying the booming global wave of anime theatrical releases.

With impressive animation, historical music, and massive box office success, this is one of the most worthwhile anime films of the year.
