Chainsaw Man Assassins Arc: MAPPA Ra Mat Anime Kinh Dien

Denji faces the most dangerous murderers from around the world, MAPPA promises bloody action and emotions far beyond season 1.

OtaHub › Anime › Chainsaw Man Assassins Arc: MAPPA Ra Mat Anime Kinh Dien
Anime 2026-06-23 6 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Chainsaw Man Assassins Arc produced by MAPPA, continuing after the events of Katana Man. New characters: Quanxi, Princi, Santa Claus. Director: Tatsuya Yoshihara. Expected 2027.

After season 1 ended with action scenes considered the pinnacle of 2022 anime, MAPPA officially confirmed the continuation of production. Chainsaw Man: Assassins Arc, the arc is considered the most complex and emotional in Tatsuki Fujimoto's entire manga.

Assassins Arc, War of Assassins

Following the events of Katana Man and Snake Devil, the Assassins Arc places Denji in his most dangerous situation yet: professional assassins from around the world are hired to kill Chainsaw Man. Not because they fear Denji, but because whoever eats Chainsaw Man will be able to eradicate a kind of fear from the world.

New Characters: Quanxi, Princi and Santa Claus

Quanxi, the Chinese murderer known as “The One” with amazing pure fighting ability. Princi, Devil incarnate of Contract, mysterious partner of Quanxi. Santa Claus, not the Santa Claus you know, but a terrifying entity from Europe with the ability to turn people into dolls.

MAPPA And Cartoon Style

Director Tatsuya Yoshihara continue to lead the project. MAPPA has confirmed they are testing a new animation style for the Assassins Arc, combining traditional hand-drawn with specially tweaked 3DCG.

Anime or Movie in Theaters?

MAPPA confirms the Assassins Arc will be TV series, not a theatrical film. Expected number of episodes: 12–16 episodes, expected broadcast in 2027.

Why Assassins Arc Is Important

The Assassins Arc is the most important turning point: it is the first time Denji truly confronts the question of the meaning of power, and its price. The plot is not just about action but also raises philosophical questions about freedom, fear and human identity.
