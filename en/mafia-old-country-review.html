Mafia: The Old Country Review: An Emotional Return to Sicilian Roots

Hangar 13 returns to its Sicilian roots in the early 20th century with an emotional, cinematic mafia story, at the expense of a more linear gameplay structure than its predecessors.

OtaHub › Reviews › Mafia: The Old Country
Reviews 2026-08-15 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Mafia: The Old Country bringing the classic series back to its roots, early 20th-century Sicily, tells the story of Enzo Favara, a young man drawn into the Sicilian mafia underworld before the organization migrated to America. The first expansion, Man of Honor, released August 14, 2026, adds two chapters that bring Enzo into contact with a young Ennio Salieri, the familiar villain from Mafia: Definitive Edition.

After two parts of the game taking players to mid-20th century America, Mafia: The Old Country do what many longtime fans have been waiting for: go back in time and space, return Sicily, the true homeland of the mafia, in the early 20th century, when this criminal organization had not yet migrated to the New World.

Enzo Favara: a humble beginning but full of depth

Players take on the role of Enzo Favara, a young man drawn into the Sicilian mafia underworld after a family tragedy. Unlike previous protagonists in the series who already held a certain position within the organization, Enzo's journey begins from the bottom of society, creating a coming-of-age story intertwined with the familiar organized crime motif of the Mafia.

Sicily in the early 20th century: a setting rich in historical detail

In terms of its worldview, Hangar 13 recreates Sicily in the early 1900s with impressive detail, from rural stone villages and citrus plantations to bustling port cities. This setting provides a completely different atmosphere from the American-style cities in previous installments, closer to classic noir with a touch of Italian mafia film.

Gameplay: more linear, but tight on storytelling

The most controversial aspect is Hangar 13's design decision to choose a linear narrative structure, focusing on meticulously crafted set pieces rather than a free-form open world to explore, contrasting with the "open world" expectations some fans had after Mafia III. In return, this approach helps maintain a tight narrative pace, less diluted by unnecessary side activities, fitting the "interactive film" spirit characteristic of the series since its first installment.

Man of Honor: DLC expansion linked to the Definitive Edition

First expansion, Man of Honor, released on August 14, 2026 for 10 USD, adds 2 chapters set in Sicily in the winter of 1905, bringing Enzo to encounter a Ennio Salieri young, the main villain who appeared in Mafia: Definitive Edition, before he became the infamous boss of Lost Heaven. This is a highly appreciated "installation" connecting the universe of the series because it adds depth to both works.

  • Platform: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam)
  • Background: Sicily in the early 20th century
  • Man of Honor DLC: August 14, 2026, price 10 USD
  • Development: Hangar 13 · Released: 2K

For those who prefer a more tightly woven narrative over a free-flowing sandbox, this is one of the most memorable mafia experiences in recent times.
