Top 10 Best Anime 2025: Year of Peaks

2025 is the best anime year of the decade, from Solo Leveling S2 to Jujutsu Kaisen ending, Dragon Ball DAIMA and many surprises.

Home › Rankings › Top 10 Best Anime 2025: Year of Peaks
Rankings 2026-06-21 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Top anime 2025: Solo Leveling S2 (1), JJK final arc (2), DAIMA (3), Mushoku Tensei S3 (4), Vinland Saga S3 (5). The Winter and Spring seasons are especially strong. Featured studios: A-1 Pictures, MAPPA, WIT.

Every year someone claims it's "anime's best year," but 2025 truly deserves that title. Not because of the number of anime, but because of the density of excellent titles: rarely does the top 5 all score above 8.5 like this.

1. Solo Leveling Season 2, The Pinnacle of Anime Webtoon

A-1 Pictures continues to do the unthinkable: turning a webtoon manhwa into a cinema-quality anime. The Jeju Island arc is the best anime sequence of 2025, and the appearance of the Monarchs opens up a whole new scale. 9.0/10. Worthwhile reason #1: there are no significant weaknesses in the entire 13 episodes.

2. Jujutsu Kaisen, The Perfect Farewell

JJK's final arc concludes the manga with some of the highest quality anime episodes MAPPA has ever produced. Although the manga's ending is controversial, the anime adaptation of the Sukuna final arc is beyond criticism. 8.9/10.

3. Dragon Ball DAIMA, Toriyama's Legacy

The last anime title under the direct supervision of Akira Toriyama before his death. DAIMA is not the perfect Dragon Ball, but it is the best Dragon Ball since the peak years of Z. 8.2/10.

4. Mushoku Tensei Season 3, The Most Serious Isekai

Studio Bind continues to maintain quality with the arc where Rudeus matures in both strength and personality. Mushoku Tensei remains the benchmark of serious isekai, and season 3 is the best so far. 8.8/10.

5. Vinland Saga Season 3, The Road to Peace Continues

Wit Studio continues to adapt Vinland Saga with absolute respect for the original. Thorfinn's journey to find a land without war remains one of manga's best stories about philosophy and humanity. 9.1/10.

6-10: Surprises and Stability

Position 6 to 10: Blue Box (light romance but perfectly executed, 8.5/10), Dandadan S2 (bizarre action comedy continues to do well, 8.3/10), Bleach TYBW Part Final (worthy ending, 8.4/10), Freeren S2 (most emotional slice-of-life isekai of the year, 9.0/10), Oshi no Ko S2 (darkest entertainment industry in anime, 8.6/10).

Conclusion: 2025, The Year of Great Anime

If we had to choose a recurring theme for the top anime of 2025, it would be: animation quality is evolving faster than ever before. A-1 Pictures, MAPPA, Wit, and Bind are all pushing the boundaries of technique and artistry in 2025. This is no coincidence; it's the result of increasing investment and competition among studios.
