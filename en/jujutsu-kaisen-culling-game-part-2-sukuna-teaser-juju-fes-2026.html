Jujutsu Kaisen Culling Game Part 2: Sukuna Teaser

At Juju Fes 2026 marking five years of the anime, MAPPA unveiled a new teaser visual for Culling Game Part 2 centered on Sukuna with the chilling line “Are you watching?”

Home › Anime › Jujutsu Kaisen Culling Game Part 2: Sukuna Teaser
Anime 2026-09-23 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
At Juju Fes 2026 marking five years of the anime, MAPPA unveiled a new teaser visual for Culling Game Part 2 centered on Sukuna with the chilling line “Are you watching?”

Marking five years since the Jujutsu Kaisen anime began, Juju Fes 2026 in Japan on August 29, 2026 delivered a big surprise: the first teaser visual for Culling Game Arc Part 2, the follow-up to Culling Game Part 1, which aired in early 2026.

Sukuna rising from the water

The new visual shows Ryomen Sukuna rising from murky water, his cold eyes staring straight at the viewer, a hand seeming to reach out of the frame with his signature smirk. The accompanying line, translated as “Are you watching?”, feels like Sukuna speaking directly to the audience.

Part 2 will center on Sukuna

According to the event, Culling Game Part 2 will be Sukuna’s “main stage” as the scattered battlefields of the Culling Game converge and push the story into a new phase. Among the manga battles fans hope to see adapted are Kinji Hakari vs. Hajime Kashimo and Kenjaku vs. Choso & Yuki Tsukumo.

Air date still unknown

Although the visual confirms Part 2 is in production at MAPPA, no official air date was announced at the event.

  • Announced at: Juju Fes 2026, August 29, 2026
  • What: first teaser visual for Culling Game Arc Part 2
  • Central character: Ryomen Sukuna
  • Air date: not announced
  • Studio: MAPPA

Sources: Anime Corner, AniTrendz, Complex, FandomWire.
