Crunchyroll Manga Adds Black Torch, Hana-Kimi in July

Crunchyroll Manga added Black Torch, Hana-Kimi and more titles to its library on July 13, 2026.

Home › Manga › Crunchyroll Manga Adds Black Torch, Hana-Kimi in July
Manga 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
In short: Crunchyroll Manga added Black Torch, Hana-Kimi and more titles on July 13, 2026, with content from Shueisha, VIZ Media, One Peace Books, Titan Manga and MobileBook.jp. The new titles are currently exclusive to Crunchyroll Manga subscribers in the US and Canada.

Crunchyroll Manga has added Black Torch, Hana-Kimi and several other titles to its library in a catalog update announced on July 13, 2026, with content coming from publishers Shueisha, VIZ Media, One Peace Books, Titan Manga and MobileBook.jp.

Black Torch, written by Tsuyoshi Takaki and originally serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump, follows a delinquent teenager who can speak with animals. Hana-Kimi, a classic shojo series by Hisaya Nakajo from the early 2000s, follows Japanese-American student Mizuki Ashiya as she disguises herself as a boy to attend the all-boys Osaka Gakuen High School.

The update also added Villainess Level 99 among other titles. According to Crunchyroll, the new additions are currently exclusive to Crunchyroll Manga subscribers in the US and Canada, readable through the Crunchyroll Manga app.
