$70–80 Games: Who Still Pays Full Price at Launch?

One Reddit question, a lot of honest answers: who still pays $70–80 for new games now that GTA 6 is locked at $79.99? OtaHub rounds up the debate and checks how fast AAA prices fall on Steam in the US and Vietnam.

Home › Gaming › $70–80 Games: Who Still Pays Full Price at Launch?
Gaming 2026-10-02 9 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
The most upvoted answer on r/AskGames is blunt: anyone who wants to play new games. But most commenters buy selectively, one or two full-price games a year, and wait for sales on the rest. Steam data from October 2, 2026 shows Battlefield 6 and Borderlands 4, both $69.99 at launch, now selling for $34.99; in Vietnam, their regional base prices are about 30% below a straight dollar conversion.

"Who is actually paying $70–80 for new games nowadays?" When that question went up on r/AskGames in mid-September 2026, it read like a complaint, but the replies were refreshingly blunt. The top answer was a single line: "Anyone who wants to play new games." It is a fair question with GTA 6 locked at $79.99 for its November 19, 2026 launch. OtaHub rounds up what the community said and checks it against real prices on Steam in the US and in Vietnam.

"Anyone who wants to play new games": the answer most people agreed with

"Anyone who wants to play new games" collected around 137 upvotes, the highest of the replies OtaHub reviewed. The point is simple: $70 is not a barrier for people who genuinely want to play on day one. One reply said they had not bought a game in a while because of their backlog, but they would definitely buy the Ocarina of Time remake on Switch 2, which was just announced in the September 2026 Nintendo Direct.

The same thread quickly split. "I want to play new games, but I’m happy to wait for a sale," one person wrote. Another was even shorter: "The $20 games I buy are new to me. Don’t really see the difference personally other than marketing and hype." Those are the three kinds of players that run through the whole discussion: buy now for the passion, buy selectively, and never pay full price.

Gaming is still a cheap hobby, if you count the hours

The second most upvoted comment (around 132) came at it from another angle: "I buy games to keep me inside and not spend money. Gaming is a pretty cheap hobby if you do it right." A reply added that it is the cheapest hobby, unless you are pouring tens of thousands into gambling-style games.

The most common argument was cost per hour. One user worked out that a game taking about 20 hours to finish costs only $3 an hour at $60. Another went further: 70 hours of play brings it down to about $1 an hour. Several compared it with a night out:

It’s easy to blow $70 on a night out with dinner and drinks. That’s fun, but is over after a few hours. A game with good replay value can be hundreds or even thousands of hours of entertainment.An r/AskGames user

Applied to GTA 6’s $79.99 price, and converted at Vietcombank’s selling rate on October 2, 2026 (26,190 VND per dollar), the math looks like this:

The debate even spilled over into books. One reader said they finish one or two books a month at $40–50, up to $600 a year, while they had spent only about $200 on games this year. The other side answered in four words: "Libraries are a thing." Both camps landed in the same place: any hobby can be cheap or expensive depending on how you do it.

The 90s were pricier: a lesson in inflation

Another branch of the thread went down memory lane. One user pointed out that Super Mario Bros. 3 was $49 at release, roughly $125 in today’s money. Others remembered Final Fantasy VI at $60–70 in 1994 and Chrono Trigger at $70 or $80; one person said they paid $79.99 for it at Target. Someone recalled Street Fighter II at about $75, which would be over $170 today if it had tracked inflation.

Those are players’ memories, not official price lists, but they point the same way as the data. According to the US consumer price index, $60 in 2006, when $59.99 was already the standard price for Xbox 360 and PS3 games, is worth about $99.70 in 2026. In real terms, a $70 AAA game today is still cheaper than a $60 game from 20 years ago.

That argument does not convince everyone. As one person put it, in 2002 convincing their mom to buy a new PS2 game was "like convincing her to buy a new washer and dryer." Inflation makes games cheaper on paper, but incomes do not rise at the same pace for everyone, or in every country.

From $59.99 to $69.99, and now $79.99

The $70–80 era did not arrive overnight. These are the key milestones:

  • July 2020: NBA 2K21 on PS5 and Xbox Series X became the first game to announce a $69.99 price, $10 above the familiar $59.99. That price quickly became the standard for the new console generation.
  • June 2025: Mario Kart World launched alongside Nintendo Switch 2 at $79.99, the first $80 game on Nintendo’s new console.
  • July 2025: Microsoft planned to sell The Outer Worlds 2 at $79.99, but after player backlash it went back to $69.99 on July 23, 2025 and refunded the difference to pre-order customers.
  • September and October 2025: Borderlands 4 (September 12) and Battlefield 6 (October 10) both launched at $69.99.
  • June 2026: Rockstar opened GTA 6 pre-orders on June 25, 2026: $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, launching November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See OtaHub’s GTA 6 countdown for more.

Worth noting: after Microsoft’s retreat, $79.99 did not become the norm. But the biggest names, Mario Kart and GTA, can still charge it, because players are willing to pay.

These discounts were live when OtaHub checked and can end at any time. Still, the trend is clear: within 12 to 18 months, many AAA games are half price or less. One Reddit user described exactly this approach: they limit themselves to one or two expensive new games a year, spent about $50 on Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 and Star Wars Zero Company this year, plus another $50–60 on sale games. About $120 in total, for more than a dozen games played.

What do players in Vietnam actually pay for a "$70" game?

For players in Vietnam, $70–80 needs a different lens. Steam uses regional pricing, so base prices in VND are usually lower than a straight conversion from dollars. At Vietcombank’s selling rate on October 2, 2026, $69.99 is about 1.83 million VND and $79.99 about 2.09 million VND.

  • Battlefield 6: base price 1,299,000 VND on Steam Vietnam, about 29% below the converted price.
  • Borderlands 4: base price 1,210,000 VND, about 34% lower.
  • EA SPORTS FC 26: $69.99 in the US but only 490,000 VND on Steam Vietnam, a huge gap.

GTA 6 is a different story: it launches only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and as of October 2, 2026 there is no official VND price. Converted directly, console players should budget about 2.1 million VND for the Standard Edition. Nintendo Switch 2, which rose to $499.99 in the US on September 1, 2026, shows hardware costs are climbing along with game prices.

Is Game Pass the way out?

Some players subscribe instead of buying. On October 1, 2025, Microsoft raised Xbox Game Pass Ultimate by 50% to $29.99 a month, promising more than 75 day-one releases a year. Twelve months costs about $360, the price of four to five $79.99 games. It pays off if you play a lot of Xbox games at launch. If you only wait for one or two big releases a year, buying on sale is still cheaper.

When is full price worth it?

After reading the whole thread, the fairest answer may be the shortest reply: "Depends on the game." OtaHub’s rule of thumb:

  • Buy now: multiplayer games, where the player base is biggest in the first months; a series you have waited years for and do not want spoiled; a game you know you will play for 40+ hours.
  • Wait: short, single-player games, or when your backlog is already long. As the table shows, waiting 6–12 months often saves 30–50%.
  • Always check: your local Steam price before buying keys or topping up a wallet in another region, because the local price may already be lower.

FAQ: $70–80 game prices

How much does GTA 6 cost?

Rockstar priced the GTA 6 Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99. The game launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S; there is no PC release date yet.

What was the first $70 game?

NBA 2K21 on PS5 and Xbox Series X, priced at $69.99 in July 2020. $69.99 then became the common price for new-generation AAA games.

How long until AAA games go on sale?

There is no fixed timeline. On Steam on October 2, 2026, Battlefield 6 (launched October 10, 2025) and Borderlands 4 (September 12, 2025) were both 50% off at $34.99, while Monster Hunter Wilds and DOOM: The Dark Ages had permanently lower list prices than at launch.

Are games cheaper on Steam Vietnam than at US prices?

Usually, because Steam uses regional pricing. Battlefield 6 has a base price of 1,299,000 VND in Vietnam, while $69.99 converts to about 1.83 million VND at Vietcombank’s selling rate on October 2, 2026.

OtaHub’s take

The Reddit question sounds like a complaint about prices, but the answers describe a fairly level-headed market. People who want to play right away still pay $70–80 for names like GTA 6, Mario Kart or Zelda. Most of the rest buy selectively, wait for sales, and still play plenty of games a year for less than a night out. For players in Vietnam, Steam’s regional pricing is a real advantage. The exception is console exclusives like GTA 6, where about 2 million VND for one game will be a genuine decision this November.
