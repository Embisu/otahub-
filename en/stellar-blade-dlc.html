Stellar Blade x NIKKE: Free Crossover DLC With Boss Scarlet And New Costumes

Shift Up releases a free crossover DLC between Stellar Blade and Goddess of Victory: NIKKE, adding wandering swordfighter Scarlet as a new boss, a lineup of Eve costumes inspired by the NIKKE cast, and a brand-new shooting minigame.

Gaming2026-06-115 minutes to readOtaHub Editorial
Summary
Stellar Blade x Goddess of Victory: NIKKE is a free crossover DLC on PS5 and PC. Main content: boss Scarlet in Boss Challenge Mode (unlocking a new costume, hairstyle, and song for Eve), a costume lineup inspired by Rapi, Modernia, Anis, Alice, and Dorothy, an in-game shop run by a NIKKE character, a new shooting minigame, NIKKE-themed collectible fish, and Volt the dog appearing to interact with Eve.

Stellar Blade continues to be Shift Up's surprise success story, and instead of a traditional story DLC, the Korean studio chose to celebrate that success with a free crossover with its own other hit gacha title: Goddess of Victory: NIKKE. The DLC is available on both PS5 and PC starting June 11, 2026.

Boss Scarlet: A New Challenge In Boss Challenge Mode

The biggest highlight of the crossover is Scarlet, the wandering swordfighter from NIKKE, who appears as a new boss in Boss Challenge Mode. The fight only unlocks after players buy out Volt's entire inventory. Defeating Scarlet unlocks the Wandering Swordfighter costume, along with a new hairstyle and song for Eve.

Eve Costumes Inspired By The NIKKE Cast

The DLC adds a new lineup of costumes for Eve, styled after well-known NIKKE characters including Rapi, Modernia, Anis, Alice, and Dorothy. All the costumes and cosmetic items are sold in a dedicated in-game shop, run by a NIKKE character behind the counter.

Shooting Minigame And Volt The Dog

Alongside the combat content, the update also introduces a brand-new shooting minigame mode inspired by NIKKE's original gameplay, plus a collectible fish system themed around the game's characters. Volt, NIKKE's familiar dog mascot, also shows up and interacts directly with Eve throughout the event.

Free, True To Shift Up's Style

Like most of Stellar Blade's post-launch content, this crossover is completely free for anyone who already owns the game on PS5 or PC. It's also one of the ways Shift Up is keeping the community engaged while waiting for more concrete details on the true sequel, Stellar Blade: Blood Rain, just announced at Summer Game Fest 2026.
