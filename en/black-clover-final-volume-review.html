Black Clover Ending Review: 11 Years and a Perfect Ending

Yuki Tabata closes the 11-year journey of Asta and the Black Bulls with a warm, worthy ending, supported by the massive Perfect Grimoire guidebook for longtime fans.

OtaHub › Reviews › Black Clover
Reviews 2026-08-15 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
More later 11 years posted, Black Clover officially closed completely with Volume 38 Released in Japan on August 4, 2026, along with the official manual Perfect Grimoire, includes profiles of more than 250 characters, details of more than 400 spells, a Q&A with the author, and an all-new 15-page short story drawn exclusively for this publication by Yuki Tabata himself.

From a manga that started modestly in 2015 with a seemingly familiar "main character without magic in a magical world" motif, Black Clover developed into one of Weekly Shounen Jump's most enduring shonen, before Yuki Tabata officially ended her run after more 11 years Post continuously.

Asta and his journey from zero to the King of Magic

Throughout 38 volumes, the journey of Asta, a boy without magic but harboring the ambition to become the Wizard King, has encompassed all the classic elements of the shonen genre: friendship, confrontations with demons, inter-kingdom wars, and ultimately, the remarkable maturation of the entire Black Bulls cast. The final chapter is considered to provide a complete feeling, concluding exactly what the series had built from the beginning instead of abruptly changing direction.

Final pace: worthy but somewhat rushed

A drawback pointed out by some readers is that the pace in the final chapters is somewhat more rushed than the slow-paced climax building style usually seen in most of the series, with some minor plot points feeling resolved faster than necessary to bring the story to a close. However, Tabata still gives ample space to the central emotional moments for Asta, Yuno, and the Black Bulls to create lasting impact.

Perfect Grimoire: a huge gift for longtime fans

Included with Volume 38 is the official manual Perfect Grimoire, one of the most comprehensive guidebooks Shueisha has ever released for a shonen series, featuring detailed profiles of over 250 characters, a catalog of over 400 spells appearing throughout the story, a Q&A session with Yuki Tabata about the creative process, and especially a brand-new 15-page short story illustrated by the author herself, a special thank-you gift for readers who have been with the series for 11 years.

  • Final volume: Volume 38, released August 4, 2026 in Japan
  • Posting time: more than 11 years (since 2015)
  • Guidebook: Black Clover: Perfect Grimoire
  • Author: Yuki Tabata (Shueisha, Weekly Shounen Jump)
