THE GHOST IN THE SHELL (2026): Season Wrap-Up

THE GHOST IN THE SHELL, Science SARU's new anime adaptation based on Masamune Shirow's original manga, has finished airing after running from July to September 2026.

Home › Anime › THE GHOST IN THE SHELL (2026): Season Wrap-Up
Anime 2026-09-27 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
THE GHOST IN THE SHELL, Science SARU's new anime adaptation based on Masamune Shirow's original manga, has finished airing after running from July to September 2026.

The 2026 version of THE GHOST IN THE SHELL has finished with 10 episodes, broadcast on Kansai TV and Fuji TV from July 7 to the final episode on September 8, 2026. The series is produced by Science SARU and returns to much of the material directly from Shirow Masamune's 1989 manga, rather than continuing the plot of the 1995 film or Stand Alone Complex.

Quick facts about the season

  • Episodes: 10; the final episode carries the parts "BRAIN DRAIN iii," "GHOST COAST" and "EPILOGUE."
  • Director: Mokochan.
  • Screenplay: Toh EnJoe.
  • Character design and chief animation director: Shuhei Handa.
  • Animation production: Science SARU.
  • Opening theme: "GO GHOST" by King Gnu; ending theme "Blue" by MILLENNIUM PARADE with Saya Gray and Daniel Caesar.

What does the story focus on?

It is set in a society where cybernetic bodies, brain-machine interfaces and data networks have become part of everyday life. Major Motoko Kusanagi leads Public Security Section 9 in handling cases that weave together technological crime, politics and questions about consciousness. The main storyline revolves around a mysterious hacker called the Puppet Master, but the episodes also explore a number of standalone cases from the manga's world.

The central question is not only "who controls the system" but also what makes an individual count as human when memories can be edited and bodies can be replaced. The name "ghost" refers to the hard-to-define part that is consciousness or the self; the "shell" is the body that holds it. The series puts this boundary under pressure from AI, data duplication and self-determination.

How is it different from the 1995 film and Stand Alone Complex?

The three versions share characters and concepts but are independent interpretations. Mamoru Oshii's 1995 film has a slow pace, a philosophical atmosphere and heavily cinematic city imagery. Stand Alone Complex expands Section 9 through a long-form investigative structure. The 2026 version has designs and energy closer to the manga, including the Fuchikoma and a satirical humor that was less prominent in some earlier adaptations.

So newcomers can start directly with the 2026 version. Knowing the older versions helps you see how each team handles the same ideas, but it is not a requirement for understanding the story.

Where to watch

In Japan, Prime Video is the service that streams early, after the TV broadcast; platforms such as Netflix, Disney+, Hulu and U-NEXT add it afterward on their domestic schedules. International licensing coverage may differ, so viewers in Vietnam need to check each service's library directly. A platform having the show in Japan does not mean it will definitely appear in every country.

Spoiler-free overall impression

Ten episodes make for a short, condensed season rich in visual ideas. Its strengths are Science SARU's animation personality and the restoration of the manga's varied tones; in return, the density of concepts and the speed of moving between cases may mean viewers unfamiliar with cyberpunk need to watch more slowly. It is a fitting choice if you want a new entry point to Ghost in the Shell without going through the franchise's entire history.
