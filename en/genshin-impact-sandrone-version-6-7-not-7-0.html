Genshin Impact launched September 28, 2020 (PS5 April 28, 2021; Xbox Series X|S November 20, 2024; HarmonyOS NEXT September 10, 2025). Sandrone, the 7th Fatui Harbinger, became a playable Cryo character in version 6.7, not 7.0. Version 7.0 "Everwinter Without Mercy" launched August 12, 2026, opening Snezhnaya with Odette and Alyosha, a third-person shooter mode and new Stellar reactions; version 6.8 was skipped.

The game had generated about $6.3 billion by its fourth anniversary and had roughly 57.3 million monthly players in 2025. Miliastra Wonderland debuted in October 2025, and in 2025 the FTC required miHoYo to pay $20 million over gacha-odds disclosures and COPPA violations.
