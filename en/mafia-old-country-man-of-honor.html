Mafia: The Old Country · Man of Honor DLC Launches Today, Brings Ennio Salieri Back to Sicily

The Man of Honor expansion for Mafia: The Old Country officially launched on August 14, 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, priced at 10 USD, with 2 new story chapters revolving around Enzo Favara and a young Ennio Salieri before becoming a notorious boss.

Home › Gaming › Mafia: Old Country · Man of Honor DLC Released Today
Gaming 2026-08-14 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Hangar 13 and 2K officially release Man of Honor, the first expansion for Mafia: The Old Country, today, August 14, 2026, on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam), for $10. The DLC adds two new story chapters set in Sicily during the winter of 1905, bringing Enzo Favara into contact with a young Ennio Salieri, the main antagonist of Mafia: Definitive Edition, before he becomes the notorious boss of Lost Heaven.

Mafia: The Old Country, the prequel to Hangar 13, set in early 20th-century Sicily, has just received its first paid content package. Man of Honor officially launches today, August 14, 2026, on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam, for $10 and requires players to already own the original game. This is the first expansion of the Mafia universe since The Old Country launched, and also the first time the series delves into the past of one of its most beloved villains.

Plot: Enzo Favara encounters young Ennio Salieri

Man of Honor includes 2 new story chapters, takes place in the early stages of Enzo Favara's service to the Torrisi family. The setting is Sicily in the winter of 1905: Enzo has proven his loyalty as a reliable warrior, and the Don assigns him and Cesare the task of assisting Ennio Salieri, a recently released \"man of honor\" seeking to reclaim what rightfully belongs to him. For long-time Mafia fans, the name Salieri is familiar: he is the powerful Don of the Salieri family in Lost Heaven in the original Mafia and its remake, Mafia: Definitive Edition. Man of Honor allows gamers to witness a younger, more "rough" version of Salieri, still respected in Sicily but now having to fend for himself in the family's affairs.

"Putting Salieri at the center of Man of Honor's Free Ride content allows us to portray who he was before he immigrated to America and became a powerful tycoon, a very interesting way to deepen the character's background."Josh Zammit, Design Director, Hangar 13

Extended Free Ride content: Salieri's Challenges and Jobs

Besides the two story chapters, owning Man of Honor also unlocks a series of exclusive missions in Free Ride mode, where players are free to explore the Sicilian countryside or undertake recurring challenges separate from the main storyline. Salieri, currently hiding in a warehouse in Porto Almaro smuggling goods, will offer players two types of contracts:

  • Challenges: Harder versions of existing Free Ride challenges, requiring significantly more skill
  • Jobs: Completely new mission types, hunt for hidden treasure, tow high-value carts to Salieri, or transport fragile goods without damaging them
  • Map of Valle Dorata Added new locations and collectibles
  • Wanted Posters collected, using members of the Hangar 13 development team as portraits, a familiar tradition of the series
  • New Stunt Jumps allow you to fly over tracks in a classic early 20th century car

Exclusive weapons, Charms and costumes

Completing Salieri's Jobs and Challenges will yield Dinari, in-game currency, which can be exchanged for exclusive DLC-only outfits, Charms, weapons, vehicles and horses. One of the most notable weapons is Rimbalzu throwing knife, has the ability to bounce to a second target, allowing it to silently defeat multiple enemies with just one throw, a game design detail that favors a "hands-on" feeling rather than absolute historical authenticity, according to the development team.

Price, platform and ownership method

  • Release date: 14/8/2026
  • Price: 10 USD
  • Platform: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam)
  • Content: 2 new story chapters, Free Ride expansion (Jobs, Challenges), exclusive items and equipment
  • Requirements: Already own the original Mafia: The Old Country
  • Developer: Hangar 13 · Publisher: 2K

Meaning for the Mafia series

Man of Honor isn't just a side quest to extend gameplay; it's how Hangar 13 expands the depth of one of the Mafia's most beloved villains, while also revealing connections between The Old Country and the original trilogy. With a young Ennio Salieri at the center of the story, longtime fans have a chance to better understand the journey of a "man of honor" becoming a notorious crime boss in Lost Heaven.
