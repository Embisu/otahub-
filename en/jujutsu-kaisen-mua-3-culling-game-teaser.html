Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 (Culling Game Arc) Releases First Teaser Trailer: MAPPA Slates Early 2027 Premiere

Studio MAPPA releases the explosive first teaser trailer for Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3: Culling Game, showcasing Yuta Okkotsu and Hakari Kinji.

Home›Anime›Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3 (Culling Game Arc) Releases First Teaser Trailer: MAPPA Slates Early 2027 Premiere
Anime Aug 24, 2026 7 min read OtaHub Editorial
Teaser Highlights
Following the critically acclaimed Shibuya Incident, MAPPA and TOHO Animation have released the first 90-second teaser trailer for Jujutsu Kaisen Season 3: Culling Game Arc. The teaser spotlights Special Grade Sorcerer Yuta Okkotsu returning to execute Yuji Itadori, alongside third-year powerhouse Hakari Kinji and deadly attorney Higuruma Hiromi.

At JUMP Special Anime Festa 2026 in Tokyo, fans received their first look at MAPPA's adaptation of Gege Akutami's thrilling and complex Culling Game Arc.

1. Tokyo in Ruins: The 10-Colony Battle Royale

Following Gojo Satoru's sealing, Kenjaku's deadly ritual forces ancient and modern sorcerers to clash in a ruthless survival tournament across Japan.

2. Major Characters Revealed: Yuta Okkotsu & Hakari Kinji

Yuta wields his katana under Rika's ominous shadow, while Hakari Kinji prepares to unleash his reality-defying Pachinko Domain Expansion.
