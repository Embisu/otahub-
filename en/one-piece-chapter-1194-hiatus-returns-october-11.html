One Piece Goes On Hiatus After Chapter 1194, Returns October 11

One Piece chapter 1194 released on September 27, 2026 in Weekly Shonen Jump, after which the manga goes on hiatus, with chapter 1195 expected to return on October 11, 2026.

Key Takeaway
One Piece chapter 1194 released on September 27, 2026 in Weekly Shonen Jump, after which the manga goes on hiatus, with chapter 1195 expected to return on October 11, 2026.

One Piece chapter 1194 released on September 27, 2026 in Weekly Shonen Jump issue 44, but at the end of the chapter, the magazine confirmed the manga would go on a two-week hiatus. Chapter 1195 is expected to return on October 11, 2026.

Key Details

  • Latest chapter: 1194, released September 27, 2026
  • Return date: chapter 1195, expected October 11, 2026
  • Context: part of the three-chapters-then-break schedule Oda has been running lately
  • Recent story: the battle at Elbaf, including Zoro vs. Sommers and Sanji vs. Killingham

Why Oda Keeps Taking Breaks

Beyond the health concerns that have been widely reported in recent years, Oda is also deeply involved in production of Netflix's live-action adaptation, currently filming in South Africa. Alternating three chapters with a break has become the manga's regular rhythm during the Elbaf arc.

Sources: GameRant, CBR, OtakuKart. As of September 28, 2026.
