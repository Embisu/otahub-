Haikyu!! The Movie: VS The Little Giant Reveals New Visual

The annual Haikyu!! Day event unveiled a new concept visual featuring Hinata and Hoshiumi, along with fresh details on the film that will close out the franchise.

Home › Anime › Haikyu!! The Movie: VS The Little Giant Reveals New Visual
Anime 2026-08-18 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
In short: The Haikyu!! Day 2026 event on August 19 revealed a new visual for Haikyu!! The Movie: VS The Little Giant and details on the special anime Where Monsters Go, both set for release in 2027.

The annual Haikyu!! Day event held on August 19, 2026 revealed new information about the upcoming film Haikyu!! the Movie: VS The Little Giant as well as the special anime Haikyu!! Where Monsters Go.

The new concept visual revealed at the event features Shoyo Hinata and Korai Hoshiumi, two players both tied to the "Little Giant" title. The film will focus on Karasuno's match against Kamomedai, highlighting the confrontation between the two spikers. Voice actors Ayumu Murase (Hinata) and Kaito Ishikawa (Kageyama) also appeared during the program.

Both VS The Little Giant and the special anime Where Monsters Go (centered on the Fukurodani vs. Mujinazaka match) are set to release in 2027. This will be the final film for the Haikyu!! franchise, catching the adaptation up with the source manga, which concluded back in 2020.

The event also announced a special two-volume manga, Haikyu!! Character Remix: Bonds – Hinata and Kageyama, focused on the relationship between the two characters, releasing in Japan starting September 4, 2026.
