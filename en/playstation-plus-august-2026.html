PlayStation Plus August 2026: Dying Light 2, Big Walk

Sony has confirmed the three PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for August 2026: Dying Light 2 Stay Human: Reloaded Edition, Big Walk and Signalis, available to members from August 4 through August 31.

Key Takeaway
In short: Sony confirmed the PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for August 2026: Dying Light 2 Stay Human: Reloaded Edition, Big Walk and Signalis, available August 4 through 31.

Sony has officially confirmed the three PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for August 2026: Dying Light 2 Stay Human: Reloaded Edition, Big Walk and Signalis, all available to PS Plus members from August 4 through August 31.

The headliner is Dying Light 2 Stay Human, though not the original 2022 release. It is the Reloaded Edition, which bundles in the Bloody Ties story DLC. Big Walk, the latest game from House House, the studio behind Untitled Goose Game, arrives day one on PS Plus and is a PS5 exclusive. Signalis, an isometric survival horror game inspired by classic Resident Evil with a bleak sci-fi art style, rounds out the month's three titles.

Beyond the Monthly Games, Sony is also adding the Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls pack to PlayStation Plus starting August 6. This month's PS Plus Extra and Premium catalog additionally brings in Helldivers 2, Kingdom Come: Deliverance II, Vampire Survivors and Hell Is Us.
