Frieren Season 2 Aired From January 2026 (10 Episodes)

Madhouse’s Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 2 aired January 16–March 27, 2026 with 10 episodes; Season 3 (Golden Land arc) is set for October 2027.

Key Takeaway
Madhouse’s Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End Season 2 aired January 16–March 27, 2026 with 10 episodes; Season 3 (Golden Land arc) is set for October 2027.

Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End (Sousou no Frieren) is an anime by Madhouse adapting the manga by Kanehito Yamada (story) and Tsukasa Abe (art), serialized in Weekly Shonen Sunday since April 28, 2020.

The seasons

  • Season 1: 28 episodes, from September 29, 2023 (a two-hour opener) to March 22, 2024, directed by Keiichirō Saitō
  • Season 2: 10 episodes, January 16–March 27, 2026, directed by Tomoya Kitagawa
  • Season 3: announced after the season 2 finale, adapting the “Golden Land” arc, expected October 2027

Manga and awards

As of January 2026 the manga has over 35 million copies in circulation, with 15 volumes as of December 2025. It has won the 14th Manga Taishō, the 25th Tezuka Osamu Cultural Prize (New Creator), the 69th Shogakukan Manga Award and the 48th Kodansha Manga Award (shōnen).

Because season 2 ended in March 2026, articles describing a “new trailer” or “journey to Aureole” as not-yet-aired in September 2026 are out of date.
