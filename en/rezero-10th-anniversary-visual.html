Re:ZERO Reveals 10th Anniversary Visual, Announces Tokyo Exhibition

Re:ZERO unveils a 10th anniversary commemorative visual featuring its full main cast, promoting the anime's first-ever exhibition at Shibuya BEAM Gallery in Tokyo from September 12 to October 4, 2026.

Home › Anime › Re:ZERO Reveals 10th Anniversary Visual, Announces Tokyo Exhibition
Anime 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
In short: Re:ZERO unveils a 10th anniversary commemorative visual featuring its full main cast, promoting the anime's first-ever exhibition at Shibuya BEAM Gallery in Tokyo from September 12 to October 4, 2026.

Re:ZERO -Starting Life in Another World- has unveiled a new commemorative visual for the anime's 10th anniversary, promoting the franchise's first-ever exhibition dedicated to the series, set to take place in Tokyo this September.

The visual was produced by studio WHITE FOX and features Subaru, Emilia, Rem, Ram, Beatrice, Otto, Garfiel, Roswaal, Petra, Frederica and Meili. It's the latest milestone in a year-long celebration marking ten years since the anime first premiered in 2016.

The visual promotes the Re:ZERO 10th Anniversary Exhibition, the franchise's first event dedicated entirely to the anime, which will run at Shibuya BEAM Gallery in Tokyo from September 12 to October 4, 2026. Visitors will be able to see production materials spanning the anime's history, including original key animation, storyboards and background art. Because the event is expected to draw large crowds, some dates will require advance reservations with timed entry.

The anniversary celebrations are running alongside the ongoing broadcast of Re:ZERO Season 4. The first half, the Loss Arc, premiered in April 2026, while the second half, the Recapture Arc, began on August 12, continuing Subaru's story at the Pleiades Watchtower. Beyond Season 4, 2026 includes a total of ten major anniversary projects, including livestream events featuring the voice cast, interactive quizzes, popularity polls and convention appearances such as Anime Expo 2026.
