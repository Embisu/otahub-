Chainsaw Man and One Piece Both Nominated at the 2026 Harvey Awards

Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc and One Piece Season 2 are both nominated for Best Adaptation at the 2026 Harvey Awards.

Home › Anime › Chainsaw Man and One Piece Both Nominated at the 2026 Harvey Awards
Anime 2026-10-01 2 min read Yu
Key Takeaway
Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc and One Piece – Season 2 are both nominated at the 2026 Harvey Awards in the Best Adaptation from Comic Book/Graphic Novel category. The two Japanese anime/manga franchises appear alongside many well-known names such as Demon Slayer, My Hero Academia and The Boys.

2026 continues to show the growing influence of anime and manga, with Chainsaw Man and One Piece both appearing on the nominee list for the 2026 Harvey Awards — one of the longest-running awards in the American comics industry.

Chainsaw Man and One Piece Compete for the Award

At the 2026 Harvey Awards, Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc and One Piece – Season 2 were both nominated in the Best Adaptation from Comic Book/Graphic Novel category.

This category honors works adapted from comics, graphic novels or manga into film, television and other forms of entertainment.

Competing alongside the two hit anime franchises are Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle, The Boys – Season 5, Invincible – Season 4, My Hero Academia – Final Season, Peacemaker – Season 2, Spider-Noir, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight and Your Letter.

Notably, Chainsaw Man is nominated for the Reze Arc film, while One Piece is recognized for Season 2 of Netflix’s live-action series.

Chainsaw Man Keeps Drawing Crowds in North America

Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc’s Harvey Awards nomination adds to the franchise’s run of success in international markets.

The film follows Denji and his encounter with Reze, combining Chainsaw Man’s signature fights with a turbulent love story.

In North America, the film opened at No. 1 with $17.3 million, beating several competitors at the box office at the time. It is one of the signs that the appeal of Japanese anime keeps growing in this market.

Beyond the big screen, Chainsaw Man also appeared at Anime Expo 2026 in Los Angeles, where the franchise continued to be showcased to North American anime fans.

The Harvey Awards Show Anime’s Growing International Presence

Founded in 1988, the Harvey Awards recognize many areas of the comics industry, including comics, graphic novels and manga. Nominees are selected and voted on by industry professionals.

Having Chainsaw Man, One Piece, Demon Slayer and My Hero Academia in the same adaptation category shows how closely Japanese manga and anime are becoming tied to the international entertainment market.

The 2026 Harvey Awards ceremony is scheduled for October 9, 2026 at New York Comic Con.
