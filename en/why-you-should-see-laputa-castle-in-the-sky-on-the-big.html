Why You Should See Laputa: Castle in the Sky on the Big Screen

Laputa: Castle in the Sky returns to cinemas in Vietnam on September 25, 2026. Here’s why this Studio Ghibli classic is worth rewatching on the big screen.

Home › Anime › Why You Should See Laputa: Castle in the Sky on the Big Screen
Anime 2026-09-29 2 min read Yu
Key Takeaway
Laputa: Castle in the Sky returns to the big screen in Vietnam, and its vast skies, hand-drawn detail and Joe Hisaishi’s score make it a film that truly shines in theaters. Nearly 40 years on, its story about power, technology and nature still offers plenty to think about.

Laputa: Castle in the Sky isn’t a new film, but its return to cinemas on September 25, 2026 gives audiences a chance to experience this Studio Ghibli classic in a different way. With its vast skyscapes, flying machines and Joe Hisaishi’s music, it’s a film especially well suited to the big-screen experience.

Laputa’s World Looks Even Better on the Big Screen

One of Laputa’s biggest highlights is the world Hayao Miyazaki built. From mining towns and airships to the giant castle in the sky, every frame is packed with detail.

The scenes of Pazu and Sheeta flying through the clouds or exploring Laputa convey a sense of a vast world that a small screen struggles to capture fully. The action sequences with airships, robots and chases also become more impressive on a big screen.

Music and Emotion Are Another Reason to Rewatch

Joe Hisaishi’s score is one of the elements behind Laputa’s lasting appeal. Its adventurous, gentle melodies mixed with climactic passages give the story extra emotional depth.

Nearly 40 years later, the film keeps its charm thanks to its blend of adventure, fantasy and childhood wonder. Seeing it again in theaters is also a chance for those who already know Laputa to revisit old memories, while new audiences can discover the world of Ghibli on the big screen for the first time.

An Old Film That Still Gives You Something to Think About

Behind Pazu and Sheeta’s adventure is a story about power, technology and nature. Laputa holds enormous power, but it is people who decide whether that power is used to protect or destroy.

That’s also why Laputa: Castle in the Sky isn’t just for children. Nearly 40 years on, the questions the film raises can still be seen in many different ways.

Laputa: Castle in the Sky returns to cinemas in Vietnam from September 25, 2026. If you want to fully experience the visuals, music and scale of Ghibli’s world, the big screen is well worth considering.
