One Piece Chapter 1193: Zoro Faces Sommers

One Piece chapter 1193 released September 13, 2026: Luffy is exhausted after Gear 5, Loki takes a hybrid form, and Zoro pierces Sommers with Conqueror’s Haki, saying he is still practicing.

Manga 2026-08-25 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
One Piece chapter 1193 released September 13, 2026: Luffy is exhausted after Gear 5, Loki takes a hybrid form, and Zoro pierces Sommers with Conqueror’s Haki, saying he is still practicing.

One Piece chapter 1193, titled “I’m Still Practicing,” released on Sunday, September 13, 2026 at 7 a.m. Pacific Time, available on MANGA Plus, Viz and Shonen Jump+. The cover features King with a tanuki.

Key events (spoilers)

  • Luffy exhausts himself using Gear 5 to break through Imu’s barrier with Gomu Gomu no Dawn Thor Bullet, then collapses
  • Loki transforms into a hybrid form as the fight against Imu continues
  • Gunko awakens; Biblo prepares to show something special to Robin, Chopper and Saul
  • Sanji’s and Zoro’s fights get brief coverage

Zoro vs. Sommers

The chapter’s climax is Zoro’s clash with Holy Knight Sommers. Surrounded by thorns, Zoro suddenly pierces Sommers’ chest, nearly fatally, leaving the knight terrified; he simply apologizes that he is “still practicing.” The counterattack shows Zoro has mastered infusing his sword with Conqueror’s Haki.

After chapter 1193, Weekly Shonen Jump takes its Silver Week break; chapter 1194 releases September 27, 2026. Articles claiming this chapter centers on “Loki’s true identity” or an “Ancient Weapon Uranus” have no basis.

Sources: Dexerto, GameRant, FandomWire. Contains chapter spoilers.
