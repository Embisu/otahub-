Naruto Confirms a New Anime After Three Quiet Years

Viz Media has officially confirmed a brand-new “limited” Naruto TV anime after three quiet years, with full details to be revealed at NYCC 2026 on October 10.

Home › Anime › Naruto Confirms a New Anime After Three Quiet Years
Anime 2026-09-30 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Viz Media has confirmed that Naruto is getting a brand-new “limited” TV anime, ending three years of silence for the franchise on television. Full details — story, episode count and premiere date — will be revealed at the “NARUTO: What's Next?” panel at New York Comic Con on October 10, 2026, featuring original voice actors Junko Takeuchi and Noriaki Sugiyama. The project is believed to be tied to the Naruto anime’s 25th anniversary in 2027.

After exactly three years with almost nothing significant happening on the TV anime front, the Naruto franchise is suddenly back in the headlines. On September 29, 2026, Viz Media, Naruto’s official publisher in North America, announced that a brand-new TV anime is in the works, opening with a teaser image of Naruto in his familiar Shippuden-era outfit. The news spread quickly on social media, with CBR’s Instagram post alone racking up more than 5,300 likes in less than a day.

Notably, Viz Media deliberately revealed nothing beyond calling it a “limited series,” which usually implies a fixed and much shorter episode count than long-running seasons like the original Naruto or Shippuden. Everything else was kept under wraps for a bigger event exactly two weeks later.

The NYCC 2026 Reveal: When, Where to Watch and Who Will Appear?

All specific details will be revealed at a panel titled “NARUTO: What's Next?” on October 10, 2026 on the Empire Stage at the Javits Center in New York, as part of New York Comic Con. The one-hour panel starts at 5:00 PM ET (2:00 PM PT), which is around 4:00 AM on October 11 in Vietnam. For fans who can’t attend in person, the organizers confirmed the entire panel will be livestreamed for free on YouTube for a global audience, so fans anywhere can watch live without an NYCC ticket.

On stage will be two faces tied to the franchise for more than two decades: voice actors Junko Takeuchi (Naruto Uzumaki) and Noriaki Sugiyama (Sasuke Uchiha), along with executive producer Yoshihiro Tominaga of Pierrot Films, a subsidiary of Studio Pierrot — the studio behind all of Naruto, Naruto Shippuden and Boruto. The presence of the two original voice actors, rather than just production representatives, is usually a sign that the project directly involves the original cast of characters rather than being an entirely new spin-off.

Why “Three Quiet Years”? The Link to the Project Postponed in 2023

The “three years” figure isn’t random. According to CBR’s analysis, this limited anime is most likely the revival of a four-part anniversary anime project that was planned for 2023 and then postponed indefinitely without any official explanation from the producers. If so, this would be the first official confirmation that the project has been revived after nearly three years of silence, just as CBR described in the headline of its original article.

The timing isn’t a coincidence either: the original Naruto anime premiered in Japan on October 3, 2002, meaning 2027 marks the 25th anniversary of the anime’s debut. A shelved project suddenly being revived right as preparations begin for this quarter-century milestone makes complete sense from a media timing standpoint, and it also explains why the teaser image features Naruto in his Shippuden look rather than the adult Naruto of his Hokage era.

Not Just Anime: Games, Films and Even a Hollywood Project

Naruto: What's Next? turns out to be just one piece of a much bigger picture that TV Tokyo hinted at back in June 2026, when the network confirmed that several new Naruto projects were being prepared in parallel for the 2027 anniversary, including games, merchandise and other animated productions, not just a TV anime.

  • A new animated film: Before the TV anime was confirmed, a report from Variety revealed that a new Naruto animated film would have its global release date announced at NYCC 2026, meaning audiences could see both major announcements at the same event.
  • Naruto Card Game: A new physical collectible card game, planned for release in 2027, will also be introduced at the NYCC panel, expanding the franchise into the booming board game/TCG space alongside rivals like the One Piece Card Game.
  • A Hollywood live-action adaptation: Completely separate from the anime projects, Lionsgate is still developing a live-action Naruto film. According to Anime News Network, actor Charles Melton (known for his role in “May December”) is reportedly in final negotiations to play Kakashi Hatake.

Taken together, these pieces show that 2027 isn’t just a token anniversary but a carefully prepared multi-platform campaign spanning anime, animated film, a card game and even a Hollywood live-action movie — a scale rarely seen for a shonen franchise that debuted back in 1999.

OtaHub’s Take

The most interesting thing about this announcement isn’t the content, which is still unknown, but how Viz Media and TV Tokyo chose their timing. Keeping every specific detail under wraps and saving it all for a live panel with a global livestream is a typical tactic for projects expected to generate major media buzz, quite different from how a mobile game or merchandise announcement is usually dropped quietly via press release.

But we should be frank: much of the reaction from the international fan community right under the original post wasn’t pure excitement. Many comments expressed skepticism or even fatigue, partly because Boruto: Naruto Next Generations never received the same consistent reception as the original, and partly because of the “announce, then go silent” pattern left by the 20th-anniversary project back in 2023. Some fans even bluntly suggested “remaking the original” instead of continuing to expand through spin-offs.

That’s why the approach this time — putting the original voice actors of Naruto and Sasuke on stage instead of just issuing a press release — may be a calculated signal to reassure exactly those skeptical fans: this is a project tied to the original memories, not an unfamiliar derivative product. Even so, until the screen lights up at the Javits Center on October 10, every bit of speculation — even the best-informed speculation from industry press — remains just that. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the new Naruto anime a TV series or a movie?

It is a TV anime that Viz Media describes as a “limited series,” meaning it won’t run as long as the original Naruto or Shippuden. The exact episode count hasn’t been announced. Separately, a new Naruto animated film is also expected to have its global release date revealed at the same NYCC 2026 event.

When will details about the new anime be announced?

Full details will be revealed at the “NARUTO: What's Next?” panel on October 10, 2026, from 5:00 to 6:00 PM ET (around 4:00 to 5:00 AM on October 11 in Vietnam) at New York Comic Con, livestreamed for free on YouTube for a global audience.

Who will appear at the NYCC 2026 reveal?

Original voice actors Junko Takeuchi (Naruto) and Noriaki Sugiyama (Sasuke), along with executive producer Yoshihiro Tominaga of Pierrot Films, will appear on stage in person.

Is this a sequel to Boruto?

There’s no official confirmation yet. Industry observers, including CBR, believe it is most likely the four-part anniversary anime project originally slated for 2023 and then postponed indefinitely, now revived to coincide with the Naruto anime’s 25th anniversary in 2027 — making it a project tied to the original Naruto rather than a direct continuation of Boruto.

Besides the anime, what other Naruto projects are planned for 2027?

TV Tokyo confirmed in June 2026 that several new Naruto projects are being prepared for the 2027 anniversary, including games, merchandise and other animated productions. At NYCC 2026, a new Naruto Card Game (planned for release in 2027) will also be introduced, alongside a live-action film adaptation from Lionsgate that is currently in development.

This article compiles and analyzes the official announcement from Viz Media, along with reporting from CBR, Anime News Network and Siliconera, rewritten from OtaHub’s own perspective. Information current as of September 30, 2026.
