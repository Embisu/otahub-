Crunchyroll Adds Romelia War Chronicle to 2026 Slate

Romelia War Chronicle is confirmed to stream in 2026, while the summer lineup expands with BLACK TORCH, Link Click Season 3 and other new series.

Home › Anime › New Crunchyroll Anime for 2026: Romelia, BLACK TORCH
Anime 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Romelia War Chronicle is confirmed to stream in 2026, while the summer lineup expands with BLACK TORCH, Link Click Season 3 and other new series.

Crunchyroll has confirmed it will stream Romelia War Chronicle in 2026 in North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East and the CIS. This is a licensing announcement for Romelia alone; it does not mean every anime mentioned in the 2026 lineup is available in the same region.

What is Romelia War Chronicle about?

Romelia helped her party of heroes defeat the Demon King but was cast out after the victory. She does not receive overwhelming combat power; her advantage lies in reading people, organizing and mobilizing resources. The story is therefore closer to a chronicle of building a force than to a motif of instant revenge.

The anime is directed by Hiroshi Shirai at studio Atra. Aya Satsuki handles series composition; Yoshiko Saito and Yuta Otaka design the characters; KOHTA YAMAMOTO composes the music. These credits are worth noting because a story with several fronts needs a script that keeps Romelia's goals, factions and progress clear.

Which anime are in the lineup being discussed?

  • Romelia War Chronicle: its 2026 streaming license has been announced by Crunchyroll.
  • BLACK TORCH: a supernatural action anime based on Tsuyoshi Takaki's manga.
  • Link Click Season 3: the next part of the Chinese animated series about the ability to enter photographs.
  • ONE PIECE HEROINES: a project focused on the female characters of One Piece.
  • Jaadugar: A Witch in Mongolia: a historical work set in the Mongol Empire.

The list above helps place the lineup, but readers should not infer identical release dates. Each project has its own schedule, territories and series page.

Does Crunchyroll stream in Vietnam?

The Romelia announcement lists broad regions but does not name Vietnam specifically. Distribution rights can change by IP, and sometimes a series page appears differently depending on the account or location. The most reliable check is to open the series page once the broadcast schedule is announced and see whether the episodes appear in the user's region.

What is still unconfirmed?

The platform announcement does not by itself confirm air dates and times, episode count, a Vietnamese dub or Vietnamese subtitles. An earlier version of this article grouped Romelia with the "summer lineup," which could lead readers to think everything airs in the same season; the content has been separated by level of confirmation.

OtaHub's take: Romelia stands out because its heroine deals with failure through leadership rather than a new gift of power. If the anime clearly portrays logistics and strategic decisions, that could be what separates a political fantasy from an ordinary isekai.

Sources: Crunchyroll: Romelia War Chronicle and the Summer 2026 lineup. Updated September 30, 2026.
