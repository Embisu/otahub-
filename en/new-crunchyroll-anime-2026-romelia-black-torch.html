Crunchyroll adds Romelia War Chronicle and expands its 2026 anime slate

Romelia War Chronicle is confirmed to stream in 2026, while the summer lineup expands with BLACK TORCH, Link Click Season 3 and other new series.

Home › Anime › New Crunchyroll anime for 2026: Romelia War Chronicle, BLACK TORCH and more
Anime 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Romelia War Chronicle is confirmed to stream in 2026, while the summer lineup expands with BLACK TORCH, Link Click Season 3 and other new series.

Why Romelia War Chronicle stands out

Crunchyroll confirms streaming in 2026 across the Americas, Europe, Africa, Oceania, the Middle East and CIS. The story follows Romelia after she is cast out of the hero's party despite helping defeat the Demon King. Hiroshi Shirai directs at Atra, Aya Satsuki handles series composition, and KOHTA YAMAMOTO composes the music.

The expanded summer slate

Crunchyroll's updated lineup adds BLACK TORCH, Jaadugar: A Witch in Mongolia, Link Click Season 3, ONE PIECE HEROINES, Grand Blue Dreaming Season 3 and The 100 Girlfriends Season 3. Availability can differ by territory.

What remains unconfirmed

A platform announcement is not a Vietnam-specific premiere date. OtaHub will add precise scheduling only after Crunchyroll or an anime's official site confirms it.

Sources: Crunchyroll on Romelia War Chronicle and its Summer 2026 lineup.
