One Piece: Final Saga Review: 25 Years Closer to Laugh Tale

Eiichiro Oda revealed the Laugh Tale map after 25 years of waiting, bringing Final Saga into the most intense and emotional period in series history.

OtaHub › Reviews › One Piece: Final Saga
Reviews 2026-08-15 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
After 25 years posted, One Piece officially entered Final Saga, the final journey of Luffy and the Straw Hats. The latest chapter reveals the island map Laugh Tale, the biggest mystery throughout the entire series, brings the adventure closer than ever to the conclusion that millions of readers have been waiting for for more than two decades.

Few works in the history of comics have such stature One Piece. From a manga that started in 1997 about a rubber boy's dream of becoming Pirate King, the series has grown into a global cultural phenomenon, selling more than 500 million copies in print worldwide. The official entry Final Saga marks a milestone that many readers thought would never come.

Laugh Tale map: the final piece of the puzzle

The biggest focus of this phase is the reveal of the map leading up to it Laugh Tale, the final island of the Grand Line, where the legendary One Piece treasure is hidden, and also the only place Pirate King Gol D. Roger ever set foot before his execution. This mystery has been sown since the first chapters of the series, and the way Oda handles this reveal-unhurried, full of emotional weight-shows masterful control over the narrative pace after a quarter of a century.

The pressure of closing the longest-running series in Jump history

Unlike many other lengthy shonen series that have been criticized for rushed endings or excessive length, One Piece's Final Saga shows that Oda meticulously prepared for this phase beforehand. Clues embedded throughout hundreds of chapters now begin to connect systematically, creating a sense of purpose rather than improvisation to fill the time.

Emotion: 25 years of companionship converge

For readers who have followed from the beginning, the emotional weight of the Final Saga comes not only from the plot but also from the clear awareness that a journey spanning a quarter of a century is nearing its end. Oda handles this by allowing characters, both main and supporting, to have meaningful moments of reflection, without slowing down the necessary fast pace of a conclusion.

  • Posting time: more than 25 years (since 1997)
  • Current stage: Final Saga
  • Key events: Laugh Tale map revealed
  • Author: Eiichiro Oda (Shueisha, Weekly Shounen Jump)
  • Sales: more than 500 million copies in print globally

With the Laugh Tale map revealed, Eiichiro Oda is concluding 25 years of adventure with the same patience and meticulousness that made his name, making this one of the most watchable periods in shonen manga history.
