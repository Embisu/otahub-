Kagurabachi Hits 5 Million Copies in Circulation as Weekly Shonen Jump Confirms TV Anime Adaptation

Takeru Hokazono's Kagurabachi officially crosses 5 million copies in circulation; Shueisha confirms a TV anime adaptation is currently in production.

Home›Manga›Kagurabachi Hits 5 Million Copies in Circulation as Weekly Shonen Jump Confirms TV Anime Adaptation
Manga Aug 24, 2026 7 min read OtaHub Editorial
Milestone & Anime Announcement
Weekly Shonen Jump has officially announced that Kagurabachi by Takeru Hokazono has surpassed 5,000,000 copies in circulation worldwide. Simultaneously, Shueisha confirmed that a major TV Anime adaptation is in active production with a targeted 2027 premiere.

Proving itself as one of the defining shonen manga of the modern era, Takeru Hokazono's dark samurai epic Kagurabachi continues to shatter publishing benchmarks.

1. From Global Phenomenon to 5 Million Copies

With cinematic paneling and brutal choreography, Volume 7 propelled the series past the 5-million mark in record time.

2. Anime Greenlit for 2027

Shueisha has officially entered pre-production with a top-tier animation studio to bring Chihiro's enchanted blade Enten to life.
