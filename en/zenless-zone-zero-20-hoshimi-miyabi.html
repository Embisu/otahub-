Zenless Zone Zero Version 2.0 "Lunar Holiday": HoYoverse Unveils S-Rank Hoshimi Miyabi & Hollow Station Tsukimi

HoYoverse announces Zenless Zone Zero Version 2.0, expanding beyond New Eridu into zero-gravity Hollow Station with Section 6 Chief Hoshimi Miyabi.

Gaming Aug 24, 2026 7 min read OtaHub Editorial
Version 2.0 Highlights
HoYoverse has hosted its Special Program for Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ) 2.0: Lunar Holiday. The landmark update expands the storyline into orbital Hollow Station Tsukimi, introduces S-Rank Ice Slash Agent Hoshimi Miyabi (Section 6 Chief), and overhauls Hollow Zero mechanics.

Entering a bold new chapter, HoYoverse's urban action RPG Zenless Zone Zero steps into Version 2.0 with groundbreaking narrative expansions and combat upgrades.

1. New Setting: Hollow Station Tsukimi

Experience zero-gravity environmental traversal and revamped TV monitor exploration aboard an orbital research platform.

2. S-Rank Agent: Hoshimi Miyabi

The highly anticipated Section 6 leader brings lethal Ice Slash combos, Frost Shards mechanic, and high-damage vacuum slice ultimate.
