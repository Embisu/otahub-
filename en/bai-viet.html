Elden Ring DLC 2: Shadow of the Erdtree II
FromSoftware confirms release Q4 2026

The biggest surprise of Summer Game Fest 2026: FromSoftware officially confirms the second DLC for Elden Ring. New lands beyond the Lands Between, never-before-seen gameplay mechanics, and a boss called "the hardest enemy in FromSoftware history."

June 1, 2026
8 minutes read

Share Facebook Copy link

On May 31, 2026, during the Summer Game Fest event held in Los Angeles, FromSoftware and Bandai Namco suddenly released a trailer for Elden Ring's second DLC named Shadow of the Erdtree II: The Waning Moon. The 4-minute trailer has no gameplay, only cinematics · but it's enough for the global gaming community to erupt in endless discussion.

Quick information

New Land: The Waning Moon Expanse

According to information from the trailer, the DLC will take players to a land completely separate from the Lands Between · The Waning Moon Expanse. This land is described as "where the moonlight never fades, but is never full. From what's seen in the trailer, the environment appears to be characterized by silvery, moonlit fields, massive abandoned castles, and creatures never seen in the original game.

In particular, the end of the trailer shows a character wearing a white robe with a giant rune engraved on his back · many people believe that this is Melina in a new form, although FromSoftware has not confirmed any information about the character.

"We want players to once again experience the feeling of exploring something completely unfamiliar, like setting foot in Limgrave for the first time." Hidetaka Miyazaki, in an interview with IGN Japan

New gameplay mechanics

FromSoftware confirmed that the DLC will bring some completely new gameplay mechanics, not expansions of existing systems. Here's what was officially revealed:

Moonlight Resonance System

The new system allows players to "resonate" with the moonlight in the new land, creating special effects depending on the phase of the moon at that time in the game. Boss and enemies will change their behavior depending on this state.

Weapon Memory

New weapons in the DLC will have a "memory" system · they become stronger when used to defeat the same enemy multiple times. This is a completely new weapon evolution mechanism, not replacing the old upgrade system.
