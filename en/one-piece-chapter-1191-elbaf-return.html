One Piece Returns With Chapter 1191, Luffy Faces Imu Again in the Elbaf Arc

After Weekly Shonen Jump's Obon break, One Piece returns on August 23 with chapter 1191, continuing the Luffy vs. Imu showdown.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-20 · 5 min read

In short: One Piece officially returns with chapter 1191 on August 23, 2026 after Weekly Shonen Jump's Obon break, continuing the confrontation between Luffy and Imu in the Elbaf arc.

After a break caused by the Obon holiday, which paused Weekly Shonen Jump, Eiichiro Oda's One Piece manga will officially return with chapter 1191 on August 23, 2026, running in issue 39 and available on Manga Plus at 11 a.m. Eastern Time.

The new chapter continues the Elbaf arc, with Monkey D. Luffy resuming his fight against Nerona Imu, the King of the World, right after Scopper Gaban's major sacrifice in the previous chapter. Just as Imu is about to kill them both, Hajrudding arrives and punches Imu away, though he too is affected by the demonic aura radiating from Imu. Luffy recovers after eating some food, and the chapter closes with a joint attack from Luffy, Loki, and Hajrudding called the "Sovereignty of the Three Generals."

Notably, chapters 1191 and 1192 (releasing August 30) will run back-to-back with no Oda break between them — unusual right after such a long hiatus. Oda will head into his next break once chapter 1192 is out. Chapter 1190 previously released on August 9, 2026.
