Monster Hunter Wilds Open Beta PC & PS5: System Requirements, Cross-Play & Hunter Rewards

Capcom opens public registration for the Monster Hunter Wilds Cross-Play Open Beta across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S with full DLSS 3 support.

Home › Gaming › Monster Hunter Wilds Open Beta: System Requirements
Gaming · Action RPG · News 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Capcom opens public registration for the Monster Hunter Wilds Cross-Play Open Beta across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S with full DLSS 3 support.
The Hunt Goes Next-Gen
Capcom launches the Monster Hunter Wilds Open Beta Demo across PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X/S with full cross-play.

Capcom has officially rolled out the comprehensive Open Beta Demo for the highly anticipated hunting action epic Monster Hunter Wilds across PC (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S, featuring seamless cross-platform multiplayer (Full Cross-Play).

1. Comprehensive PC System Requirements & Hardware Tiers

Powered by the cutting-edge RE Engine, Monster Hunter Wilds renders dynamic weather catastrophes, dense herds, and expansive ecosystems. Capcom has detailed the necessary hardware tiers for stable framerates:

  • Minimum Specs (1080p 30 FPS / Low Settings): Intel Core i5-10600K / AMD Ryzen 5 3600, Nvidia GeForce GTX 1660 Super / AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT, 16GB RAM, 140GB SSD.
  • Recommended Specs (1080p 60 FPS / Medium Settings): Intel Core i7-11700 / AMD Ryzen 7 5700X, Nvidia GeForce RTX 3060 Ti / AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT, 16GB RAM, SSD required.
  • Ultra 4K Tier (4K 60+ FPS / High Settings with Ray Tracing): Intel Core i7-14700K / AMD Ryzen 7 7800X3D, Nvidia GeForce RTX 4080 Super / AMD Radeon RX 7900 XTX, 32GB RAM.

2. Focus Mode Mechanics & The Agile Seikret Mount

The demo introduces hunters to the innovative Focus Mode, enabling precise directional aiming to target wounded monster parts, crack tough hides, and sever tails with surgical precision. Additionally, the agile Seikret mount features autonomous navigation, permitting players to sharpen weapons, consume potions, and swap secondary weapon loadouts while on the run.

Game Title:Monster Hunter Wilds
Developer & Publisher:Capcom
Engine:RE Engine 2.0
Features:Full Cross-Play, Focus Mode, Seikret Mount, DLSS 3 / FSR 3 Support
