Frieren Season 2: Beyond Journey's End – Madhouse Unveils New Trailer & Aureole Journey

Madhouse and TOHO Animation unveil a brand-new trailer for Frieren: Beyond Journey's End Season 2, spotlighting the Northern Plateau expedition and the quest for Aureole.

Key Highlights
Madhouse and TOHO Animation have officially released a brand-new trailer for Frieren: Beyond Journey's End (Sousou no Frieren) Season 2. The upcoming season will adapt the Northern Plateau Arc and the mystical journey to the soul-resting realm of Aureole, featuring the return of composer Evan Call.

Following the monumental global acclaim of its inaugural season, animation powerhouse Madhouse and distributor TOHO Animation have unveiled the latest promotional trailer for Frieren: Beyond Journey's End Season 2, promising elevated emotional depth and expanded fantasy warfare.

1. Venturing Deep Into the Northern Plateau

Picking up directly from the aftermath of the First-Class Mage Exam, the party comprising Frieren, Fern, and Stark steps into the untamed terrain of the Northern Plateau. This hazardous zone remains rife with the most dangerous demonic forces left behind since the Demon King's demise.

The trailer showcases signature breathtaking vistas: sun-drenched hilltops, blizzard-swept ancient pines, and forgotten magical ruins that conceal centuries of lost arcane knowledge.

2. The Mysteries of Aureole & the Seven Sages of Destruction

At the heart of Season 2 lies Frieren's ultimate destination: Aureole, the resting place of souls located at the northernmost tip of the continent, where she hopes to speak with the hero Himmel once more.

  • High-Stakes Magic Clashes: Intense skirmishes against remaining Greater Demons and remnants of the Seven Sages of Destruction.
  • Growth of Fern and Stark: Fern demonstrating her prowess as a First-Class Mage with rapid-fire Zoltraak strikes, alongside Stark's mountain-shattering axe combat.
  • Cherished Flashbacks: Poignant memories of Himmel, Heiter, and Eisen illuminating Frieren's emotional evolution throughout the quest.

3. Madhouse Production & Evan Call's Orchestral Score

Madhouse's core creative staff returns to ensure the pristine hand-drawn 2D animation and evocative cinematic lighting that defined the first season remain uncompromised.

Acclaimed composer Evan Call (Violet Evergarden, Frieren Season 1) confirms an all-new original symphonic soundtrack blending Celtic folk acoustics with sweeping European orchestral arrangements.
