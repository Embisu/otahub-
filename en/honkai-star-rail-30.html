Honkai: Star Rail 3.0, Amphoreus and the New Era

Amphoreus, planet of ancient gods and heroes, opens the most epic new chapter in Honkai history: the Star Rail.

OtaHub›Gaming›Honkai: Star Rail 3.0, Amphoreus and the New Era
Gaming2026-06-238 minutes readOtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
HSR 3.0 launched in January 2025 to the world of Amphoreus. New characters: Castorice, Hyacine, Tribbie. Epic storyline of Trailblaze Mission: The Eternal Holy War. Metacritic: 91.

Honkai: Star Rail has proven itself to be the best gacha RPG on the market since its launch in 2023. Update 3.0 Amphoreus: Crux of the Eons More than just a numerical milestone, this is the biggest shift in the game's narrative and worldbuilding, and it surpasses all expectations.

Amphoreus, World of Gods and Heroes

Before arriving at Amphoreus, Trailblazers had explored many planets with diverse styles, but none quite like this one. Amphoreus is directly inspired by Greek mythology, with Titans, Heroes, and Gods operating within an epic universe. This world is not just a backdrop but the center of conflict: an eternal war between divine forces is coming to an end, and Trailblazers are inadvertently drawn into it.

Castorice, the Most Anticipated 5-Star Character of 2025

Castorice, the girl connected to Thanatos (the Greek god of death), was Amphoreus' first 5-star unit and immediately became one of the most beloved characters in game history. This wasn't just because of her incredibly powerful combat kit (Nihility/Imaginary with a unique death synergy), but also because of her heartbreaking backstory and excellent character writing. Castorice is proof that HoYoverse is seriously investing in storytelling.

Gameplay Improvements: Memory Turbulence And Pure Fiction

Along with the new storyline, patch 3.0 also reshuffles the endgame system. Apocalyptic Shadow was added as a third combat mode alongside Memory of Chaos and Pure Fiction, targeting players who prefer pure turn-based strategy rather than speed run. This is HoYoverse's direct response to community feedback, and it shows they listen to their players.

Comparison with Genshin: Is Star Rail Overtaking?

A frequently asked question in the gacha community is: Is Honkai: Star Rail currently better than Genshin Impact? In terms of narrative depth and character writing, the answer increasingly leans towards Star Rail. Amphoreus, in particular, has a storyline with a scope and ambition that Genshin Impact in its equivalent patch (3.x) hasn't achieved. Of course, Genshin wins in terms of open world and combat feel, but in terms of storytelling, Star Rail 3.0 is a new pinnacle.

Score: 9.2/10, Best Gacha RPG Currently

Honkai: Star Rail 3.0 Amphoreus is undeniable proof that gacha games have evolved to the point where they can compete with traditional AAA RPGs in terms of narrative quality. 9.2/10 is not "good for gacha", this is good by any standards.
