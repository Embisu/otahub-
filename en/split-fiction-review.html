Split Fiction Review: Hazelight Redefine Co-op Gaming Again

Hazelight Studios surpasses It Takes Two with a co-op game with unprecedented gameplay diversity, reaching Metascore 91, the highest rated game of 2025. OtaHub comprehensive review.

OtaHub › Reviews › Split Fiction
Reviews 2026-08-15 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Split Fiction is proof that Hazelight Studios has not stopped after the success of It Takes Two. With a Metascore of 91 and over 26 perfect score reviews, this is the studio's most critically acclaimed co-op game ever, and the #1 Best-Reviewed game of 2025.

Josef Fares and the Hazelight Studios team, creators of A Way Out and It Takes Two (Game of the Year 2021, 23 million copies sold), return with Split Fiction, a co-op adventure that takes Mio and Zoe, two young writers, through the world of science fiction and fantasy to reclaim stolen creative ideas.

Gameplay: incredibly diverse

What makes Split Fiction so popular is the dense concentration of gameplay ideas throughout the experience. Players will encounter dozens of completely different mechanics: from escaping a supernova-like exploding sun, challenging monkeys to jump, skateboarding, battling monstrous cats, to driving gravity cars and riding sand sharks. Each level is almost a separate game crammed into one title, avoiding repetition of old formulas.

Side Stories: a surprising layer of side content

Beyond the main storyline, Split Fiction also incorporates \"Side Stories,\" mysterious portals that lead Mio and Zoe into unfinished story drafts, offering short but creative gameplay twists: climbing giant mountains, surfing sand dunes across the desert on the back of a shark, or waterskiing behind a flying train. This is optional content, but almost no one wants to miss it.

"Split Fiction has some of the craziest stuff you've ever seen in a co-op game. The gameplay variety is unbelievable and I'm incredibly proud of the level of polish we've achieved."Josef Fares, Creative Director, Hazelight Studios

Friend's Pass and cross-play across the platform

In terms of accessibility, Split Fiction continues Hazelight's Friend's Pass tradition, allowing a game buyer to invite friends to play along for free with all the content. Notably, this is the first time the studio has fully supported cross-play between PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, making inviting friends to play together easier than ever.
