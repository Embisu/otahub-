Black Myth: Wukong, A Beginner's Guide From A to Z

Black Myth: Wukong is more complicated than it looks, this is a comprehensive guide for you to conquer every boss from chapter 1 to chapter 6.

Game Science's action RPG, inspired by Journey to the West. Three poses: Smash / Pillar / Thrust. Craft Vessel to unlock special skills. Farmable Sparks from reborn boss.

Black Myth: Wukong by Game Science is an action RPG inspired by Journey to the West, released in August 2024 and quickly becoming one of the best-selling PC Game in history. However, many new players are overwhelmed by the complex combat system and diverse bosses. This guide will help you master each important mechanism.

Three Fighting Stances, the Foundation of All Battles

Unlike many action RPG games, Black Myth: Wukong has three completely different stances, each suitable for a different playing style:

Smash Stance: Default stance, fast and flexible combo. Ideal for beginners because it is easy to learn but difficult to master. Smash's Charged attack can interrupt many bosses mid-combo.

Pillar Stance: Turns Iron Table into a long column, attacks from afar and has a large AoE. Extremely strong against small groups of enemies and bosses that keep their distance. Combine with Spirit Pillar Stance to cause huge damage.

Thrust Stance: Fast attack in a straight line, highest damage on single hit. Suitable for players who like speed and aggressive play.

Vessel, The Mysterious Power From the Treasured Bottle

Vessels are the most distinctive system in Black Myth: Wukong. By collecting materials from bosses and crafting at the Shrine, you unlock Vessels that provide powerful buffs when activated. Each Vessel has its own unique characteristics: Loong Vessel increases critical rate and Spark damage, Azure Dust Vessel allows you to absorb health from downed enemies, while Supreme Vessel is the strongest Vessel and can only be obtained after completing the True Ending.

Spirit, Turns Enemies into Power

After defeating certain bosses and minibosses, you can collect their spirits and use them as special skills. There are over 30 different spirits, and choosing the right one for each situation is crucial: Red Loong Spirit for burst damage, Wandering Wight Spirit for tankiness, and Whiteclad Noble Spirit for healing.

Tips to Overcome Difficult Bosses

Some community-tested tips: Always keep at least 2 Gourd charges for boss fights. Learn the boss's animation before attacking; most bosses have a clear punish window after a 3-4 hit combo. Use the Immobilize spell when the boss is low on health to deal extra damage. Don't forget to upgrade your Gourd at the Shrine to increase the healing amount per drink.

Build Recommendations for Newcomers

If you're just starting out, invest in the skill tree Smash Stance to unlock Focus Points fastest. Priority: upgrade Gourd → unlock Immobilize → get Critical Strike from Foundation tree → then depending on preference. Don't spread everything evenly, investing deeply in one position will be much more effective in the early stages of the game.
