Berserk Final Arc: Studio Gaga Completes the Legacy

Kentaro Miura died in May 2021 after 33 years with Berserk, but he left enough notes for his students at Studio Gaga to carry the final arc forward.

Home › Manga › Berserk Final Arc: Studio Gaga Completes the Legacy
Manga 2026-06-16 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Berserk final arc: Casca restores memory, Fantasia Arc, Skellig Island, Elfhelm. Studio Gaga (Miura's student) continued based on his notes. The manga is still releasing in 2025.

When Kentaro Miura passed away in May 2021 after 33 years with Berserk, the manga world witnessed an irreparable loss. But Miura left enough notes and plot details for his students to follow Studio Gaga can continue, and what they are doing is proving to be the truest way to honor their heritage.

Casca Recovery, The Moment 30 Years Waited For

After Eclipse, Casca loses her memory and becomes a child in an adult body. For nearly 30 years since the manga's publication, readers have awaited the moment of her recovery. When that finally happens on Elfhelm Island, the community's reaction is one of the most emotional moments in manga history. But that recovery doesn't mean happiness; Casca's memories include Eclipse, and she must confront the question: can she forgive Guts?

Fantasia Arc, Berserk World Changed Forever

The Fantasia Arc marks the biggest transformation in the world of Berserk: the boundary between the human world and the spirit world is erased, and monsters, spirits, and mystical entities freely appear in reality. Griffith, with the title of Femto and the world's new god of war, has made this a reality as part of his plan for domination. This is no longer just the story of Guts; it is the story of the fate of all humanity.

Studio Gaga And Its Commitment To The Miura Legacy

The most notable thing about the Berserk chapters after Miura's death is that they do not feel like a substitute. Studio Gaga, who have worked alongside Miura for many years, understands the visual language, narrative rhythm, and philosophy behind each page. They use Miura's notes and rough sketches as a compass, and the result is chapters that can stand alongside the original content.

What Will The End Be Like?

Based on what Miura has shared in interviews, he wants Berserk to end in "light" rather than complete darkness. This suggests that Guts will have the opportunity to face Griffith in a final battle, and the outcome, whatever it may be, will not simply be a winner and a loser. Berserk has never been that simple.
