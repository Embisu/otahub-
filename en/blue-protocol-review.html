Blue Protocol Review: Beautiful Anime MMORPG, Core Issues

Can Blue Protocol, an action MMORPG with the most beautiful anime graphics ever, overcome the problems of content and monetization to become a game worth playing?

Home › Reviews › Blue Protocol Review: Beautiful Anime MMORPG, Core Issues
Reviews 2026-06-17 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Blue Protocol (Bandai Namco) releases PC Global 2024. F2P with battle pass and gacha cosmetics. 5 classes: Eager Wing, Twin Striker, Keen Strider, Spell Caster, Heavy Smasher. Metacritic: 71.

Blue Protocol has been awaited for years as the "ultimate anime MMORPG," a game with truly anime-like graphics, smooth action gameplay, and a vast world. After 6 months, this is the most honest review of what the game actually delivers compared to what it promised.

Visual: Unbelievably Excellent

No exaggeration: Blue Protocol looks so beautiful that many people thought the trailers were actually anime. Bandai Namco's custom cel-shading engine creates a visual style unmistakable to any other game; the lighting, shadows, and particle effects are all optimized to look like hand-drawn animation. The character creator is also one of the best in any MMORPG.

Combat: Smooth But Lacks Depth

Blue Protocol's combat action system is responsive and satisfying at a basic level, with 5 classes each having a distinct playstyle, and early-stage bosses that are fairly well-designed with attack patterns to learn. The problem is that after about 30 hours, combat starts to feel repetitive. There isn't enough depth to make hardcore players want to optimize, which is the game's most fundamental weakness.

Content and Endgame: The Biggest Problem

After 6 months, Blue Protocol's most obvious problem is its lack of content. Story quests end too soon, the endgame loop is thin and repetitive, and seasonal events aren't fresh enough to keep players coming back. Compared to Final Fantasy XIV or Guild Wars 2, MMOs that truly offer hundreds of hours of rich content, Blue Protocol feels like a game catching up rather than leading the way.

Monetization: F2P But...

Blue Protocol is F2P (free-to-play) with real currency primarily used for cosmetics (character outfits and accessories) and battle passes. Gacha only provides cosmetic items; there is no pay-to-win gameplay. This is good in principle, but the prices of cosmetic items are quite high and battle pass rewards are unconvincing. F2P players can still enjoy the game, but will miss out on a lot of visual content.

Score: 7.0/10, Untapped Potential

Blue Protocol is a game worth trying, free, beautiful and has a good foundation. But if you're looking for an MMORPG to invest hundreds of hours into, it's not the right answer right now. 7.0/10 is a fair score for a game with great visuals but the content has not kept up with expectations.
