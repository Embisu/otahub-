Blue Protocol: Star Resonance, Global Overview (Unrated)

Blue Protocol: Star Resonance review: a free action MMORPG launched October 9, 2025 with about 95,000 concurrent Steam players but Mixed reviews over servers and monetization; pretty but time-hungry.

Home › Reviews › Blue Protocol: Star Resonance, Global Overview (Unrated)
Reviews 2026-09-24 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Blue Protocol (Bandai Namco) releases PC Global 2024. F2P with battle pass and gacha cosmetics. 5 classes: Eager Wing, Twin Striker, Keen Strider, Spell Caster, Heavy Smasher. Metacritic: 71.

Blue Protocol: Star Resonance is an anime-styled action MMORPG that draws players from day one with its visuals and music. But after a few dozen hours, many players feel it demands too much time while monetization is heavy, leaving its Steam reviews Mixed.

The game launched globally on October 9, 2025 on PC (Steam, Epic Games Store), iOS and Android.

Story

The game centers on an anime-styled open world in which players explore the map, join side activities and tackle content with teammates in traditional roles. Public information about the detailed plot is limited, so this section focuses on the world and systems.

It is a game from developer Shanghai Bokura, a successor in spirit to the original Blue Protocol that Bandai Namco ran from 2023 to 2025 but a distinct product.

Behind The Scenes

Blue Protocol: Star Resonance is developed by Shanghai Bokura Network Technology and published by A Plus Japan. It is a different product from Bandai Namco's Blue Protocol, which ran from 2023 to 2025.

The game launched globally on October 9, 2025 on Steam, the Epic Games Store, iOS and Android, peaking at about 95,000 concurrent players in the first 24 hours on Steam.

Experience

Combat and roles

It is an action MMORPG with traditional DPS, tank and healer roles plus real-time dodging and combo chains. Players gather resources, join social activities and explore an open map with many side activities.

Visuals and music

The most praised aspects are the anime style and music. Fans mention the variety of side activities in the open map. This is what keeps players engaged in the first hours.

Monetization and servers

Players complained about a lack of things to do, pay-to-win accusations and content lockouts. The game was said to lack localized servers, forcing players outside the US to connect to a North American server and causing connectivity problems. Game8 titled its review "Not Bad, But Not Good".

Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths

  • Attractive anime style and music
  • Many side activities on the open map
  • Free to download on PC and mobile
  • Drew a large player base on launch day

Weaknesses

  • Heavy monetization, criticized as pay-to-win
  • No regional servers, causing lag for many players
  • Slow progression, said not to respect players' time

What Critics Say

The game reached over 90,000 concurrent players on Steam on launch day with a 24-hour peak around 94,000, yet user reviews sat at Mixed over server issues and monetization. Massively Overpowered noted the game earned mixed reviews in its opening weekend.

Analysts argued the game is pretty but not worth the time investment, with heavy monetization and slow progression keeping it from becoming a worthy opponent.

Who Is It For

For players who like anime-styled MMORPGs and do not mind repeated side activities. If you dislike pay-to-win or need stable regional servers, wait for improvements. Players who value time and low cost may choose alternatives.

FAQ

Is Blue Protocol: Star Resonance free?

Yes, free-to-play with gacha for cosmetics, items and combat abilities.

Is it related to Bandai Namco's Blue Protocol?

No, it is a separate product from Bokura, a successor in spirit.

Why are Steam reviews Mixed?

Players cited server issues, lack of content and heavy monetization.
