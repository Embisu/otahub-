Genshin Impact 7.0: Snezhnaya Review: An Ambitious Final Chapter

HoYoverse concludes the 7-year journey of building Teyvat with Snezhnaya, the most ambitious nation, solving Genshin Impact's longest-running plot mysteries.

OtaHub › Reviews › Genshin Impact 7.0: Snezhnaya
Reviews 2026-08-15 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Genshin Impact version 7.0 "Everwinter Without Mercy" officially opened Snezhnaya, the 7th country and also the penultimate chapter in Teyvat's main storyline. The update introduces a new character, Odette, along with a brand-new shooting combat mode, and also solves a series of storyline mysteries that HoYoverse has been woven into since the early versions.

After many years of being mentioned as a mysterious frozen land where Fatui and Traveler had a deep connection, Snezhnaya is finally officially open in Genshin Impact 7.0. As the 7th country of Teyvat, this is considered the final chapter in the main storyline journey that has lasted since the game's launch in 2020.

Odette and shooting mode: groundbreaking gameplay

The new character Odette comes with HoYoverse introducing a completely new combat mode based on shooting mechanics, significantly expanding the familiar combat system from the previous 7 versions. This is one of the boldest gameplay changes in game history, showing that HoYoverse still has many new ideas to keep long-time players engaged.

Plot: solve Genshin's longest running mystery

In terms of storyline, Snezhnaya is where many of Genshin Impact's biggest pieces are connected, from the true identities of the Harbingers, the relationship between the Traveler and his missing sibling, to the true nature of the war between the Gods and the Celestial Realm. HoYoverse's handling of this massive amount of lore is considered controlled, not overly compressed despite being the most information-rich chapter to date.

World design: icy but diverse

Breaking the stereotype that "ice nations are monotonous," the Snezhnaya design team created a remarkably diverse landscape, from gloomy industrial cities to majestic icy mountains, accurately reflecting the character of a highly militarized empire while still retaining the distinctive aesthetic beauty of Genshin.
