Anime 2026-08-22 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Studio ufotable and Aniplex have officially dropped an explosive 3-minute theatrical trailer for Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – Infinity Castle: Part 1, locking in a worldwide theatrical rollout across IMAX and Dolby Cinema formats.

The international anime community erupted as acclaimed animation powerhouse ufotable and distributor Aniplex released the definitive 3-minute trailer for Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – Infinity Castle: Part 1.

A Visual Triumph from Studio ufotable

The trailer showcases breathtaking dimensional sweeps across the shifting chambers of Nakime's fortress. Director Haruo Sotozaki seamlessly combines intricate 3D camera angles with fluid hand-drawn character animation.

The highlight features the high-stakes clash between Tanjiro Kamado, Water Hashira Giyu Tomioka, and Upper Moon 3 Akaza, brought to life with devastating Sun Breathing and Water Breathing visual effects.

Global Theatrical Release in IMAX & Dolby Cinema

The film is confirmed for theatrical release across more than 140 countries in premium formats including IMAX, Dolby Cinema, 4DX, and ScreenX. The new main theme is performed as a historic duet between iconic vocalists LiSA and Aimer.
