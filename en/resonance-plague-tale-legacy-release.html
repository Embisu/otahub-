Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy Released August 27, 2026

Asobo Studio and Focus Entertainment have confirmed that Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, a prequel set 15 years before Innocence, will be released on August 27, 2026, on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S, telling the story of young pirate Sophia on her journey to uncover her family's secrets on Minotaur Island.

Summary
Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, a prequel to the series A Plague Tale popular, will be released on 27/8/2026 above PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC (Steam, Epic Games Store, available day one on Xbox Game Pass). The game takes place 15 years ago Innocence event, taking players in the footsteps of a young female pirate Sophia on the journey to find family secrets at Minotaur Island, with a score by the composer Olivier Derivière undertake.

Focus Entertainment and Asobo Studio officially confirmed Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy, the prequel to the award-winning action-adventure game series A Plague Tale, will be released on 27/8/2026, present at the same time on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. New information and trailer were announced at the Xbox Games Showcase 2026 event.

15 years ago Innocence, a completely new perspective

Resonance offers a new perspective on the world A Plague Tale takes place in 15 years ago events in Innocence and Requiem. Players take on the role Sophia, a young and resilient female pirate, sets out for the distant shore that has always "called" her name since childhood, Minotaur Island. Unfortunately, the companions she tried to escape never went away. As she gradually discovers the connection between her family and the island, Sophia can rely on her companion Leni to face dangerous enemies and deadly challenges.

Background of the Middle Ages and the Minoan era

A special feature of Resonance is the intersectional context the Middle Ages and ancient Minoan civilization, opens up a new mythical world compared to the previous two parts. In the depths of Minotaur Island, untold secrets will gradually be revealed, including the appearance of a unexpected ally, a legendary hero coming out of the most ancient legends. The game's soundtrack is by a composer who has been praised by critics many times Olivier Derivière undertake.

Release and pre-order packages

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is now open for pre-order on all platforms, with additional downloadable content for free Heritage Pack includes the Minoan sword, Alec's pendant, and an in-game artbook of over 30 pages. The game will launch simultaneously on Steam, Epic Games Store, and be available from day one on Xbox Game Pass and supports Xbox Play Anywhere.

  • Launch date: 27/8/2026
  • Platform: PC (Steam, Epic Games Store), PS5, Xbox Series X|S
  • Developer: Asobo Studio
  • Publisher: Focus Entertainment
  • Main character: Sophia, with her companion Leni
  • Background: 15 years before A Plague Tale: Innocence, on Minotaur Island
  • Pre-order offers: Heritage Pack (Minoan sword, Alec pendant, 30+ page artbook)
