Food Wars Duo And We Never Learn Author Return On Jump GIGA Summer 2026

The Jump GIGA Summer 2026 issue brings together two notable one-shots: the Food Wars author duo returns after the failure of Tenmaku Cinema, and the We Never Learn author appears alongside the running series Animal Signal.

OtaHub › Manga › Food Wars Duo And We Never Learn Author Return On Jump GIGA Summer 2026
Manga 2026-08-17 5 minutes reading OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Magazine Jump GIGA Shueisha's Summer 2026 issue officially hits shelves in Japan today 17/8/2026, brings together two notable one-shots: "Futari Dake no Quiz-bu" by the duo Yuto Tsukuda and Shun Saeki, author of Food Wars! Shokugeki no Soma, and "Kanojo no Melting Outdoors" by Taishi Tsutsui, author We Never Learn: Bokuben.

Magazines Jump Shueisha's is having a much better year than before, with most of its series ending naturally instead of being canceled midway like last year. In addition, many authors whose series were canceled are returning with new projects, and the number Jump GIGA Summer 2026, released in Japan on 17/8/2026, is a clear proof.

The Food Wars duo returns after the Tenmaku Cinema stumble

Most notable is the return of Yuto Tsukuda and Shun Saeki, the duo behind the huge success of Food Wars! Shokugeki no Soma, the series completed its entire journey on Shounen Jump in 2019. After that, the two tried their hand at the new series Tenmaku Cinema in 2023, but this work was canceled after less than six months of posting because it did not gain the expected appeal. This time, they brought a 4-page one-shot titled "Futari Dake no Quiz-bu" (roughly translated: Just Two People Quiz Club), a short romantic comedy story.

Taishi Tsutsui: just released a one-shot, and has a running series

In parallel, Taishi Tsutsui, father of We Never Learn: Bokuben, also featured in a 4-page romantic comedy one-shot "Kanojo no Melting Outdoors". Notably, his follow-up project We Never Learn is titled Syd Craft: Love is a Mystery was canceled shortly after its release last year. However, currently, Tsutsui is still actively working on Weekly Shounen Jump as an artist for a humorous series about animals. Animal Signal, has only published a few chapters.

Second chances for veteran authors

While surviving in the weekly Jump magazine is always a tough challenge with the pressure of achieving almost immediate success, the reality is that no cancellation is the complete end for an author. Shueisha's continued giving opportunities to veteran writers to return with new projects, whether short one-shots or long-running series, is a positive sign that fans can still look forward to seeing familiar faces in the future.

  • Release date: August 17, 2026 (Japan)
  • One-shot 1: "Futari Dake no Quiz-bu", Yuto Tsukuda & Shun Saeki (Food Wars!)
  • One-shot 2: "Kanojo no Melting Outdoors", Taishi Tsutsui (We Never Learn)
  • Format: Romantic comedy short stories, 4 pages per set
