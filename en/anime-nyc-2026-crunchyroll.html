Anime NYC 2026: premieres and panels to watch

Crunchyroll brings two premiere screenings, the 10th anniversaries of Re:ZERO and Bungo Stray Dogs, and a Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun production panel to Javits Center on August 20–23.

OtaHub › Anime › Anime NYC 2026: premieres and panels to watch
Anime 2026-08-18 7 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Anime NYC 2026 runs at Javits Center from August 20–23. Crunchyroll's official schedule includes two compilation premiere screenings, 10th-anniversary panels for Re:ZERO and Bungo Stray Dogs, and a production session for Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun.

Anime NYC 2026 takes place at New York's Javits Center from August 20 through 23. Crunchyroll's confirmed program combines early screenings, production discussions and anniversary events for established franchises.

Key context: Times below are local to New York. The full title lineup for the two compilation premiere screenings may be announced closer to the event.

Crunchyroll schedule highlights

Aug. 21 · 12:15–1:15 p.m.
Bungo Stray Dogs anime 10th anniversary with Yuto Uemura and producer Chiaki Kurakane.
Aug. 21 · 2:30–4:15 p.m.
Re:ZERO 10th anniversary concert and Q&A with Yusuke Kobayashi and Konomi Suzuki.
Aug. 22 · noon–1 p.m.
Welcome to Demon School! Iruma-kun panel with Setsuo Ito and general director Makoto Moriwaki.
Aug. 22 · 1:30–2:30 p.m.
World premiere of Magical Buffs Episodes 1 and 2 with production staff.
Aug. 22 · 5:15–7:15 p.m.
Crunchyroll Premiere Screening 1.
Aug. 23 · 10:45 a.m.–12:30 p.m.
Crunchyroll Premiere Screening 2.

Why the premiere screenings matter

The initial announcement leaves parts of both screening lineups open. These sessions are therefore the likeliest places for fresh episode previews, trailers and streaming details. OtaHub will distinguish confirmed platform information from audience reports and social-media speculation.

Anniversary panels emphasize production history

The Re:ZERO program adds a live concert, while Bungo Stray Dogs brings a lead cast member and producer together. Both sessions offer a stronger opportunity for reliable behind-the-scenes context than unsourced rumor cycles.

What OtaHub will update next

We will track the final premiere lineup, trailers, territorial streaming details and official panel announcements throughout the event.
