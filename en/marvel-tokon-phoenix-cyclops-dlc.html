What has been confirmed?

  • Phoenix Cyclops character
  • first Year 1 DLC
  • developed by Arc System Works

The announcement comes from the official source linked below. Dates, platforms and regional availability can still change, so OtaHub will update this page when the publisher provides additional information.

Why this update matters

MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls is Arc System Works' new Marvel fighting game — the studio behind Guilty Gear and the acclaimed 2D-in-3D style of Dragon Ball FighterZ — so this DLC roster and season cadence will be read as an early indicator of whether ASW can sustain a live-service fighter the way FighterZ did.

What should readers watch next?

Watch for the rest of the Year 1 roster following Phoenix Cyclops, and whether ASW follows FighterZ's balance-patch and tournament-support model. Readers can also browse our latest news, Gaming coverage and in-depth features.

OtaHub assessment

A promising sign if Arc System Works applies its FighterZ post-launch playbook here — choosing a fan-favorite X-Men combo as the first DLC character is an early positive signal for roster ambition.
