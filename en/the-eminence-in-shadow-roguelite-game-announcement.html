The Eminence in Shadow New Roguelite Game Announced: Alpha Leads Shadow Garden Against Diabolos Cult

Sony State of Play officially unveils a fast-paced roguelite action game based on The Eminence in Shadow for PS5 and PC, featuring Alpha and the Seven Shadows in cinematic combat.

Home › Gaming › The Eminence in Shadow New Roguelite Game Announced: Alpha Leads Shadow Garden Against Diabolos Cult
Gaming 2026-09-09 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Sony State of Play officially unveils a fast-paced roguelite action game based on The Eminence in Shadow for PS5 and PC, featuring Alpha and the Seven Shadows in cinematic combat.

During the Sony State of Play broadcast in September 2026, fans of The Eminence in Shadow (Kage no Jitsuryokusha ni Naritakute!) received major news: A brand-new premium 3D Roguelite action game is officially in development for PlayStation 5 and PC, targeting a 2027 worldwide release.

1. High-Speed Hack-and-Slash Roguelite Mechanics

Departing from conventional mobile gacha titles, this standalone project focuses on fast-paced hack-and-slash combat blended with deep dungeon-crawling roguelite progression. Players delve into subterranean Diabolos Cult fortresses, harvesting Magic Crystals to upgrade custom Slime Suits and adapt to escalating combat challenges.

Each dungeon run grants randomized ancient runes, unlocking signature abilities like shadow sword beams, illusions, and explosive finishing maneuvers unique to Shadow Garden lore.

2. Alpha & The Seven Shadows Take the Lead

In a thrilling narrative shift, Alpha — First Seat of the Seven Shadows — serves as the primary protagonist. Players can seamlessly swap between key operatives including Beta, Gamma, Delta, Epsilon, Zeta, and Eta, each armed with bespoke weapon loadouts and distinct tactical skill trees.

Lord Shadow (Cid Kagenou) makes dramatic appearances as an ultimate summonable asset, unleashing his iconic screen-clearing I AM ATOMIC strike during pivotal boss confrontations.

3. Unreal Engine 5 Visuals & Global Launch

Built with Unreal Engine 5, the title showcases vibrant cel-shaded aesthetics, real-time particle lighting, and cinematic fluidity matching top-tier anime movies. A public playable demo is slated for Tokyo Game Show (TGS) ahead of global release.
