Shueisha Launches MANGA MILLION: Read Nearly 400 Manga Sets For Free

To celebrate its 100th anniversary, Shueisha launches MANGA MILLION, a free, no-registration manga reading platform, with nearly 400 series translated into more than 100 languages.

OtaHub › Manga › Shueisha Launches MANGA MILLION: Read Nearly 400 Manga Sets For Free
Manga 2026-08-12 6 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Shueisha has just launched MANGA MILLION, a free online manga reading platform that doesn't require an account, offering approximately 1 million pages of manga from nearly 400 series translated into over 100 languages. The service features major franchises such as One Piece, Naruto, Bleach, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, SPY x FAMILY... and will operate until the end of December 2027.

For many manga readers outside of Japan, the biggest barrier has never been the lack of good stories, but the lack of translations. Thousands of titles existed for decades only in Japanese, never reaching readers in smaller markets. On the occasion of the anniversary 100 years of establishment, Shueisha, Japan's largest manga publisher, seems to have decided to tackle that exact problem at once: a new manga reading platform called MANGA MILLION Just officially launched, completely free and does not require anyone to create an account.

One million pages, over 100 languages

The number that Shueisha gave is quite impressive: approx 1 million manga pages, stretching nearly 400 series, translated to more 100 languages They are different. Even more noteworthy than the scale is its unprecedented nature; nearly 300 of these titles had never had official translations before, while around 50 works were translated into multiple languages simultaneously. In other words, this wasn't simply gathering existing translations onto a website, but a large-scale localization effort undertaken almost from scratch.

The brand lineup couldn't be more famous

Looking at the launch list, it's hard to say Shueisha did it half-heartedly. ONE PIECE, NARUTO, BLEACH, Demon Slayer, JUJUTSU KAISEN, Chainsaw Man, SPY x FAMILY, Kingdom, Reborn!, Yu Yu Hakusho, OSHI NO KO, Nana and Kimi ni Todoke: From Me to You were present from day one, a collection ranging from monuments that have shaped an entire generation of readers to phenomena that are still making waves on social networks.

No account, runs until 2027

The feature that many users will likely appreciate most: MANGA MILLION doesn't require account registration. Simply go directly to the official website to read, no password, no email confirmation – a rare experience on licensed manga reading platforms. The service officially launched on August 6, 2026, and is expected to run until December 2027, considered a key part of the publisher's centenary celebrations.

  • Launch: August 6, 2026, running until December 2027
  • Content: ~1 million pages, nearly 400 manga volumes
  • Language: over 100 languages, ~300 titles officially translated for the first time
  • Access: Completely free, no need to register an account

Why this matters to international readers

This is perhaps one of the boldest globalization moves a major Japanese manga publisher has ever made, and it's particularly significant for readers in markets that have never had many licensed reading options in their native language. Compared to familiar commercial platforms like Manga Plus or Shounen Jump+, which still require accounts and sometimes even payments, MANGA MILLION's completely free access virtually eliminates the barriers to entry.

It remains to be seen whether Shueisha will maintain this free model after the anniversary campaign ends at the end of 2027. But right now, with nearly 400 manga series and millions of pages of comics just a click away, MANGA MILLION is opening the door that many international readers have long awaited.
