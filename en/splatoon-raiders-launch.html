Splatoon Raiders Launches On Nintendo Switch 2

Splatoon Raiders, the first single-player-focused spin-off in Splatoon history, launched July 23, 2026 exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2, sending players alongside Deep Cut to explore the Spirhalite Islands.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-18 · 6 min read

In short: Splatoon Raiders officially launched July 23, 2026, exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2, offering a single-player adventure with Deep Cut across the Spirhalite Islands, plus a 4-player co-op mode.

Splatoon Raiders, the first single-player-focused spin-off in the history of the Splatoon series, officially launched July 23, 2026, exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2.

Players take on the role of an Inkling or Octoling working as a mechanic, joining the musical trio Deep Cut, first introduced in Splatoon 3, on an expedition to the mysterious Spirhalite Islands in search of treasure. Along the way, players can customize their character and equip mechanical Gadgets alongside a full set of ink weapons to fend off waves of Salmonid enemies.

Alongside its single-player campaign, which is the game's main focus, Splatoon Raiders also includes a co-op mode for up to four players, both locally and online. Nintendo first announced the game on June 10, 2025 via the Nintendo Today! app, alongside a set of Joy-Con controllers styled after Deep Cut and a series of daily comic strips telling the story leading up to the game's events, released from June 23 through launch.
