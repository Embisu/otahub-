Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Goes Playable At Gamescom 2026, Confirmed For Nintendo Switch 2

At the Xbox booth at Gamescom 2026 in Cologne, attendees got hands-on time with Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4's Core 6v6 Multiplayer mode for the first time, while Activision confirmed the blockbuster shooter will launch day-and-date on Nintendo Switch 2 on October 23.

Author: OtaHub Editorial
Published: 2026-08-29
Reading time: 6 min read
Category: Gaming News
First Hands-On Time With MW4
Xbox brought 140 playable stations and 25 games to Gamescom 2026, with Modern Warfare 4 as the centerpiece — a Core 6v6 Multiplayer demo on Xbox Series X showing off overhauled fluid movement and ballistics.

1. Modern Warfare 4 Goes Hands-On At Gamescom

Xbox returned to Gamescom 2026 in Cologne with its biggest presence yet: 140 playable stations spread across 25 games, marking 25 years of the Xbox brand. The star of the booth was unquestionably Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, which let press and fans get hands-on with its Core 6v6 Multiplayer mode on Xbox Series X stations for the first time, showcasing the fluid movement and signature ballistics that define the Modern Warfare sub-series.

2. An Unprecedented Korean Peninsula Setting

First announced by Infinity Ward in May 2026, this Modern Warfare 4 campaign drops players into an entirely new global crisis: North Korea launches a full-scale invasion of South Korea, pushing the world toward destabilization. The story uses a dual-protagonist structure — Private Park, a young South Korean soldier facing live combat for the first time alongside his squad on a collapsing front line, and the legendary Captain Price, now operating outside the official chain of command on a personal mission that pulls him back to the center of the conflict.

3. A Global Journey Across Multiple Fronts

The campaign takes players through brutal trench warfare in Korea, tense close-quarters combat in New York City, high-speed chases through Paris, SAS night raids in Mumbai, and city-scale battles to reclaim occupied territory. The development team describes it as the most grounded Modern Warfare story yet, with a darker tone, large-scale cinematic missions, and a global crisis with real stakes.

4. Release Date And Platforms

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 officially launches on October 23, 2026 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC — and for the first time in the series' history, day-and-date on Nintendo Switch 2. Bringing a flagship Call of Duty title to a Nintendo platform at launch is seen as a major strategic move, significantly expanding the potential player base for gaming's best-selling shooter franchise.
