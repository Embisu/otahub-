Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Anime Officially Announced with First Teaser Trailer

REDICE Studio, D&C Media, and Aniplex officially announce the global anime adaptation of Solo Leveling: Ragnarok, following Sung Suho's monarch legacy.

🕒 Sep 07, 2026 ✍️ Tik (Khanh Quynh) ⏱️ 6 min read
⚡ Key Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Highlights
  • New Protagonist: Sung Suho, the son of the Shadow Monarch Sung Jinwoo and Cha Hae-in.
  • Animation Studio: A-1 Pictures returns to lead animation production alongside Aniplex and D&C Media.
  • Epic Soundtrack: Legendary composer Hiroyuki Sawano returns to deliver the orchestral score.
  • Global Premiere: Streaming worldwide on Crunchyroll and premier streaming platforms.

1. A New Era Following Sung Jinwoo's Departure

Following the tremendous worldwide triumph of Solo Leveling, REDICE Studio, D&C Media, and Aniplex have officially greenlit the anime adaptation of the blockbuster sequel: Solo Leveling: Ragnarok.

Set years after Sung Jinwoo ventured into deep dimensional space to battle the Outer Gods (Itarim) in defense of Earth, humanity faces renewed existential catastrophe as higher-tier dimensional Gates re-emerge. The narrative shifts focus to Sung Suho, Jinwoo's son, who must awaken his dormant Shadow Monarch bloodline.

2. A-1 Pictures & Hiroyuki Sawano Return

Acclaimed animation powerhouse A-1 Pictures returns to produce the sequel, guaranteeing top-tier battle choreography and fluid Sakuga animation. Renowned composer Hiroyuki Sawano is confirmed to compose the cinematic soundtrack.
