Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium Review: Tactical 3D Masterpiece & Cel-Shaded Artistry

A Bold Tactical Evolution
MICA Team reinvents the franchise into a high-production turn-based tactical RPG with deep cover mechanics.

After years of anticipation and meticulous development, Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium from MICA Team (Sunborn Network) has officially launched worldwide. Transitioning away from the 2D chibi tile-battler roots of its predecessor, Exilium delivers an ambitious turn-based tactical RPG heavily influenced by strategy stalwarts like XCOM, wrapped in pristine 3D anime cel-shading.

1. Gritty Post-Apocalyptic Story: 10 Years After Griffon & Kryuger

Set a decade after the catastrophic collapse of PMC Griffon & Kryuger, players step back into the combat boots of the Commander. Now operating as an independent bounty hunter aboard the armored mobile base Elmo, you traverse the radiation-ravaged Yellow Zones. The narrative strikes a somber, military-authentic tone, examining post-war trauma and the bonds between humans and Tactical Dolls (T-Dolls).

2. Strategic Cover Systems & Stability Break Dynamics

The core combat elevates tactical decision-making far beyond standard mobile gacha experiences:

  • Dynamic Cover & Elevation: Utilizing half and full cover alongside vertical high ground drastically mitigates incoming ballistic damage and maximizes critical strike probability.
  • Stability Breakdown Mechanic: Breaking down an enemy unit's stability shield triggers devastating follow-up counterattacks and coordinated crossfire executions.
  • Detailed 3D Dormitory Interactions: High-fidelity modeling for fan-favorite T-Dolls like Groza, Nemesis, and Charolic with expressive animations and weapon maintenance interactions.

3. Final Verdict & Tactical Recommendation

With console-grade visual fidelity, fluid cinematic camera angles, and rigorous tactical depth, Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium establishes itself as a premier turn-based strategy experience in 2026.

8.8
EXCELLENT · DEFINITIVE TACTICAL RPG
Breathtaking 3D visual leap, addictive hardcore XCOM mechanics, and a mature post-war storyline.
