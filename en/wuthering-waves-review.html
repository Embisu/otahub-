Wuthering Waves Review: Open-World Combat (9.5/10)

Wuthering Waves review: Kuro Games' open-world gacha action RPG launched May 23, 2024, with Echo and parry combat and parkour traversal; Metacritic 71 to 76, a forgiving gacha but a buggy start.

Home › Reviews › Wuthering Waves Review: Open-World Combat (9.5/10)
Mobile / Action RPG · Review 2026-08-18 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Wuthering Waves review: Kuro Games' open-world gacha action RPG launched May 23, 2024, with Echo and parry combat and parkour traversal; Metacritic 71 to 76, a forgiving gacha but a buggy start.

Wuthering Waves is Kuro Games' attempt to bring real-time action to the open-world gacha genre. It leans into fast combat, constant character switching and precise parrying rather than the turn-based strategy many rivals favor. Despite launching with a string of bugs, the game stabilized and became one of Tencent's successful gacha titles.

The game launched on May 23, 2024 on Windows, Android and iOS, then reached PS5 (January 2, 2025), macOS (March 27, 2025) and Xbox Series X|S (July 10, 2026).

Quick Facts

  • Release: May 23, 2024 (PC, mobile); PS5 January 2, 2025; macOS March 27, 2025; Xbox July 10, 2026
  • Developer: Kuro Games; free-to-play gacha on Unreal Engine
  • Genre: Open-world action RPG
  • Critic scores: Metacritic 71 (PC), 73 (iOS), 76 (PS5); OpenCritic 82%; Famitsu 34/40; IGN 7/10
  • Sales: Over 30 million pre-registrations and most downloaded in over 100 regions in its first week

Story

The game takes place on the planet Solaris-3 after catastrophic events called Laments. Players take the role of Rover, an amnesiac who gradually uncovers a connection to the Black Shores organization, while the Fractsidus seek to intensify the True Lament to drive human evolution.

The world spans three nations: Huanglong with seven cities, Rinascita, a confederation of city-states, and Lahai-Roi, an underground tribal nation. Version 2.0 takes players to Rinascita.

Behind The Scenes

Wuthering Waves is developed and published by Kuro Games, initially on Unreal Engine 4 and upgraded to Engine 5 in May 2024. After launch the game expanded its platforms to PS5, macOS and Xbox, and added mechanics across versions: flight modules in 2.0, the Expedition Motorbike in 3.0 and Tune Break in December 2025.

A Chinese animated series titled Wuthering Waves: Elysium was announced on July 18, 2026.

Experience

Combat

The system revolves around switching characters and triggering Intro/Outro skills, using Echoes for stat bonuses and precise parries to counter. Players control Rover alongside 4-star and 5-star Resonators. Later updates added mechanics such as Tune Break in December 2025 for more tactical depth. Critics praised the combat as refreshing.

Exploration and traversal

Players climb with stamina, wall-run, dodge in mid-air and glide; running in open fields does not drain stamina. Version 2.0 added flight modules, and 3.0 added the Expedition Motorbike for ground traversal.

Gacha and launch state

The Convene system has 5-star pity guarantees and critics called it forgiving. At launch, however, the game suffered bugs such as accessing unreleased content by changing the clock, audio delays and Fatal Error screens; Kuro Games patched and compensated players with rewards.

Strengths

  • Fast combat with character switching and parries feels like true action
  • Flexible parkour traversal with no stamina drain while running
  • The gacha is regarded as forgiving
  • Regular updates add vehicles and mechanics

Weaknesses

  • A buggy, troubled launch
  • Metacritic scores of 71 to 76, lower than many genre peers
  • Still gacha, with characters and weapons behind pulls

What Critics Say

Initial reception was mixed but stabilized: Metacritic 71 on PC, 73 on iOS and 76 on PS5, with OpenCritic at 82%. Famitsu scored 34/40, IGN 7/10, RPGFan 90/100 and Pocket Gamer 4.5/5.

In its first week the game topped downloads in over 100 regions with over 30 million pre-registrations. In September 2025, Tencent named Wuthering Waves among six new titles passing its Evergreen revenue line, alongside Zenless Zone Zero, Black Myth: Wukong and Delta Force. It also won Best Original Song at the Hollywood Music in Media Awards and three Gold Awards at Taipei Game Show 2026.

Who Is It For

For players who like real-time action in an open world and do not mind gacha. Those used to turn-based combat may find the pace brisk. If you prioritize a polished day-one experience, remember the game had a rocky start.

Verdict

Wuthering Waves is an open-world action gacha worth trying, strong in combat and traversal and improving steadily with each update. The lower Metacritic scores and rocky launch are two caveats. OtaHub scores it 9.5/10.

FAQ

Is Wuthering Waves free?

Yes, it is free-to-play with gacha (Convene).

Which platforms is it on?

Windows, Android, iOS, PS5, macOS and Xbox Series X|S.

Is the gacha forgiving?

Critics described the gacha as forgiving, with 5-star pity guarantees.
