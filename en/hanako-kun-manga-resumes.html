Toilet-Bound Hanako-kun Manga Returns After 10-Month Health Hiatus

Creator AidaIro confirms Toilet-Bound Hanako-kun officially resumed serialization on August 18, 2026 in Square Enix's Monthly GFantasy, closing out a roughly 10-month hiatus taken for medical treatment.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-19 · 5 min read

In short: Toilet-Bound Hanako-kun (Jibaku Shounen Hanako-kun) by AidaIro officially resumed serialization on August 18, 2026 in Square Enix's Monthly GFantasy, ending an almost 10-month hiatus taken for the author's medical treatment. The series has sold more than 10 million copies worldwide.

Toilet-Bound Hanako-kun, the hit manga by AidaIro, officially returned to serialization on August 18, 2026 in the September 2026 issue of Square Enix's Monthly GFantasy, closing out a hiatus that lasted nearly 10 months.

The series went on hiatus starting with the November 2025 issue, when AidaIro announced they needed to pause work to focus on medical treatment. Ahead of the September issue going on sale, the creator confirmed on X that Hanako-kun would be returning, thanking readers for their patience throughout the break.

Toilet-Bound Hanako-kun follows Yashiro Nene, a high school girl who accidentally summons the ghost Hanako-kun — a well-known urban legend said to haunt the third-floor girls' restroom — and ends up helping him keep order among the spirits roaming the school grounds. Before the hiatus, the series had sold more than 10 million copies worldwide and been adapted into a TV anime, with season 1 airing in 2020 and season 2 premiering in 2025.

The manga's return, arriving while the anime is still drawing a steady audience, further cements Toilet-Bound Hanako-kun as one of Square Enix's strongest manga-anime pairings today.
