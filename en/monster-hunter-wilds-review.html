Monster Hunter Wilds Review: Next-Gen Hunting (9.4/10)

Monster Hunter Wilds review: Capcom adds Focus Mode, the Seikret mount and a dynamic ecosystem, Metacritic 88 and 8 million copies in three days; PC performance and low difficulty are the drawbacks.

Home › Gaming › Monster Hunter Wilds Review: Next-Gen Hunting (9.4/10)
PC / Console Game · Review 2026-08-18 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Monster Hunter Wilds review: Capcom adds Focus Mode, the Seikret mount and a dynamic ecosystem, Metacritic 88 and 8 million copies in three days; PC performance and low difficulty are the drawbacks.

Monster Hunter Wilds is a big step forward in presentation for Capcom: a seamless world where monsters interact with each other and the weather, and players can hunt without loading screens in between. In return, critics called it easier than previous entries and its endgame less compelling.

The game launched on February 28, 2025 on PS5, Windows and Xbox Series X|S, becoming Capcom's fastest-selling game with over 8 million copies in three days.

Quick Facts

  • Release: February 28, 2025
  • Developer: Capcom; RE Engine; directed by Yuya Tokuda
  • Modes: Up to four-player online co-op with cross-platform play; solo players get AI support hunters
  • Critic scores: Metacritic 88 (PS5, PC), 90 (Xbox Series X); OpenCritic 95%; Famitsu 39/40
  • Sales: Over 8 million in three days, 10 million by end of March 2025

Story

Players guide Nata, a hunter from the Forbidden Lands, and the Avis Unit as they investigate the White Wraith Arkveld, the Landspine structure and the ancient civilization Wyveria. Along the way they face Zoh Shia, a colossal guardian dragon, and Frenzy Virus-infected Gore Magala, and must protect the Dragontorch.

Some critics praised a more focused story than earlier entries: Destructoid called Nata's story captivating, and Game Informer said it was pleasantly surprised the plot kept it invested throughout the campaign.

Behind The Scenes

Monster Hunter Wilds is developed by Capcom on the RE Engine, directed by Yuya Tokuda. After launch, the game received free updates from April 2025 adding monsters, quests, gear and multiplayer activities such as the Grand Hub, plus collaborations with Street Fighter 6 (May 2025) and Final Fantasy XIV (September 2025) featuring the Omega monster and a Chocobo mount.

The major expansion Monster Hunter Wilds: Ascendance is planned for 2027, and early-2026 patches targeted CPU and GPU optimization on PC.

Experience

Seamless hunting and Focus Mode

The game features 14 weapon types with new moves. The Seikret, a mount that accompanies the player, provides fast travel and carries two weapons into hunts. Focus Mode lets players target specific monster parts; repeated strikes create wounds, and a Focus Strike destroys them for heavy damage and a stagger. Pop-up camps make continuous hunting possible across the open map.

Ecosystem and weather

Monsters in Wilds live in a dynamic ecosystem: they interact with each other, move in herds and are affected by weather and time of day. This makes the world feel more alive than earlier entries, and underpins the free updates adding monsters, quests and gear from April 2025.

Performance and challenge

The PC version is the biggest problem: by July 2025, Steam reviews turned Overwhelmingly Negative due to frame-rate drops and graphical glitches despite multiple patches. On challenge, IGN called the game light on any real difficulty, while Edge said post-credits content lacks the self-sustaining drive of earlier entries.

Strengths

  • A seamless world with a dynamic ecosystem
  • Focus Mode and wounds add new depth to combat
  • 14 weapons and the Seikret make hunting more flexible
  • A more focused story than earlier entries

Weaknesses

  • PC performance is unstable despite many patches
  • Low difficulty, with IGN citing a lack of real challenge
  • Endgame content is seen as lacking drive

What Critics Say

Metacritic lists 88 on PS5 and Windows and 90 on Xbox Series X, with OpenCritic at 95% recommend. Destructoid scored 9.5/10, Famitsu 39/40, Video Games Chronicle 5/5, GamesRadar+ 4.5/5, Game Informer 8.75/10 and GameSpot 8/10. Criticism came from IGN on difficulty and Edge on the endgame.

The game won the Award for Excellence at the 2025 Japan Game Awards and was nominated at The Game Awards 2025 (Best Role Playing Game). Q2 2025 sales slowed to just 477,000 units while Capcom continued with free updates and collaborations with Street Fighter 6 and Final Fantasy XIV.

Who Is It For

For newcomers wanting a more approachable way into the series, and fans who want a seamless hunting world. PC players should watch optimization patches before buying. Veteran hunters seeking high challenge may find it easy.

Verdict

Monster Hunter Wilds is Capcom's best-looking and most seamless monster hunter, strong in ecosystem and combat, weaker in PC performance and endgame. OtaHub scores it 9.4/10.
