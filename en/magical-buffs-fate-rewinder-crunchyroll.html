Magical Buffs and Fate Rewinder are coming to Crunchyroll

Crunchyroll confirmed several new anime acquisitions following its Anime Expo 2026 showcase.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-18 · 6 min read

In short: Crunchyroll confirmed several new anime acquisitions following its Anime Expo 2026 showcase.

Crunchyroll confirmed several new anime acquisitions following its Anime Expo 2026 showcase.

What has been confirmed?

  • Magical Buffs is included
  • Fate Rewinder is confirmed for streaming
  • territories vary by title

The announcement comes from the official source linked below. Dates, platforms and regional availability can still change, so OtaHub will update this page when the publisher provides additional information.

Why this update matters

Adding two titles individually rather than as part of a seasonal simulcast block suggests Crunchyroll continuing to backfill its catalog with smaller and mid-tier titles that didn't get day-one global simulcast slots.

What should readers watch next?

Watch for regional availability differences between the two titles, since they may not share the same licensing territories. Readers can also browse our latest news, Anime coverage and in-depth features.

OtaHub assessment

Useful if you were already following either title, but this is catalog-expansion news rather than a signal of a broader strategy shift.

Official source: www.crunchyroll.com.
