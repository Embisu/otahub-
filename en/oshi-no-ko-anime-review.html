Oshi no Ko Anime Review: A Hidden Corner of the Entertainment Industry Through a Sharp Lens

From the assassination of Ai Hoshino to Aqua and Ruby's journey of revenge, Oshi no Ko is one of the anime that dares to expose the dark side of the idol industry most frankly.

Home › Anime › Oshi no Ko Anime Review: A Hidden Corner of the Entertainment Industry
Reviews · Anime 2026-08-16 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Oshi no Ko, adapted from the manga by Aka Akasaka (script) and Mengo Yokoyari (illustrations), serialized in Weekly Young Jump, continues to solidify its position as one of the most incisive anime critiques of the entertainment industry. Season 2 debuted with a new voice cast including Ayane Sakura and Daisuke Ono, along with GEMN's opening theme song "Fatale," before Season 3 concluded with a highly successful finale and Season 4 has been confirmed.

Few anime dare to delve into the dark side of the Japanese entertainment industry like this Oshi no Ko. Beginning with the shocking assassination of idol Ai Hoshino, the series quickly turns into a complex story about revenge, reincarnation, and the price of survival in the glamorous yet ruthless entertainment industry.

Sharp script by Aka Akasaka

What makes Oshi no Ko unique is Aka Akasaka's script, which fearlessly exposes the dark side of the industry, from harassment and media manipulation to the immense pressure young stars face. Combined with Mengo Yokoyari's emotionally charged artwork, the series creates an experience that is both visually stunning and gritty.

Season 2: new cast, quality maintained

Season 2 welcomes additional voice cast members Ayane Sakura and Daisuke Ono, along with a new opening theme song "Fatale" by artist GEMN, continuing the storyline revolving around Aqua and Ruby as they search for the truth behind their mother's death, while expanding the world of the entertainment industry with new, in-depth characters.

Season 3 ended, Season 4 has been confirmed

Season 3 ended with a finale that attracted a lot of attention in the fan community, and soon after, Season 4 was officially confirmed for production, a signal that the sustainable appeal of this brand has not diminished.

  • Original work: Aka Akasaka (screenplay), Mengo Yokoyari (art)
  • OP Season 2: "Fatale", GEMN
  • New voice acting Season 2: Ayane Sakura, Daisuke Ono
  • Condition: Season 3 has ended, Season 4 has been confirmed
