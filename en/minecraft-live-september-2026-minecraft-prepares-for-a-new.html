Minecraft Live September 2026: Minecraft Prepares for a New Dimension

Minecraft Live in September 2026 brought plenty of big announcements, but the highlight was The Sift, Minecraft’s fourth dimension, coming to Java and Bedrock in 2027.

Home › Gaming › Minecraft Live September 2026: Minecraft Prepares for a New Dimension
Gaming 2026-09-30 5 min read Yu
Key Takeaway
Minecraft Live in September 2026 brought plenty of big announcements, but the highlight was The Sift, Minecraft’s fourth dimension, coming to Java and Bedrock in 2027. Players can also look forward to Ice Caves, the Frozen Zombie, Minecraft Dungeons II and a dedicated version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2.

Minecraft Live in September 2026 delivered a slate of notable announcements for the Minecraft community, headlined by The Sift, Minecraft’s first new dimension in more than 14 years. Mojang also introduced the Ice Caves biome, a new mob, a dedicated version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2, Minecraft Dungeons II and several changes to its content creation systems.

The event took place on September 26 and marked one of Minecraft’s most significant expansions in years. In particular, the addition of a completely new dimension has the community especially interested in where the sandbox game goes next.

The Sift Takes Center Stage at Minecraft Live

The biggest announcement of the event was The Sift, a completely new dimension coming to Minecraft Java and Bedrock in 2027.

It’s the first time in more than 14 years that Minecraft has added a new dimension. Until now, players had only three main realms: the Overworld, the Nether and the End. The arrival of The Sift is therefore seen as a significant change to Minecraft’s world structure.

Mojang hasn’t yet fully revealed how players will reach The Sift in regular Minecraft. However, players will get to explore the dimension earlier through Minecraft Dungeons II, which was scheduled to release on September 29.

In Minecraft Dungeons II, The Sift is presented as a soul-rich world with many new areas and environments. Mojang also revealed some of the creatures that live there, although most details about The Sift for the original Minecraft remain under wraps.

That gives the Minecraft community another reason to look forward to future Minecraft Live shows, where Mojang will likely reveal more about how the new dimension works.

Ice Caves Bring the Cold Underground

Besides The Sift, Mojang also introduced Ice Caves, new content coming to Minecraft in the near future.

As the name suggests, Ice Caves extend cold areas deep underground. There, players can find glowing ice crystals, icicles and many new structures.

One notable addition is Ice Crystals. These crystals glow and can be placed in the environment, opening up more decoration options for builds.

Icicles also have their own mechanics. Using light in the area can affect them, adding another factor for players to consider when exploring or building inside Ice Caves.

Ice Caves also introduce a new zombie variant called the Frozen Zombie. This creature can inflict a freezing effect on players and uses ice balls in combat.

Ice balls aren’t just used as weapons; they can also become materials for certain other items. This gives the new biome more gameplay value instead of just adding scenery.

Minecraft Dungeons II Is About to Launch

Another big name at the event was Minecraft Dungeons II.

The sequel to Minecraft’s action RPG series officially releases on September 29, 2026. It keeps the formula of combat, exploration and loot collecting but expands the scale of the world compared with the first game.

Instead of separate levels, Minecraft Dungeons II aims for a connected world with larger areas to explore. Characters can also jump and perform aerial attacks, making movement more flexible.

The gear system has also been expanded with more equipment slots and a new item type called Talismans, which provide various supporting effects.

The game supports single-player and multiplayer, allowing up to four players to team up. The Sift will also appear in Minecraft Dungeons II from launch.

Minecraft Officially Comes to Nintendo Switch 2

Mojang also confirmed a dedicated version of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2.

This version launches on October 27, 2026 and supports Vibrant Visuals, improving image quality and lighting in the world of Minecraft.

It’s a notable upgrade for Nintendo players, especially since Switch 2 has more powerful hardware than the previous generation. Having a dedicated version also lets Mojang make better use of the new console’s capabilities.

Mojang Wants Minecraft to Be a Bigger Creative Platform

This Minecraft Live didn’t focus only on content for players.

Mojang also talked about plans to build a creator platform, aiming to make it easier for more people to create, manage and share Minecraft content.

Specific details about the platform haven’t been announced yet. However, Mojang’s direction shows that Minecraft is being developed not just as a game but as an ecosystem for creators.

In addition, Sound Effects Packs are coming to Minecraft Marketplace later this year. Players can use different sound packs to change how the game sounds, from classic styles to funny or bizarre effects.

Minecraft World and Experiences Beyond the Game

Minecraft Live also extended the brand into experiences outside the game.

Minecraft World is coming to Chessington World of Adventures in London in 2027. The area is expected to feature many Minecraft-inspired attractions, including a roller coaster, shops and fan experience zones.

One highlight is Escape the Nether, a roller coaster inspired by the Nether. The project also includes the first Minecraft hotel, further extending the Minecraft brand into real-world entertainment.

A Bigger Minecraft in the Years Ahead

Minecraft Live in September 2026 shows Mojang continuing to expand Minecraft in many different directions. From in-game content like Ice Caves and The Sift to Minecraft Dungeons II, the Switch 2 version and creator systems, the franchise is growing beyond the scope of a single sandbox game.

The biggest highlight is still The Sift. Minecraft getting a fourth dimension after more than 14 years could open up a huge amount of new content in exploration, creatures, resources and world-building.

However, most details about The Sift in Minecraft Java and Bedrock haven’t been revealed yet. Players will have to wait for further announcements from Mojang to learn how the dimension will work and how it will change the Minecraft experience.

Key Announcements

  • The Sift: A new dimension, coming to Minecraft Java & Bedrock in 2027.
  • Ice Caves: New content with an underground ice biome and the Frozen Zombie.
  • Minecraft Dungeons II: Released September 29, 2026.
  • Minecraft on Nintendo Switch 2: Launches October 27, 2026.
  • Sound Effects Packs: Coming to Minecraft Marketplace in late 2026.
  • Minecraft World: Expected to open in London in 2027.
  • Creator Platform: Mojang is developing a system to help players create and share content.
