Anime Summer 2026: The Most Worth Watching Broadcast Schedule

Summer 2026 is the anime season with the strongest lineup of the year with Chainsaw Man Assassins Arc, new One Piece arc, Naruto remake and many.

Home › Anime › Anime Summer 2026: The Most Worth Watching Broadcast Schedule
Anime 2026-06-18 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Anime Summer 2026: Chainsaw Man Assassins Arc (MAPPA), new One Piece arc, Naruto remake Pain arc, Blue Exorcist S4, Delicious in Dungeon S2, Re:Zero S3. Broadcast season: July-September 2026.

The Summer 2026 anime schedule (July to September) is one of the most anticipated lineups in years. Not only does it feature highly anticipated sequels, but also ambitious original anime and significant remakes. Here's a comprehensive summary of what to watch and why.

Chainsaw Man: Assassins Arc, the Biggest Anime Event of the Summer

MAPPA has chosen summer 2026 to launch the most anticipated arc by fans, the Assassins Arc, featuring Quanxi, Princi, and Santa Claus. This is the manga arc most loved by Chainsaw Man fans (surpassing even the Katana Man arc), and if MAPPA maintains the quality from season 1, this will be the anime event of the year. Broadcast: one episode per week, starting July 2026. Expectations: Extremely high.

Naruto Remake: Pain Arc, Legend Revisited

Studio Pierrot and a new animation team are making Naruto fans' long-held dream a reality: remaking the most iconic arcs with modern, filler-free animation. The Pain Arc, especially the Battle of Konoha, if done correctly, will be one of the biggest anime events of the year. It's scheduled for release in August 2026 with two movie episodes to kick things off. Expectations: Very high.

One Piece: Post-Egghead Arc, Entering the Final War

After the Egghead Arc ended with shocking revelations, Toei Animation began the next adaptation arc, where the story of One Piece truly enters its conclusion. I can't spoil much, but those who have read the manga recently know that this is when everything begins to converge. Expectations: High.

Notable Original Anime

Summer 2026 also has two original anime worth watching: Kanon 2026, a sci-fi thriller from studio Bones about memory and identity in a ubiquitous AR/VR world, and Alya-san wa Kioku o Nakushita, romantic comedy about a girl who loses her short-term memories. Both have good original source material and suitable studios.

Stable Sequels

In addition to major events, summer 2026 also includes: Delicious in Dungeon S2 (Studio Trigger continues its weird and wonderful culinary isekai), Re:Zero S3 Part 2 (White Fox entering the Sanctuary arc is a fan favorite), and Overlord V (Madhouse returns with Ains Ooal Gown). These are solid sequels for those who want solid content every week.

Broadcast Schedule Summary

July 2026: Chainsaw Man Assassins Arc, Delicious in Dungeon Season 2, Kanon 2026. August 2026: Naruto Remake Pain Arc (movie premiere), One Piece continues, Re:Zero Season 3 Part 2. September 2026: Overlord V, Blue Exorcist Season 4, summer anime seasons end. This is the busiest season of the year with the most packed viewing schedule.
