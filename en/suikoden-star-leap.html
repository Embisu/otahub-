Suikoden STAR LEAP Launches Mobile in Japan: 108 New Destiny Stars, Setting Between Suikoden V and I

Konami officially releases Suikoden STAR LEAP on iOS/Android in Japan from August 7, 2026. The first mobile game in the series is set in Solar Year 453, between Suikoden V and Suikoden I, with 108 all-new Stars of Destiny.

Home › Gaming › Suikoden STAR LEAP Launches Mobile in Japan: 108 Stars
Gaming 2026-08-14 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Konami has just officially released it Suikoden STAR LEAP, the first mobile game in the history of the Suikoden franchise, on iOS and Android in the Japanese market from today 7/8/2026. By studio MYTHRIL development, the game is set in Solar Year 453, between the events of Suikoden V and Suikoden I, introducing 108 Stars of Destiny Completely new for a standalone story.

After many years of only existing as HD Remasters, the brand Suikoden Konami's legend finally makes its big move into the mobile market. Suikoden STAR LEAP has officially been released on the App Store and Google Play in Japan since August 7, 2026, marking the first time this series has an original game designed specifically for the mobile platform.

Setting: between Suikoden V and Suikoden I

In terms of plot, STAR LEAP is neither a remake nor a spin-off, but one Completely new story, takes place in Solar Year 453, a period between the events of Suikoden V and Suikoden I in the series' official timeline. This was a wise production choice: close enough to utilize locations and factions familiar to long-time fans, but far enough away to avoid directly encroaching on the storylines of the two original games.

The main character and the new 108 Stars of Destiny

The main character of the game has not been officially named (the game allows players to customize), accompanied by three key characters: Hisui, Shirin, and Shapur. True to the core tradition of the series, STAR LEAP introduces an ensemble 108 Stars of Destiny (108 Stars of Destiny) is completely new, does not overlap with characters that have appeared in previous games, a huge amount of content shows that Konami's ambition with this project is not small even though it is only released on mobile.

However, STAR LEAP still cleverly installs connections with the original Suikoden universe. Two familiar characters, Tengaar and Hix, the famous couple who appeared in both Suikoden I and Suikoden II, have been confirmed to appear in the game through separate character introduction trailers, bringing a clear sense of nostalgia to players who have been with the series since the early days.

Gameplay: upgraded pixel art, timeline combat

In terms of gameplay, STAR LEAP retains the series' signature pixel art graphic style but is significantly upgraded in detail, combined with a dynamic sound system that changes according to the progress of the match. The combat system uses mechanics timeline-based combat, allows players to plan the actions of each member of the squad in real-time sequence instead of traditional fixed turns.

  • Platform: iOS, Android (Japan, from August 7, 2026)
  • Developer: MYTHRIL, published by Konami
  • Background: Solar Year 453, between Suikoden V and Suikoden I
  • Main character: Hisui, Shirin, Shapur
  • Content: 108 completely new Stars of Destiny
  • The cameo character confirmed: Tengaar, Hix

Release roadmap: Japan first, global and PC later

Konami has clearly announced a phased release roadmap: the mobile version in Japan will launch first, followed by a global mobile version, and finally a PC/Steam version, although specific release dates for the latter two phases have not yet been revealed. Ahead of the launch, Konami held a live stream called "Suikoden Live" on August 6, 2026, on its official Japanese YouTube channel, revealing more details about the storyline and gameplay system.

Implications for the future of the brand

Konami's choice of mobile as the first platform to launch a brand-new Suikoden story is a noteworthy signal. Following the success of Suikoden I & II HD Remaster, which revived community interest, STAR LEAP shows Konami experimenting with a broader player reach before bringing content to traditional platforms. With the scale of 108 new Destiny Stars and a storyline carefully placed in the gaps in the official timeline, this is clearly not a hastily made spin-off project, but a serious test for the next direction of the entire series.
