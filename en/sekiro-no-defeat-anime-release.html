From The Sengoku Battlefield To The Big Screen
Sekiro: No Defeat, the theatrical anime adaptation of FromSoftware's acclaimed action game Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, has confirmed a three-week Japanese theatrical run starting September 4, 2026, ahead of a global Crunchyroll release.

1. Release Date Is Nearly Here, Three-Week Japan Exclusive Run

After an out-of-competition screening at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival in June 2026, Sekiro: No Defeat will officially open in Japanese theaters for three weeks starting September 4, 2026. Crunchyroll has confirmed it will stream the film exclusively worldwide in 2026, excluding Japan, China, South Korea, Russia, and Belarus.

2. The Game's Original Japanese Cast Returns

The detail most exciting to fans of the original game is that the entire Japanese voice cast of Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice is reprising their roles, including Daisuke Namikawa as Sekiro, Miyuki Satō as Kuro, Kenjirō Tsuda as Genichiro Ashina, Jin Urayama as the Sculptor, Shizuka Itō as Emma, Akimitsu Takase as Hanbei, Takaya Hashi as Owl, and Tetsuo Kanao as Isshin Ashina — preserving full emotional continuity for anyone who played the original game.

3. A Stacked Production Team, With Music Tied To Ryuichi Sakamoto's Legacy

The film is directed by Kenichi Kutsuna at studio Qzil.la, written by Takuya Satou, with character designs by Takahiro Kishida (Durarara!!). Notably, the theme song "Blu" is drawn from the late composer Ryuichi Sakamoto's legacy album The Best of 'Playing the Orchestra 2014', while the film's new score is composed by Shūta Hasunuma.

4. A "No Generative AI" Pledge Amid Industry-Wide Debate

On August 22, 2026, the production staff issued an official statement confirming that no generative AI technology was used anywhere in the making of the film — a statement that lands right as the anime industry faces intense debate over AI's role in animation production, earning Sekiro: No Defeat goodwill from a fanbase that values hand-crafted work.
