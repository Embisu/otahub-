Monster Hunter Wilds: Focus Mode Mastery Guide & 14-Weapon Combos on Seikret Mount

Master the Wound System, Focus Strikes, and dual-weapon mounted transitions in Monster Hunter Wilds.

Focus Mode and the Wound System redefine combat pacing in Monster Hunter Wilds. Learn pinpoint aiming, finisher timing, and top dual-weapon combos.

Unlike previous mechanics, Focus Mode in Monster Hunter Wilds decouples weapon strike direction from character facing, allowing hunters to target precise monster wounds and execute devastating Focus Strikes.

1. Exploiting the Wound System & Focus Strikes

  • Phase 1 (Damage Accumulation): Repeatedly attacking specific body parts causes scales to rupture into glowing red wounds.
  • Phase 2 (Focus Strike Finisher): When the Focus reticle turns crimson, press Heavy Attack to trigger cinematic wound-destroying finishers that topple the monster.

2. Dual-Weapon Transitions on Your Seikret Mount

Your Seikret mount carries a secondary weapon holster. Recommended top pairings include:

  1. Great Sword + Light Bowgun: Inflict Sleep or Paralysis from range on your Seikret, then swap into Great Sword for guaranteed True Charged Slash wake-up damage.
  2. Dual Blades + Bow: Utilize hyper-mobile Dual Blades up close and instantly switch to Bow spread shots when the monster takes flight.
