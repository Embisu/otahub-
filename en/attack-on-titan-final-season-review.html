Attack on Titan Final Season Review: A Worthy End for a Legend

The manga's 34-episode journey turned into one of the most controversial yet memorable anime endings in the genre's history.

OtaHub › Reviews › Attack on Titan: Final Season
Reviews 2026-08-15 9 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Attack on Titan: Final Season is the final chapter in the adaptation of Hajime Isayama's popular manga (139 chapters, 34 volumes, completed in April 2021). After WIT Studio handled the first three seasons, MAPPA took over in December 2020 with 16 volumes under the direction of director Yuichiro Hayashi and screenwriter Hiroshi Seko, bringing the story of Eren Yeager and the Eldia-Marley war to a haunting conclusion.

Few anime franchises leave such a deep impression as Attack on Titan. From a shounen series about man-eating giants, Hajime Isayama turned it into a complex political-philosophical story about war, freedom, and the cycle of hatred. Final Season is where all those themes converge.

MAPPA takeover: no small challenge

After the first three seasons produced by WIT Studio with their distinctive visual style, MAPPA's takeover from the end of 2020 was no easy transition. Director Yuichiro Hayashi and screenwriter Hiroshi Seko had to balance maintaining the spirit of the original work while adapting to the new visual style, especially in large-scale action sequences such as the battle at Shiganshina.

Eren Yeager: from hero to villain

The final season is where Eren Yeager's transformation reaches its climax, from a boy yearning for freedom to the central figure in a global tragedy. The way the anime handles this psychological shift, particularly through flashbacks and the multi-faceted perspectives of various characters, is one of the greatest achievements of both the manga and the adaptation.

Heritage of a brand

With 139 manga chapters concluding in April 2021 after years of serialization, Attack on Titan has completed its full adaptation journey from the pages to the screen. Although the ending sparked considerable debate among fans, the series' influence on both the anime industry and global popular culture is undeniable.

  • Total number of Final Season episodes: 16 episodes
  • Studio: MAPPA (from the final season)
  • Director: Yuichiro Hayashi
  • Screenwriter: Hiroshi Seko
  • Original work: Hajime Isayama, 139 chapters/34 episodes
