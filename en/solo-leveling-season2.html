Solo Leveling Season 2: Jeju Island and the Journey to the Top

Jeju Island, where Sung Jinwoo demonstrated his Shadow Monarch power to the whole world and began his journey to confront the forces of the universe.

OtaHub › Anime › Solo Leveling Season 2: Jeju Island and the Journey to Reach
Anime 2026-06-23 7 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Solo Leveling S2 is produced by A-1 Pictures. 13 episodes, broadcast 2025. Main arc: Jeju Island + the appearance of the Monarchs. Cinema quality animation.

The first season of Solo Leveling (A-1 Pictures, 2024) proved that webtoon manhwa can be adapted into anime with top-notch quality. Season 2 not only raised the bar, it completely surpassed it with the Jeju Island arc, which many viewers consider the best anime action sequence in the past 5 years.

Jeju Island Arc, The Battle That Redefined Sung Jinwoo

Jeju Island in the world of Solo Leveling has become a death trap completely overrun by Ant-type monsters. This is the strongest dungeon in the world, where many S-rank Hunter teams have perished. But Jinwoo didn't come to conquer the dungeon; he came to test the limits of Shadow Monarch and absorb the most powerful thing he had ever encountered: Ant King Beru.

The Monarchs Step Out into the Darkness

Season 2 reveals for the first time the force behind it all, The Monarchs, transcendent entities have been using Earth as a battleground for power struggles. The emergence of the Monarch of Frost and the Monarch of Destruction brings a whole new level of threat. This is where Solo Leveling expands from shonen action to cosmic horror.

A-1 Pictures Upgrades Animation to Cinema Level

The scene where Jinwoo summons the entire Shadow Army on Jeju Beach became one of the most shared visual spectacles in the anime community in 2025. A-1 Pictures assembled a special team for Solo Leveling S2, including many animators from the Violet Evergarden project.

Rating: 9.0/10, The Pinnacle of Action Anime

Solo Leveling Season 2 is proof that webtoon anime are not “second rate”, with enough studio talent and dedication, they can compete with any manga adaptation. 9.0/10 is a worthy score.
