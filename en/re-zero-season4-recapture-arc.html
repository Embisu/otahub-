Re:Zero Season 4: Recapture Arc Officially Airs Today

Episode 12 of Re:Zero Season 4 aired today, August 12, opening the 8-episode Recapture Arc. Season 4 will end with a total of 19 episodes divided into 2 courses, ending on September 30.

OtaHub › Anime › Re:Zero Season 4: Recapture Arc Officially Airs Today
Anime 2026-08-12 5 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Episode 12 of Re:Zero Season 4, the opening episode of the 8-episode Recapture Arc, officially airs today, August 12, 2026, on Crunchyroll. Season 4 has a total of 19 episodes, divided into 2 cours, and is expected to conclude on September 30. The anime adaptation is produced by White Fox, and an early screening of the first episode of the season took place at Anime Expo 2026 in early July.

After a break between two courses, Re:Zero kara Hajimeru Isekai Seikatsu Season 4 officially returns today with episode 12, marking the beginning of Recapture Arc, the 8-episode journey is expected to push the storyline of Subaru and his teammates an important step forward after the overwhelming events in the first half of season 4.

Broadcast schedule: 19 episodes, divided into 2 cours

According to the official schedule, the entire Season 4 will last 19 episodes, divided into two broadcast cours. Recapture Arc is the second cour, starting with episode 12 today and expected to end with episode 19, broadcast on 30/9/2026. This is the longest season of the series to date, reflecting the huge amount of content that the team wants to fully adapt from the original light novel instead of shortening it.

Launching early at Anime Expo 2026

Before being broadcast to the general public, the opening episode of Recapture Arc was shown early to audiences at Anime Expo 2026 in early July, within the framework of KADOKAWA's 10th anniversary panel. This is the way studios and publishers often use to create word-of-mouth effects before a new series officially launches widely, and the reaction from the audience at the event was evaluated positively.

Why Recapture Arc is notable

Without going into plot details to avoid spoilers, this is generally the stage where the main characters are forced to directly confront the consequences of their choices in previous arcs, true to the "Return by Death" spirit characteristic of Re:Zero, where every step forward comes at a cost. For fans who have followed since Season 1, this is one of the most anticipated stages of the entire series.

  • Today's episode: Episode 12 (Recapture Arc opening)
  • Arc length: 8 episodes
  • Total number of Season 4 episodes: 19 episodes, 2 courses
  • Season finale: Episode 19, expected September 30, 2026
  • Broadcast platform: Crunchyroll
  • Production studio: White Fox

Where to follow?

Re:Zero Season 4 airs exclusively on Crunchyroll according to weekly simulcast schedule. With season 4 lasting until the end of September, this will be one of the "mainstay" anime throughout the summer and early fall of 2026 that the isekai fan community definitely does not want to miss.
