Wuthering Waves 2.0: The Black Shores Expansion, Shorekeeper & 120 FPS PC Mode

Kuro Games officially reveals Wuthering Waves 2.0 The Black Shores, unveiling 5-star character Shorekeeper, subterranean archipelago regions, and native 120 FPS PC support.

Author: Minh Hoàng & OtaHub Editorial
Date: Sep 03, 2026
Read Time: 6 min read
👁️ 2,130 views
To the Shore's End
Kuro Games has officially broadcasted the full special program for Wuthering Waves Version 2.0 "To the Shore's End", introducing a complete overhaul of the combat system, deep lore, and next-gen PC performance.

1. The Black Shores Archipelago & Tethys Deep

The Black Shores is a mysterious and secluded organization anchored amidst deep ocean currents. Rovers will explore two starkly contrasting layers:

  • Upper Archipelago: Floating islands shrouded in bioluminescent flora with futuristic technology.
  • Tethys Deep Data Space: Subterranean digital dimension holding ancient memories and distorted Tacet Discords.

2. Shorekeeper & Camellya Debut

The highlight of Version 2.0 is Shorekeeper, a 5-star Spectro Rectifier Resonator providing massive team healing, critical damage boosts, and seamless aerial warping.

3. 120 FPS Support & DLSS 3.5 on PC

  • Native 120 FPS: Fully unlocked for high-refresh PC gaming.
  • NVIDIA DLSS 3.5 & Frame Generation: Uncompromising smoothness and responsiveness.
  • Ray Traced Reflections: Realistic ocean water rendering and light reflections.
