Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Movie 1 Smashes Global Box Office Records with Ufotable Visuals

Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Movie 1 achieves history with over $150M global opening box office, spotlighting Ufotable’s boundary-pushing 3D camera cinematography.

🕒 07/09/2026 ✍️ Tik (Khanh Quynh) ⏱️ 6 min read
⚡ Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Movie 1 Key Highlights
  • Record-Breaking Opening: Crosses $150 Million worldwide within its first 3 days across Japan, North America, and key global territories.
  • Mind-Blowing 3D Architecture: Ufotable harnesses zero-gravity dynamic 3D camera tracking and hyper-realistic volumetric lighting inside the Infinity Castle.
  • Tanjiro & Giyu vs. Akaza Showdown: The emotional vengeance for Rengoku delivers a seamless 25-minute sakuga climax.
  • Vietnam & Southeast Asia Release: IMAX and 4DX premium theatrical screenings open late September 2026.

The definitive theatrical anime event of 2026 has arrived as Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba – The Infinity Castle Movie 1 shatters global box-office records across theaters worldwide.

Historic $150 Million Opening Weekend Worldwide

According to box-office tracking by Toho and Aniplex, the first installment of the Infinity Castle trilogy earned a staggering $150 Million within its first 72 hours. The film comfortably eclipsed the previous milestone set by Mugen Train in 2020, solidifying Demon Slayer as an unprecedented cinematic phenomenon.

Ufotable’s Animation Masterclass: Zero-Gravity Architectural Sakuga

Critics and fans universally lauded Ufotable for pushing cinematic boundaries. The shifting, gravity-defying architecture of the Infinity Castle features millions of rotating Shoji panels rendered in breathtaking 3D environments, seamlessly intertwined with hand-drawn 2D combat animation.

The pivotal 25-minute duel featuring Tanjiro Kamado and Giyu Tomioka against Upper Rank Three Akaza represents the pinnacle of swordplay choreography, enhanced by 360-degree rotational camera movements and volumetric Hinokami Kagura flame effects.
