WIT Studio Reveals the Mystery of Attack on Titan on August 20

WIT Studio released teaser images with 6 Attack on Titan characters, scheduled to be officially announced on August 20, 2026. Mikasa wears a strange eye patch, Eren holds chopsticks and plays with noodles during a "special dinner".

Home › Anime › WIT Studio Reveals the Mystery of Attack on Titan August 20
Anime 2026-08-15 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
WIT Studio, the original studio that adapted the first 3 seasons Attack on Titan, suddenly released a mysterious teaser image gathering 6 key characters of the series, along with the text scheduled to be announced on 20/8/2026. Image details show that this is most likely not a new anime season, but a collaboration project or other product.

Three years after ending with the compilation movie THE LAST ATTACK, Attack on Titan suddenly returned to the center of attention when WIT Studio, the studio that adapted the first three anime seasons before MAPPA took over, officially posted a mysterious teaser image on its official X account, with the message: "It looks like they, during 'that period', will be holding a special dinner."

Six characters, one mysterious dinner

The teaser image brings together the full cast of characters most loved by fans: Eren Jaeger, Mikasa Ackerman, Armin Arlert, Levi Ackerman, Erwin Smith and Hange Zoe, along with the text "20/8/2026" displayed in the bottom corner of the image. Notably, observers quickly spotted unusual details: Mikasa is wearing an eye patch, something she never wore throughout the entire original series, while Eren is depicted playing with chopsticks as if toying with a bowl of noodles. These unconventional details further reinforce the notion that the upcoming project is not a new anime season, but more likely a collaborative product line, possibly related to food or special merchandise.

Context: an eventful year for the Attack on Titan legacy

This unexpected return comes right after a series of celebratory activities throughout 2026: MAPPA just released a special illustration for Attack on Titan to celebrate the studio's 15th anniversary, and also confirmed a new opening animation created by MAPPA itself for the game. Attack on Titan 3 coming soon. Manufacturer Tetsuya Kinoshita also opened up about new film projects and collaborations in the brand's future.

  • Expected announcement date: 20/8/2026
  • Announcing unit: WIT Studio (original studio season 1-3)
  • Characters appearing: Eren, Mikasa, Armin, Levi, Erwin, Hange
  • Original work: Hajime Isayama (Kodansha, Bessatsu Shounen Magazine)
  • Recent background: Movie THE LAST ATTACK surpasses 1 billion yen in revenue, Attack on Titan 3 (game) is coming soon

Meaning: the brand still has room to exploit

Although the manga ended in 2021 and the anime concluded with the Final Chapters and a compilation movie, Attack on Titan continues to demonstrate immense commercial and cultural appeal for many years after its conclusion. The fact that WIT Studio, the name associated with some of the series' most highly-rated anime seasons, is announcing a new project, even if its nature is unclear, will undoubtedly keep the global fan community closely watching until August 20th.
