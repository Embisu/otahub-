Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium – Beginner's Guide, Reroll Tier List & Top 5 SSR Squad Comps

Complete tactical guide for Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium: Fast 10-min reroll route, Stability & Cover mechanics breakdown, and top 5 meta SSR doll team comps.

🕒 Sep 07, 2026 ✍️ Lin (Khanh Linh) ⏱️ 7 min read
⚡ Key Beginner Takeaways
  • Fast Reroll Target: Aim for Tololo (Top Tier Single-Target DPS) or Qiongjiu (Follow-up Attack Queen) on the discounted beginner banner.
  • Stability System: Break enemy Stability gauges before unleashing ultimate skills for maximum multiplier bonus.
  • 3D Cover Rules: End doll movement behind Full Cover tiles to reduce incoming ranged damage by 60% and prevent critical hits.
  • Top 5 Meta Comps: Tololo Hypercarry, Qiongjiu-Sharkry Follow-up, Nemesis Long-range Sniper, Groza Stability Breaker, and Starter F2P Friendly squad.

1. 3D Tactical Combat & Cover Mechanics Breakdown

Marking a massive evolutionary leap from its 2D predecessor, Girls' Frontline 2: Exilium introduces deeply strategic, turn-based 3D tactical combat inspired by XCOM. As the Commander, players command elite squads of Tactical Dolls across hazardous Yellow Zones.

Mastering two fundamental pillars is essential for success in high-difficulty operations:

  • Stability Gauge: Every doll and enemy unit possesses a Stability bar. When reduced to zero (Guard Break), the target is stunned, loses retaliatory Overwatch capability, and takes 40% increased damage from all subsequent attacks.
  • Cover System: Battlefields feature three terrain states: Open (No Cover), Half Cover (30% damage reduction), and Full Cover (60% damage reduction & critical strike immunity). Always prioritize finishing moves behind Full Cover barriers.

2. Fast Reroll Strategy & SSR Doll Tier List

New commanders receive 20 discounted Beginner Gacha pulls and 10 Special Banner tickets upon clearing Chapter 1-4. A full reroll cycle takes less than 10 minutes by skipping non-combat dialogue cutscenes.

3. Top 5 Meta Squad Compositions

Here are the 5 highest performing team compositions verified by OtaHub tactical editors:

  1. Hypercarry Burst (Tololo + Qiongjiu + Colphne + Krolik): Maximizes actions per turn to obliterate bosses within the first two phases.
  2. Thermal Ignite (Sharkry + Qiongjiu + Sabrina + Nemesis): Stacks persistent burn procs to steadily strip boss Stability gauges every turn.
  3. Overwatch Sniper Fortress (Nemesis + Tololo + Cheeta + Ksenia): Controls line of sight from maximum distance, picking off advancing foes instantly.
  4. Armor Shred Blitz (Groza + Vepley + Krolik + Colphne): Designed specifically to dismantle heavily shielded mechanical units in late-game stages.
  5. F2P Starter Friendly (Groza + 4-Star Nemesis + Colphne + Cheeta): 100% free-to-play squad capable of effortlessly conquering Chapter 4.

4. Resource Allocation & Dormitory Affection Tips

Do not waste rare weapon enhancement chips on low-tier gear. Instead, invest daily stamina in the interactive 3D Doll Dormitory to raise Affection levels. Each milestone unlocks permanent 5% stat boosts and exclusive lore scenarios.
