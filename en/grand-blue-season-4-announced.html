Grand Blue Dreaming Officially Returns for Season 2 After 6-Year Hiatus: Original Cast & Director Confirmed

After a 6-year hiatus, comedy diving anime Grand Blue Dreaming officially announces Season 2 with the original voice cast and director returning.

Home › Anime › Grand Blue Dreaming Season 2
Anime 2026-09-26 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Grand Blue Dreaming — the beloved college comedy anime centered on scuba diving and relentless frat-style shenanigans — has officially confirmed production on Season 2 after a six-year hiatus. Director Shinji Takamatsu returns alongside studios Zero-G and Liber, with the entire original star-studded voice cast reprising their iconic roles in the Peek a Boo diving club.

Following more than six years of eager anticipation since its debut season concluded in September 2018, hit comedy anime Grand Blue Dreaming (known simply in Japan as ぐらんぶる - Grand Blue) has officially greenlit its long-awaited Season 2. The announcement was unveiled during a special 10th-anniversary celebration of the original manga series, igniting worldwide excitement across the anime community.

Adapted from the critically acclaimed manga penned by Kenji Inoue and illustrated by Kimitake Yoshioka, Grand Blue Dreaming is celebrated as one of the definitive college comedies in modern Japanese pop culture. The announcement arrived paired with a dynamic teaser visual, reuniting fans with the sun-drenched coast of Izu and the eccentric members of the Peek a Boo (PaB) diving club.

The Long-Awaited College Reunion: Peek a Boo Rides Again

Since the first 12-episode season wrapped in 2018, fans had persistently campaigned for a follow-up. The series follows college freshman Iori Kitahara as he relocates to the coastal town of Izu to attend university, lodging at his uncle’s dive shop, "Grand Blue." Expecting an idyllic, seaside romantic comedy full of beautiful coeds, Iori is instead drafted by force into the wildly energetic Peek a Boo scuba diving club — a fraternity of muscular upperclassmen whose passion for diving is rivaled only by their thirst for flammable "Oolong tea" drinking contests.

What elevates Grand Blue Dreaming above generic slapstick is its deft narrative balance. Beneath the outrageous, hyper-expressive character comedy lies an authentic reverence for marine exploration, diving safety, and the genuine camaraderie formed during university life.

Director Shinji Takamatsu and Original Star Cast Return

The most reassuring revelation for veteran fans is the return of the key creative leadership. Renowned director Shinji Takamatsu (lauded for his comedy mastery on Gintama and Daily Lives of High School Boys) will once again direct and oversee series script composition.

Animation duties will see a collaborative co-production between Zero-G (the original studio behind Season 1) and Studio Liber, promising enhanced underwater visuals, lush coastal lighting, and fluid physical comedy choreography.

The entire beloved Japanese voice cast has officially confirmed their return:

  • Yuma Uchida as the beleaguered protagonist Iori Kitahara.
  • Ryohei Kimura as the hardcore otaku best friend Kohei Imamura.
  • Chika Anzai as the sharp-tongued diving prodigy Chisa Kotegawa.
  • Katsuyuki Konishi and Hiroki Yasumoto reprising the heavyweight senior duo Shinji Tokita and Ryujiro Kotegawa.
  • Maaya Uchida as the doting yet terrifying elder sister Nanaka Kotegawa.
  • Toa Yukinari as charismatic senior Azusa Hamaoka and Kana Asumi as feisty classmate Aina Yoshiwara.

Over 9 Million Manga Copies in Circulation

Serialized in Kodansha's monthly Good! Afternoon magazine since April 2014, Grand Blue has surpassed 22 collected tankobon volumes and boasts over 9 million copies in circulation worldwide. In 2020, the franchise also spawned a successful live-action feature film.

With production underway and the original creative engine back in place, Season 2 is primed to be one of the most anticipated anime comedy events of the coming broadcast cycle.

Sources: Comic Natalie, Anime News Network, Avex Pictures, Kodansha Official.
