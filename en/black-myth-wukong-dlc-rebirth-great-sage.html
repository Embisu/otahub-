Black Myth: Wukong Kỷ Niệm 2 Năm, Game Science Chính Thức Công Bố DLC Cốt Truyện 'Rebirth of the Great Sage'

Nhân dịp kỷ niệm 2 năm ra mắt kiệt tác Black Myth: Wukong, Game Science công bố DLC cốt truyện Rebirth of the Great Sage trên Unreal Engine 5.5.

OtaHub › Gaming › Black Myth: Wukong Kỷ Niệm 2 Năm, Game Science Chính Thức Công Bố DLC Cốt Truyện 'Rebirth of the Great Sage'
Gaming 2026-08-22 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Celebrating the 2-year anniversary of Black Myth: Wukong, Game Science surprised fans at Gamescom 2026 by unveiling the teaser trailer for its first major story DLC, Rebirth of the Great Sage. Upgraded with Unreal Engine 5.5, the expansion takes players directly into the Heavenly Realm and launches in early 2027.

Marking the exact 2-year anniversary of Black Myth: Wukong (August 20, 2024 – August 20, 2026), developer Game Science ignited the Gamescom 2026 stage by officially revealing its first major story expansion: Rebirth of the Great Sage.

Storming the Heavenly Realm in Sun Wukong's Prime

Unlike the base game's journey of the Destined One reclaiming the Six Relics, Rebirth of the Great Sage transports players into the golden era of Sun Wukong, waging war against the Heavenly Court.

The new map spans the Southern Heavenly Gate, Lingxiao Palace, and Northern Kuru continent. Players will directly clash with mythological demigods, the Four Heavenly Kings in their prime, and celestial generals with formidable multi-phase combat mechanics.

New Combat Stances & Expanded Transformations

Game Science introduces two new staff combat stances alongside the original three, accompanied by expanded transformation spells granting distinct celestial movesets.

Unreal Engine 5.5 Graphical Upgrades

Built upon the latest Unreal Engine 5.5, the DLC enhances Lumen path tracing and Nanite geometric detail for ethereal celestial lighting across PC and PlayStation 5.

  • Title: Black Myth: Wukong – Rebirth of the Great Sage
  • Developer & Publisher: Game Science
  • Genre: Action RPG / Souls-like
  • Platforms: PC (Steam, Epic) & PlayStation 5
  • Release Window: Q1 2027
