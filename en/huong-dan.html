01 Need to Know Before Playing ▼

Elden Ring: Nightreign is the standalone version of Elden Ring, focusing on co-op roguelite gameplay. Each "run" lasts about 40 minutes over 3 in-game days, ending with an extremely difficult Nightlord boss.

💡 Tips

If this is your first FromSoftware game, start with Class Hero (Warrior), simplest, balanced stats, easiest to learn mechanism.

02 Combat Mechanics ▼

Nightreign combines classic Souls-like combat with roguelite mechanics. Each run you pick up Relics (items that temporarily increase your power) and build in a random direction.

Relic System

Relic is the soul of Nightreign. Each Relic has a rarity from Common → Rare → Legendary and creates synergy with each other. Prioritize synergy stacks instead of choosing single strong Relics.

⚠️ Warning

Don't pick Relic randomly without a plan. Conflict synergy will make your build weaker than playing without Relic.

Combat Fundamentals

Dodge timing, pose breaking, and positional play are the three pillars. Master dodge first, everything else can be learned later.

💡 Advanced techniques

Poise Break Combo: Use Heavy Attack → Charged Attack to stagger the enemy. Then perform Critical Strike (R1 when the enemy falls) for maximum damage.

04 Boss & Strategy ▼

Nightreign has 8 main Nightlord bosses and dozens of secondary field bosses. Below are the most important bosses and how to approach them.

☽ Nightlord Gael
💀 Very difficult
Boss at the end of the game. There are 3 phases, each phase changes the attack pattern completely. Prioritize dodging everything, not blocking Phase 3.
Weaknesses: Holy damage, backstab window
🔥 Furnace Golem
⚔️ Average
Climb your feet to avoid the AOE stomp. Focus hit the knee as it stopped. Avoid standing frontally.
Weaknesses: Fire resistance → use Lightning
🐺 Revenant Wolf
✅ Relatively easy
Pattern is easy to read. Dodge to the left when it jumps. Punish with 2–3 Heavy Attacks after each of its combos.
Weaknesses: Bleed, stance break
🌊 Tidal Wyrm
💀 Difficult
Do not stand in water, AOE will 1-shot. Move continuously on dry platforms. Hit the fish's gills.
Weaknesses: Lightning damage ×2
🚨 Dangerous

Boss Nightlord Gael Phase 3 Has instant-kill attack if HP is below 40%. Always keep Flask and use Flask as soon as HP drops to 50%.

07 Tips & Tricks ▼

10 Tips Newbies Need to Know

  • Always burn the first Site of Grace you encounter to open a respawn point
  • Don't miss NPC dialogue, many free items and hidden quests
  • Torrent (horse) is 3 times faster than running, use as much as possible
  • Coop with stronger people to learn boss attack patterns for free
  • Day 3 Night Phase is extremely dangerous, complete your build before entering
  • Merchant Roundtable sells Smithing Stone every run, buy all before entering
  • Parry only works with humanoid enemies, not big bosses
  • Stack with type Relic effect to trigger bonus threshold
  • Boss field (non-Nightlord) drops Relic rare better than regular chests
  • Ping teammates when seeing Legendary Relic, decide which one is more suitable to pick
📝 Pro tip

After clearing a run, watch the replay to analyze when unnecessary damage occurred. Nightreign has a built-in replay system.
