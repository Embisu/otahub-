Lies of P: Complete Edition Now Available on Nintendo Switch 2

Neowiz and Round8 Studio bring the full Lies of P experience, including the Overture DLC, to Switch 2 with hardware-specific performance optimizations.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-20 · 5 min read

In short: Neowiz and Round8 Studio have released Lies of P: Complete Edition on Nintendo Switch 2 from August 6, 2026, bundling the base game with the Overture DLC for $69.99, with a physical release on October 2, 2026.

Neowiz and Round8 Studio have officially released Lies of P: Complete Edition on Nintendo Switch 2 as of August 6, 2026, marking the first time this Pinocchio-inspired soulslike has appeared on a Nintendo platform.

The Complete Edition bundles the original Lies of P (2023) with the full Overture expansion, which previously released as a separate DLC. The Switch 2 version has been optimized for the hardware and packs in every post-launch quality-of-life update, along with new difficulty and challenge modes. A day one patch added a dedicated performance mode for handheld play.

The digital edition is priced at $69.99. A physical release is planned for October 2, 2026 through publisher iam8bit, shipping the full game on a cartridge with no additional download required. The special iam8bit Edition will include the complete Overture DLC along with a set of six collectible Boss Lore Cards.
