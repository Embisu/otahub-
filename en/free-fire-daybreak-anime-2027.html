Free Fire Daybreak Reveals Key Visual, Sets Spring 2027 Debut

Garena and KADOKAWA have officially named the Free Fire anime adaptation Free Fire Daybreak, unveiling its first key visual for a global spring 2027 premiere.

Home › Anime › Free Fire Daybreak Reveals Key Visual, Sets Spring 2027 Debut
Anime 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
In short: Free Fire Daybreak, the anime adaptation of the Free Fire game co-produced by Garena and KADOKAWA, has revealed its first key visual featuring Kelly, Hayato and Moco. The anime is produced by studio CANDYBOX and directed by Ken Takahashi, premiering worldwide in spring 2027.

Garena and KADOKAWA have officially named the anime adaptation of Free Fire Free Fire Daybreak, unveiling its first key visual and confirming a global premiere in spring 2027.

The key visual introduces the main cast: Kelly, Hayato and Moco. Kelly appears amid shattered glass fragments reflecting an image of herself after awakening a special ability, hinting at part of the story to come.

The project is produced by studio CANDYBOX, directed by Ken Takahashi, with Hotaru Asafuji handling series composition and screenplay, and Kouji Fujimoto of Sus4 Inc. composing the music. Free Fire Daybreak is a joint investment between Garena and KADOKAWA and will air simultaneously in Japan and worldwide.
