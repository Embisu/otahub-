Link Click Season 3 Part 1 Officially Aired Today

Link Click Season 3 Part 1 officially airs today August 14, 2026 on Bilibili and Crunchyroll. The new season revolves around the disappearance of Ha Phi, marking the return of Vein and the awakening power of Kieu Linh.

Link Click (Time Travel) Season 3 Part 1 officially airs today, August 14, 2026, on Bilibili and Crunchyroll. Part 1 consists of 12 episodes, out of a total of 24 episodes divided into two parts of season 3, produced by LAN Studio. The plot revolves around the mysterious disappearance of Xia Fei, drawing Cheng Xiaoshi and Lu Guang back into the dangerous vortex of time travel.

After the necessary silence to "cool down" after the Ly Thien Than incident, Link Click, the Chinese animated series (donghua) that made a big splash thanks to its emotional time travel plot, officially returns with Season 3 Part 1, airs today on Bilibili at 11:00 China time, and streams on Crunchyroll for a global audience.

Structure of season 3: divided into two parts, total 24 episodes

Unlike the previous two seasons, which aired continuously, this season 3 is divided into split-cour: Part 1 consists of 12 episodes starting today, while Part 2 is expected to air in 2027. In total, season 3 will have 24 episodes, double the average length of previous seasons, showing the ambition of the crew to tell a larger-scale story. LAN Studio.

Plot: the mystery of Ha Phi's disappearance

After the Ly Thien Than incident ended, Time Photo Studio seemed to return to its peaceful daily life. But news about the Ha Phi's disappearance once again pushing everyone into a fateful crossroads. Along with the return of Vein and the awakening power of Kieu Linh, the two main characters Trinh Tieu Thi and Luc Quang gradually uncover the secret behind the Bahati fire, one of the big knots planted in previous seasons.

For new viewers, Link Click follows a couple who run Time Photo Studio, a small photo shop in the heart of a modern city, where every photo order can lead to dangerous time travel adventures. The series is renowned for its refined character development and emotional twists, making it one of the most highly regarded donghua in the international anime community.

  • Air date Part 1: 14/8/2026
  • Number of episodes Part 1: 12 episodes
  • Total number of episodes season 3: 24 episodes (2 parts)
  • Part 2: Expected 2027
  • Production studio: LAN Studio
  • Platform: Bilibili (original), Crunchyroll (international)

Why is it worth watching?

Link Click is one of the few donghua that truly captivated international audiences thanks to its storytelling quality, not just its beautiful visuals. With season 3 doubling in scale and promising to resolve unfinished mysteries from season 2, it's definitely the most noteworthy anime to watch in the summer 2026 anime season for fans of time travel and psychological drama.
