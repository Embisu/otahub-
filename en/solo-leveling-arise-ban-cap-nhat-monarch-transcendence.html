Milestone & Update Highlights
Netmarble has announced that action RPG Solo Leveling: Arise has surpassed 50,000,000 global downloads. Celebrating with Monarch Transcendence for Sung Jinwoo and 100 Custom Draw Tickets.

Holding the #1 grossing mobile rank across 80+ countries, Solo Leveling: Arise delivers ultimate shadow monarch powerups.

Unlock Divine Shadow Extraction, Monarch Domain, and Ashborn Robes granting massive HP and critical strike multipliers.

Co-op with 4 hunters against mutated Ant King Beru and claim 100 Custom Draw Tickets + SSR Hunter Cha Hae-In.
