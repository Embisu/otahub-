Senren Banka Gets Anime Adaptation, 2027 Premiere

Yuzusoft confirms an anime adaptation of its visual novel Senren＊Banka for 2027, with the original voice cast reprising their roles and KOTOKO performing the opening theme.

Home › Anime › Senren Banka Gets Anime Adaptation, 2027 Premiere
Anime 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
In short: Yuzusoft confirms an anime adaptation of its visual novel Senren＊Banka for 2027, with the original voice cast reprising their roles and KOTOKO performing the opening theme.

Yuzusoft confirmed at Yuzusoft SONGFES 2026 on July 26 that its visual novel Senren＊Banka will be adapted into an anime, expected to premiere in 2027. It is the first anime adaptation of a game from the company.

What has been confirmed?

  • Release year: 2027; no specific date or season yet.
  • Cast: Sora Haruka (Yoshino), Yuki Kotorii (Mako), Mikan Sato (Murasame) and Sawa Sawawa (Lena) return.
  • Music: KOTOKO performs the opening theme.
  • Not yet announced: studio, director, episode count, international streaming platform and age rating.

What is Senren＊Banka about?

The story takes place in Hoori, a mountain village that still keeps ancient customs. Masaomi Arichi accidentally breaks the divine sword Murasamemaru and is asked to become engaged to the shrine maiden Yoshino Tomotake to make amends. The romantic-comedy premise gradually opens up a mystery about a curse, the sword spirit Murasame and the history of the village.

The visual novel was released in Japan in 2016, and an English version was later published by NekoNyan. The original is built around multiple character routes, meaning the player’s choices lead to different stories and endings.

The challenge of adapting a multi-route visual novel

The anime has to choose between a single central route or combining the shared events before heading to a conclusion. Yoshino is tied directly to the engagement and the traditions of Hoori, while Murasame holds the key to the supernatural side. How the writers balance these two routes will decide whether the project keeps the main mystery while still giving Mako and Lena enough screen time.

Newcomers do not have to play the game first, because the premise is self-contained enough for the anime to introduce it from the start. Fans of the original, however, will care most about which route is chosen and how well the romantic-comedy tone, the local culture and the supernatural elements are preserved.

A note on the original’s content

The original game is a visual novel for adults. That does not automatically mean the anime will keep the game’s content or rating. Until the producers release a trailer, a broadcast slot and a rating, there is no reason to assume the anime is either 18+ content or toned down for a general audience.

OtaHub’s take: the project’s biggest advantage is its familiar cast and a music catalog that already has an identity. The risk lies in compressing a long, branching story into a limited number of TV episodes. The first trailer and the studio’s name will be the two most important pieces of information for judging the quality of the adaptation.

Source: Yuzusoft and AUTOMATON WEST. Updated September 30, 2026.
