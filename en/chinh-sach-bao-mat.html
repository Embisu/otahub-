Brief summary: OtaHub is a free news site. We collect anonymous analytics data to improve the experience. We do not sell your personal data. You can turn off cookies at any time.

01 Information we collect

OtaHub collects the following types of information when you use the website:

  • Anonymous usage data: Pages viewed, length of stay, device and browser, via Google Analytics 4. Not linked to personal identities.
  • Email (voluntary): When you sign up for the newsletter or contact via form. Only used to send content you request.
  • Contact form data: Full name, email, content sent via contact page, temporarily saved to respond to requests.
  • Technical cookies: Save language preferences (VI/EN), no personal information.

We do not collect: passwords, payment information, phone numbers, physical addresses, or sensitive data.

02 How we use information
  • Analyze which articles are most read to improve content
  • Send newsletter to subscribers (can cancel at any time)
  • Respond to your requests for contact and cooperation
  • Detect and prevent unusual access, protect the website
  • Improve page loading speed and user experience
03 Cookies and tracking technology

OtaHub uses the following types of cookies:

  • Necessary cookies: Save UI state (menu, language). Cannot be disabled because it is needed for the site to function.
  • Analytical cookies (Google Analytics): Collect anonymous data about page views. You can turn it off Google Analytics Opt-out.
  • Advertising cookies: If Google AdSense is displayed, Google will use cookies to display relevant ads. See Google policy for more.

You can delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

04 Sharing information with third parties

OtaHub Not for sale, not for rent your personal data. We only share data with:

  • Google LLC: Analytics and AdSense, anonymized data according to Google's terms
  • Cloudflare Inc.: CDN and website security, see Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Sanity.io: Content management system, does not process reader data
  • Formspree: Handling contact forms and temporary emails to forward feedback

In case required by law, we may have to provide information to competent authorities in Vietnam.

05 Google Analytics & Advertising

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure traffic anonymously. IP is anonymized. You can refuse at: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

If the website is visible Google AdSense, Google may use the DoubleClick cookie to display ads based on browsing history. You can manage your advertising preferences at: adssettings.google.com.

06 Data security

We employ industry standard security measures:

  • HTTPS/TLS for entire connection (via Cloudflare SSL)
  • Do not save passwords or payment information
  • Restrict internal access to user data
  • Cloudflare WAF protects against DDoS and injection attacks

However, no system is 100% secure. If we discover a security breach affecting your data, we will notify you within 72 hours.

07 Your rights

According to Vietnam Cyber Information Security Law and GDPR (applicable to EU users), you have the right to:

  • See personal data we hold about you
  • Delete email from the newsletter list at any time (unsubscribe link in each email)
  • Request deletion contact form data, send email to hi@otahub.asia
  • Turn off cookies in your browser settings
08 Policy changes

We may update this policy when there are changes to our services or the law. Important changes will be notified via newsletter (if you have registered) or banner on the home page.
