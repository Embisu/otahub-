Wuthering Waves 3.6: Ren Realm, Sword Flight, And Two New Characters Qingxiao And Jingran

Wuthering Waves version 3.6 'Lamplight in Mirage, Sword's Resolve in Heart' launches August 20, 2026, opening up the new Ren Realm area, the Sword Flight traversal mechanic, playable musical instruments in the open world, and two new Resonators, Qingxiao and Jingran.

Summary
Wuthering Waves 3.6 launches August 20, 2026. New area: Ren Realm. New features: Sword Flight traversal and playable open-world musical instruments. New characters: Qingxiao (Aero, main DPS, Phase 1 alongside a Denia rerun) and Jingran (Phase 2 alongside Hiyuki and Mornye reruns). Events: Resonance Sim Realm and The Strings Remember.

Kuro Games continues expanding the world of Solaris-3 with Wuthering Waves version 3.6, titled Lamplight in Mirage, Sword's Resolve in Heart, launching August 20, 2026. This update focuses on a new explorable area and a fresh set of traversal mechanics, alongside two new Resonators joining the roster.

Ren Realm, A New Area To Explore

Ren Realm is the new region introduced in 3.6, arriving alongside a brand-new traversal mechanic called Sword Flight, letting the Rover move around the terrain with far more freedom. Notably, this version also lets players perform on musical instruments directly in the open world, an environmental interaction never seen in previous updates.

Qingxiao And Jingran, Two New Resonators

Qingxiao is an Aero sword-wielding Resonator who serves as a main DPS, with a kit built around Tune Strain and stacking Mindlock to amplify her own damage. She arrives in the Phase 1 banner alongside a rerun for Denia. In Phase 2, Jingran (nicknamed the "Cat of the Nether Lamp") launches alongside reruns for Hiyuki and Mornye.

Events: Resonance Sim Realm And The Strings Remember

Beyond the main content, 3.6 brings a packed event lineup led by Resonance Sim Realm and The Strings Remember, both tied to the musical theme running throughout this update.

With an expanded map in Ren Realm, the new Sword Flight mechanic, and two notable Resonators, Wuthering Waves 3.6 continues to show Kuro Games keeping up a steady update pace to retain both longtime and new players alike.
