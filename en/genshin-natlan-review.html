Genshin Impact Natlan: Land of Fire and the Secret of Celestia

Natlan isn't just a new region, it's where Genshin Impact begins to answer the questions players have been asking since day one.

Reviews2026-06-2310 minutes to readOtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Genshin Impact Natlan releases patch 5.0 in August 2024. New element: Phlogiston (Pyro evolution). Characters: Xilonen, Chasca, Ifa, Mavuika. The plot reveals secrets about Celestia and the Sustainer.

After Fontaine completely surprised everyone with one of the best storylines in Genshin history (and possibly in gacha in general), expectations for Natlan were extremely high. HoYoverse not only met them, they surpassed them in a completely different way, because Natlan was unlike any region before.

Natlan, Land of Eternal War

Unlike Mondstadt or Liyue, Natlan is not a peaceful place under threat; it is a land that has fought for thousands of years against "the Abyss" and has never ceased. Natlan's entire culture, politics, and society are built around war: the Melusines fight for good, the Tribes possess special Pyro abilities, and the Pyro Archon, Xianyun, protects the land with an unquenchable flame.

Phlogiston, a New Mechanism that Changes Experience

Natlan introduced Phlogiston, a special energy source of the region that allows players to interact with the environment in a completely new way. Characters can "consume" Phlogiston to dash, climb faster, and activate special Natlan mechanics. This is the biggest gameplay change since Fontaine introduced underwater exploration, and it makes moving around Natlan incredibly satisfying.

Revelations About Celestia and the Sustainer

This is the part most fans have been waiting for, and Natlan delivers. Natlan's Arch Quests reveal important clues about Sustainer of Heavenly Principles (the entity that killed the old Archon of Liyue and Mondstadt), the true relationship between Celestia and Teyvat, and the question of why the Traveler came here. These revelations don't end the arc, they're big puzzle pieces set before the finale in Snezhnaya.

Outstanding Characters: Mavuika and Xilonen

Mavuika, the new Archon Pyro, was warmly received by players with its powerful Pyro/Physical hybrid combat kit and emotional backstory. Xilonen, the legendary blacksmith of Natlan, is one of the most useful support units Genshin has ever had. Both represent a HoYoverse that is increasingly focusing on characters being good in both story and gameplay.

Score: 9.3/10, Best Region in Genshin

Natlan is the pinnacle of Genshin Impact, not because it's perfect in every way, but because it succeeds in being both a great experience right from the start and laying the foundation for the finale of the entire Genshin Impact franchise. 9.3/10 and the only region I recommend even those who quit the game in 2022 to return and experience.
