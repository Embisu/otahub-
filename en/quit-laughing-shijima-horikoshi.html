The Creator Of My Hero Academia Returns To Jump With A New Horror One-shot

Kohei Horikoshi officially returns to Weekly Shounen Jump after 2 years with the 61-page horror one-shot "Quit Laughing, Shijima", a "boy-meets-girl horror" story about a strange town hiding a dark secret.

OtaHub › Manga › Quit Laughing, Shijima
Manga 2026-08-15 5 minutes reading OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Kohei Horikoshi, father My Hero Academia, officially returned Weekly Shounen Jump After 2 years of absence with a 61-page horror one-shot titled "Quit Laughing, Shijima" (Warawanaide yo Shijima-san), published in double issue 37/38 in Japan on 10/8/2026 and is also available on MANGA Plus by Shueisha.

Two years after ending his 10-year journey My Hero Academia, the series has reached more than 100 million copies in global circulation and has become one of the pillars of Weekly Shounen Jump, author Kohei Horikoshi officially marks the return to a completely different genre: "boy-meets-girl" horror.

Quit Laughing, Shijima: the secret behind the smile

A 61-page one-shot, including a color page in the middle, is briefly described by VIZ as: \"Everyone in this strange town seems to admire Shijima, but hidden beneath that is a dark secret...\" This is a notable shift for Horikoshi, from the familiar colorful superhero genre to the dark, chilling atmosphere of psychological horror.

Returning to Jump for the first time since My Hero Academia ended

This is Horikoshi's first manga work to be serialized in Weekly Shounen Jump since My Hero Academia concluded in August 2024. For a decade, My Hero Academia not only launched Horikoshi's career but also inspired numerous anime seasons, theatrical films, games, and spin-off projects. A new anime focusing on the character Eri, a spin-off of the series, also aired just the week before this new one-shot was released, showing that the My Hero Academia legacy continues to be explored alongside the author's new direction.

  • Original title: Warawanaide yo Shijima-san
  • Number of pages: 61 pages, including color pages in the middle of the story
  • Category: "boy-meets-girl" horror
  • Release date: August 10, 2026 (Weekly Shounen Jump No. 37/38)
  • Read at: MANGA Plus by Shueisha

Same issue: KochiKame 50th anniversary

In the same issue as Horikoshi's one-shot debut, Weekly Shounen Jump also devoted a special commemorative section 50 years classic series KochiKame: Tokyo Beat Cops by Osamu Akimoto, with supervision from horror novelist Settsu, author of "About a Certain Place in the Kinki Region" and "The Mouth Survey," shows Jump is having a particularly noteworthy week for both traditional shonen and horror fans.
