Kagurabachi Passes 4 Million Copies, Anime April 2027

Takeru Hokazono’s Kagurabachi passed 4 million copies in circulation by April 2026; the anime by Cypic premieres in April 2027.

Home › Manga › Kagurabachi Passes 4 Million Copies, Anime April 2027
Manga 2026-09-19 3 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Takeru Hokazono’s Kagurabachi passed 4 million copies in circulation by April 2026; the anime by Cypic premieres in April 2027.

Kagurabachi will be adapted into a TV anime in April 2027, produced by studio Cypic. The official announcement also confirmed that Takeru Hokazono's manga has passed 4 million copies in circulation, digital included, with the release of volume 11 in Japan. The project has Tetsuya Takeuchi as director and Taihi Kimura voicing Chihiro Rokuhira.

What has been confirmed about the Kagurabachi anime

  • Broadcast and streaming: April 2027; no specific date announced.
  • Studio: Cypic, an animation company under the CyberAgent group.
  • Director: Tetsuya Takeuchi.
  • Character design: Keigo Sasaki.
  • Chihiro Rokuhira: Taihi Kimura.
  • Production committee leads: Shochiku and CyberAgent.

CyberAgent's press release says the project will be broadcast and streamed worldwide with MUSE, SMG Holdings and other partners. The official sources do not currently list Crunchyroll, so it is too early to state that the service holds global rights until a separate announcement is made.

What is Kagurabachi about?

Chihiro once trained as a swordsmith under his father, Kunishige Rokuhira. His peaceful life ends when the sorcerer organization Hishaku murders Kunishige and steals six Enchanted Blades, swords with enough power to affect the fate of an entire nation. Chihiro takes up Enten, the seventh blade his father left behind, and sets out to take revenge and recover the stolen weapons.

The revenge premise is only the outer layer. The manga also asks about the responsibility of those who make weapons, the consequences of war, and how much of his humanity Chihiro can hold on to. Its combat system combines swordsmanship with the unique supernatural ability of each Enchanted Blade, giving the anime room to show off movement, water effects and the strong contrasts of the original artwork.

What does the 4 million milestone mean?

Kagurabachi began running in Weekly Shonen Jump in September 2023. According to a press release dated April 27, 2026, total circulation would pass 4 million copies when volume 11 came out on May 1, counting digital copies. “Copies in circulation” does not mean every book has been sold to readers, but it is still an indicator of the scale of the print run and the expectations the publisher has for the work.

The series won first place in the print manga category at the Next Manga Awards 2024. Its track record and international readership position the anime as a global project from the moment of the announcement, rather than waiting for reactions after a domestic broadcast.

What is the Kagurabachi World Tour?

From summer 2026, the production team has been holding preview screenings of the first 20 minutes of episode 1 at anime events in several countries. The final stop in Japan is planned for spring 2027 and will screen the full first episode for the first time ahead of its TV broadcast. This is a promotional preview event; it does not mean episode 1 has been released widely.

Do newcomers need to read the manga first?

Not necessarily, since the anime will start from Chihiro's origins. Reading the manga first suits those who want to understand how Hokazono lays out the sword fights and to check how faithful the adaptation is. If you would rather wait for the anime, just keep three things in mind: Chihiro, the Hishaku organization and the seven Enchanted Blades. The episode count, the day-by-day broadcast schedule and the platform in Vietnam have not yet been confirmed.

Official sources: Kagurabachi Anime and CyberAgent. Information checked on September 30, 2026.
