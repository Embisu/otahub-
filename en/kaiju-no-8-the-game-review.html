Kaiju No.8 THE GAME Review: Worthwhile Adaptation After 1 Year of Operation

After 1 year of operation, Kaiju No.8 THE GAME proves that a game adaptation from a phenomenal manga can be both faithful to the original while maintaining an attractive long-term co-op loop.

OtaHub › Reviews › Kaiju No.8 THE GAME
Reviews 2026-08-15 7 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Kaiju No.8 THE GAME, a co-op action game adaptation of Naoya Matsumoto's phenomenal manga, has been running for a year with regular content updates, most recently adding Reno Ichikawa and Kikoru Shinomiya to the list of playable characters. The game stays true to the high-speed kaiju combat spirit of the original while building an engaging co-op loop for multiple players.

Adapting a game from a phenomenal manga is always a risky gamble, easily disappointing fans of the original if it doesn't properly capture the core spirit. Kaiju No.8 THE GAME passed that test after a year of operation, building a loyal community of players thanks to high-speed co-op action gameplay that closely follows the kaiju fighting spirit of the original manga.

Co-op action: true kaiju fighting spirit

In terms of gameplay, the game focuses on multiplayer co-op battles against giant kaiju, quite effectively recreating the tension and scale of the combat segments in the manga. The diverse character class system allows players to coordinate tactics, from direct attacks to remote support, creating tactical depth far beyond a simple hack-and-slash game.

Update roadmap: regular and clearly planned

To celebrate its one-year anniversary, the development team announced a clear update roadmap: Reno Ichikawa joins the list of playable characters, followed by Kikoru Shinomiya, along with a special livestream event. This is a positive sign that the game is receiving serious long-term content investment instead of being abandoned after its launch.

Minus point: solo content is limited

The notable minus point is that the content for solo players is quite thin compared to the co-op experience, the game is clearly designed optimally for groups of players, making those who like to experience alone may feel lacking in content and variety.

After a year of stable operation, this is one of the most worthwhile co-op action game adaptations for Kaiju No.8 fans.
