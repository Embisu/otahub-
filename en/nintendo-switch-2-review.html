Nintendo Switch 2 Review: Perfect Console or Upgrade?

Nintendo Switch 2 review: launched June 5, 2025 at $449.99, 7.9-inch 1080p 120Hz LCD, 12GB RAM, magnetic Joy-Con 2; strengths, limits and 23.68 million units sold.

Home › Reviews › Nintendo Switch 2 Review: Perfect Console or Upgrade?
Reviews 2026-05-28 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Nintendo Switch 2 launched on June 5, 2025. Price: 449 USD. Screen: 7.9-inch 1080p LCD. CPU/GPU: NVIDIA custom. RAM: 12GB. Magnetic Joy-Con (no slide required). Launch title: Mario Kart World.

This OtaHub review is compiled from official information and critics' reviews; it is not a personal playthrough diary. The score is an editorial judgment.

Quick Facts

  • Launch: June 5, 2025; $449.99 in the US (€469.99 in Europe, £395.99 in the UK)
  • Display: 7.9-inch LCD, 1080p (279 ppi), up to 120Hz, HDR10 and variable refresh rate; docked output is 1440p at 120Hz or 4K at 60Hz
  • Hardware: custom Nvidia Tegra T239, octa-core ARM Cortex-A78C CPU, 12GB LPDDR5X RAM, 256GB internal storage (expandable with microSD Express up to 2TB)
  • Joy-Con 2: magnetic attachment with a mechanical release button, a mouse mode for supported games and a C button that activates GameChat
  • Sales: 23.68 million units as of June 30, 2026

Design And Display

The Switch 2 is larger than the original with a 7.9-inch LCD, and the Joy-Con 2 are larger to match the body. Magnetic attachment with a mechanical release button replaces the sliding rail. The new C button opens GameChat, which supports chatting while playing.

Performance

The Nvidia T239 chip and 12GB of RAM bring a leap in performance over the previous generation, allowing output up to 4K at 60Hz when docked, while the handheld screen supports 120Hz with HDR10. The console is backward compatible with Switch games.

What Critics Say

Reviews praised the social features, performance improvements and backward compatibility. The criticism is the high price and the fact that the Joy-Con still lack a definitive answer to stick drift.

Verdict

The Switch 2 is a comprehensive upgrade of the Switch formula: a bigger screen, higher performance and more social features, with sales above 23 million about a year in. Buyers should weigh the $449.99 price and the risk of stick drift.
