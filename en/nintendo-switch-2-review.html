Nintendo Switch 2 Review: Perfect Console or Upgrade?

Nintendo Switch 2 review: a 7.9-inch 120Hz HDR10 display, magnetic Joy-Con 2 and a $449.99 launch price, 23.68 million sold; short battery life, no fix for stick drift and a high price are the drawbacks.

Home › Reviews › Nintendo Switch 2 Review: Perfect Console or Upgrade?
Reviews 2026-05-28 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Nintendo Switch 2 launched on June 5, 2025. Price: 449 USD. Screen: 7.9-inch 1080p LCD. CPU/GPU: NVIDIA custom. RAM: 12GB. Magnetic Joy-Con (no slide required). Launch title: Mario Kart World.

The Nintendo Switch 2 is not a generational leap but a thorough upgrade of a successful formula. A bigger screen, more performance, magnetically attached Joy-Con 2 and new social features such as GameChat make it easier to use in both handheld and docked modes.

The console launched on June 5, 2025 at $449.99, selling over 3.5 million units in four days and reaching 23.68 million by June 30, 2026.

Quick Facts

  • Release: June 5, 2025
  • Price: $449.99 at launch (about 50% above the original Switch); Nintendo later raised it to $499.99
  • Developer: Custom Nvidia Tegra T239, 12GB LPDDR5X RAM, 256GB storage (expandable via microSD Express)
  • Platforms: 7.9-inch 1080p display, 120Hz, HDR10; dock outputs 1440p at 120 or 4K at 60
  • Sales: 3.5 million in four days; 23.68 million by June 30, 2026; Mario Kart World at 15.39 million copies

Story

The Switch 2 keeps its predecessor's hybrid philosophy: a console playable both handheld and docked. The big change is a 7.9-inch LCD supporting refresh rates up to 120Hz and HDR10, with docked output up to 1440p at 120Hz or 4K at 60Hz.

The console is backward compatible with Switch games, so the old library remains usable.

Behind The Scenes

The Nintendo Switch 2 uses a custom Nvidia Tegra T239 chip, 12GB of LPDDR5X RAM and 256GB of storage expandable with microSD Express. It launched on June 5, 2025 at $449.99, about 50% above the original Switch at its launch, and Nintendo later raised the price to $499.99.

Sales passed 3.5 million units in four days and reached 23.68 million by June 30, 2026, with Mario Kart World the best-selling title (15.39 million copies).

Experience

Design and Joy-Con 2

The Joy-Con 2 are larger, attached magnetically with a mechanical release button, with a mouse mode that works even on a leg and a C button that opens GameChat. Critics praised the larger SL and SR buttons and the magnetic mechanism. The dock has better ventilation and a sturdier kickstand.

Performance and display

The Nvidia T239 chip and 12GB of RAM deliver a performance leap, and the 120Hz HDR10 screen is praised as a clear improvement over the previous generation, though some wanted OLED. It is still not the most powerful device; The Verge said it remains behind the latest PC handhelds in raw horsepower.

Battery and durability

Battery life drew the most criticism: an estimate of 2 to 6.5 hours disappointed critics, and TechRadar also cited Bluetooth audio problems and an uninspiring interface. Many were also frustrated that Nintendo did not use Hall-effect sensors to solve stick drift.

Strengths

  • A 7.9-inch 120Hz HDR10 screen and clearly improved performance
  • Magnetic Joy-Con 2 with larger buttons and a mouse mode
  • Backward compatibility with Switch games
  • Social features such as GameChat and an improved dock

Weaknesses

  • Battery of 2 to 6.5 hours criticized, and the $449.99 price about 50% above the original Switch
  • No fix for stick drift
  • Still behind the latest PC handhelds in raw horsepower, per The Verge

What Critics Say

Reviews are mostly positive but uneven: IGN gave 7/10, The Verge 80/100, Wired 9/10, Engadget 93/100 and The Guardian 4/5. Praise went to the 120Hz HDR10 display, Joy-Con 2 and dock; criticism targeted battery life, stick drift and the price.

Commercially, the console sold over 3.5 million units in four days and 23.68 million by June 30, 2026, with Mario Kart World the best-selling title at 15.39 million copies.

Who Is It For

For those who loved the Switch and want a better screen, performance and social features. Players mainly in handheld mode should weigh battery and cost. If you own an original Switch and play rarely, the higher price may be hard to justify.

Verdict

The Nintendo Switch 2 is a worthwhile upgrade with a better screen, performance and controller design, though battery, stick drift and price are drawbacks. OtaHub scores it 9.0/10.

FAQ

Does the Switch 2 play old Switch games?

Yes, the console is backward compatible with Switch games.

How long does the battery last?

An estimated 2 to 6.5 hours depending on the game, a point critics faulted.

What is the current price?

Launch price $449.99, later raised by Nintendo to $499.99 in the US.
