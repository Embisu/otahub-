JoJo Steel Ball Run Stages 2–3 Premiere September 25 on Netflix

STEEL BALL RUN JoJo's Bizarre Adventure 2nd–3rd STAGE streams exclusively on Netflix starting September 25, 2026, featuring 11 weekly episodes animated by David Production.

Home › Anime › JoJo Steel Ball Run
Anime Sep 26, 2026 5 min read OtaHub Editorial

David Production and Netflix have officially confirmed that JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: STEEL BALL RUN – 2nd & 3rd STAGE will premiere worldwide on September 25, 2026. The new batch spans 11 episodes released weekly across major global regions.

Crossing the Arizona Desert: The Race Intensifies

Following the climactic conclusion of the 1st Stage at the San Diego beach, Johnny Joestar and Gyro Zeppeli plunge into the unforgiving heat of the Arizona desert. The second and third stages introduce some of Hirohiko Araki's most inventive Stand battles to date, including the deadly pursuit of the mysterious Corpse Parts.

Director Yasuhiro Kimura returns alongside character designer Daisuke Tsumagari, maintaining the distinct gritty, high-contrast visual aesthetic reminiscent of the manga's transition to Ultra Jump.

Production Highlights & Streaming Schedule

The 11 new episodes will stream in 4K HDR with Dolby Atmos support on Netflix, featuring both the original Japanese voice cast and multi-language dubbing including English, Spanish, and French.

Broadcast Details:
  • Title: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: STEEL BALL RUN (Stages 2–3)
  • Episodes: 11 (Episodes 13–23)
  • Release Date: September 25, 2026
  • Platform: Netflix Worldwide
  • Studio: David Production
