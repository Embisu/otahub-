Kaiju No.8 THE GAME: 1 Year Update Roadmap, Reno Ichikawa Playable

Kaiju No.8 THE GAME announces the 1-year anniversary update roadmap: Reno Ichikawa joins the roster from August 10, Kikoru Shinomiya from August 20, and a special livestream to close the event series on August 30.

OtaHub › Gaming › Kaiju No.8 THE GAME: 1 Year Update Roadmap, Reno Ichikawa Playable
Gaming 2026-08-12 5 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
To celebrate the 1-year anniversary of its launch, Kaiju No.8 THE GAME launches an update roadmap throughout August 2026: Reno Ichikawa is officially playable from August 10, Kikoru Shinomiya joins the roster from August 20, and a special livestream announcing the next content takes place on August 30.

One year since its launch, Kaiju No.8 THE GAME marks this milestone with a series of updates spanning almost the entire month of August 2026, instead of just a single in-game event. The development team confirms this will be the most content-packed month since the game's launch, with a focus on adding two of the most requested characters from the community.

Reno Ichikawa joined the roster on August 10

Reno Ichikawa, a young but ambitious member of the 3rd Defense Division, officially became a playable character on August 10th, kicking off a series of celebratory events. Reno is one of the supporting characters beloved by both anime and manga fans due to his well-developed personality, and bringing him into the game is seen as a logical step to enrich team composition options.

Kikoru Shinomiya followed on August 20

Ten days later, it was my turn Kikoru Shinomiya, the female warrior possessing superior strength in the 3rd Division, officially joined the list of playable characters from August 20th. With high popularity among fans thanks to her overwhelming fighting style and distinctive proud personality, Kikoru's appearance is predicted to attract a significant return of players.

Special livestream closing the anniversary month

The chain of events will end with one special livestream on August 30, where the development team plans to reveal the content direction for the next phase of the game, including the possibility of adding new characters, game modes, or cooperative events. This is also an opportunity for the studio to share summary data from the past year of operation, something the player community is always interested in to assess the long-term "health" of the game.

  • Reno Ichikawa playable: From August 10, 2026
  • Kikoru Shinomiya playable: From August 20, 2026
  • Special livestream: 30/8/2026
  • Milestone: Celebrating 1 year of game launch
  • Platform: PC (Steam)

Why is this update important?

For live-service games based on anime IPs, the one-year mark is always a crucial test of long-term player retention. Kaiju No.8 THE GAME's decision to cram a lot of quality content into one month, instead of scattering it sporadically, shows the development team is betting on creating a big enough "boost" to bring back both old players and attract new ones before entering its second year of operation.
