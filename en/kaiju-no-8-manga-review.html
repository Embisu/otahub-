Kaiju No.8 Review: Jump+'s Phenomenal Shounen Monsters

Naoya Matsumoto turns a 32-year-old monster cleaner into a new shonen star, combining high-speed action with an emotional story about unfinished dreams and second chances.

Home › Manga › Kaiju No.8 Review: Jump+'s Phenomenal Shounen Monster
Reviews · Manga 2026-08-15 5 min read OtaHub Editorial
Summary
Kaiju No.8 Naoya Matsumoto's has become one of the biggest manga phenomena on the Shounen Jump+ platform, telling the story of Kafuka Hibino, a 32-year-old man who cleans up monster corpses after battles, who unexpectedly gains the ability to transform into a kaiju after an accident. The series combines fast-paced action with themes of age, unfulfilled dreams, and the meaning of starting over, a rare perspective in the shonen genre which usually focuses on young protagonists.

In a shonen market filled with teenage protagonists pursuing their dreams for the first time, Kaiju No.8 Chooses a remarkably different path: the protagonist, Kafuka Hibino, is 32 years old and once dreamed of becoming a monster soldier in her youth, but ultimately ends up cleaning up monster corpses after battles-a position considered lowly in the society the series portrays.

Transform into a monster: life-changing twist

The turning point of the series comes when Kafuka accidentally swallows a small creature and gains the ability to transform into a giant kaiju, which is "Kaiju No. 8" in the series' title. This twist not only opens up visually stunning action but also places the protagonist in a paradoxical situation: he joins the defense force to fight against kaiju, while possessing the power of the very enemy he must defeat.

Second chance theme: rare depth

What makes Kaiju No. 8 stand out from many competitors in the same genre is how the series explores the themes of age and unfulfilled dreams. Kafuka isn't a young genius, but someone who \"missed the boat\" by societal standards, and his journey delivers a rare and relatable message: it's never too late to start over, as long as you're determined.

Monster design and action: visual peak

Visually, Naoya Matsumoto, who previously worked at Marvel before becoming a manga artist, delivers diverse, terrifying, and creative kaiju designs, combined with fast-paced, decisive action sequences that create a cinematic feel even on a static page.

  • Author: Naoya Matsumoto
  • Platform: Shounen Jump+ (Shueisha)
  • Main character: Kafuka Hibino, 32 years old
  • Category: Action, Kaiju, Seinen-shonen
  • Anime adaptation: available, along with the game Kaiju No.8 THE GAME

With visually stunning action, impressive monster designs, and an inspiring message about second chances, it deserves to be considered one of the most noteworthy manga phenomena of the decade.
