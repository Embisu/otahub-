GTA 6: Everything We Know About 2026's Biggest Blockbuster

Vice City returns with the first female protagonist in GTA franchise history, and an open world that promises to change the definition of the genre forever.

OtaHub › Gaming › GTA 6: Everything We Know About the Biggest Blockbuster
Gaming 2026-06-23 8 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
GTA 6 launches fall 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. The main character is Lucia (female), accompanying Jason. Setting: Leonida state (modern Vice City). Price: 80 USD / ~2 million VND.

Twelve years after GTA V and more than a decade of endless waiting, Rockstar Games officially set the date: Grand Theft Auto VI will be released in fall 2026. Not a late PC port, not a remaster, this is a game built entirely from the ground up with an estimated budget exceeding $2 billion, making it the most expensive game in history.

Setting: Leonida State and Vice City New Generation

Leonida is the name Rockstar gives to the fictional state inspired by Florida in the GTA universe. Vice City remains the center, but this time it's no longer just a bustling neon city; it's an entire state with vast suburbs, Everglades-style swamps, rainforests, and small coastal towns. According to several reliable leaks, the GTA 6 map will be larger than GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined, with a day-night cycle, dynamic weather, and the most vibrant ecosystem ever.

Lucia: The First Female Protagonist In GTA History

This is what makes GTA 6 historic. Lucia, a young Latina woman with a criminal record, is the main character the player will accompany for much of the experience. Along with her is Jason, Lucia's boyfriend, forming a Bonnie and Clyde-style couple that Rockstar has built with maturity and consequences, not just violence. Trailer 2 reveals Lucia in various situations: bank robbery, transnational smuggling, and everyday moments between intense incidents.

Living World: NPCs Have Memory, Economic Fluctuations

Leaked internal documents and hints from the developer indicate the most advanced NPC system ever built in an open-world game. NPCs will remember players' faces across multiple play sessions, can file civil lawsuits if you damage their property, and react differently depending on the time of day. The economy also fluctuates based on player actions: real estate prices increase in areas where you cause disturbances, and in-game news reflects the heist you just committed.

Multiplayer: GTA Online 2.0

GTA Online still generates millions of dollars a month for Rockstar even though GTA V was released in 2013, so it's inevitable that the company will continue developing this aspect in GTA 6. According to official confirmation, GTA 6 Online will launch a few months after the solo version, with larger servers, seasonal events, and a much more sophisticated online economy. Rockstar promises no pay-to-win, only additional cosmetic and storyline content.

Price and Versions

GTA 6 will cost 80 USD (approximately 2 million VND) on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. A PC version will not be available at launch, as confirmed by Rockstar. The Collector's Edition, priced at $150, includes an art book, pin set, character model, and exclusive in-game content. Vietnamese gamers can purchase it through the PSN Store Asia or Xbox Store at the original price.

When we first let Lucia walk through Vice City, we knew this was going to be a game that redefined the entire genre. Aaron Garbut, Art Director, Rockstar Games

Overall Assessment

GTA 6 carries immense pressure: the sequel to the best-selling game of all time, built over a decade. Every piece of confirmed information points to Rockstar pouring all the experience from Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA Online into a world that is not only geographically vast but also more narratively profound and vibrant than ever before. Fall 2026 will be a milestone not to be missed.
