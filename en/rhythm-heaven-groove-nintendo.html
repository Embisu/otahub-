Rhythm Heaven Groove Is Out Now After 11 Years, Free Demo Still Live

Rhythm Heaven Groove, the first new entry in Nintendo's rhythm series since 2015's Megamix, launched July 2 for Nintendo Switch, and the free Starter Demo is still available on the eShop.

OtaHub Editorial · 2026-08-18 · 6 min read

In short: Rhythm Heaven Groove officially launched July 2 for Nintendo Switch, the series' first new entry since 2015's Megamix. The free Starter Demo, released June 22, is still available on the eShop.

Rhythm Heaven Groove, the first new entry in Nintendo's rhythm series since 2015's Megamix, officially launched July 2 for Nintendo Switch and is also playable on Nintendo Switch 2.

Ahead of launch, Nintendo released a free Starter Demo on the eShop on June 22, featuring five solo minigames, Hoop Trundling, Brolly Good Show, Disc Dog, Feeding the Beast, and Remix 1, plus a multiplayer minigame called Rhythm Tweezers. Progress made in the demo carries directly into the full game, so players who finished the demo's opening chapter resume from exactly where they left off after buying the full release.

The demo remains free to download on the Nintendo eShop for anyone still deciding whether to buy the game, and it keeps the series' trademark formula of simple inputs that demand precise timing, the same feel that has defined the franchise since its original Game Boy Advance debut.
