Seven Seas Announces 18 New Licenses at Anime NYC 2026

A License Downpour At Anime NYC
Seven Seas Entertainment revealed 18 new publishing licenses at Anime NYC 2026, spanning its Seven Seas BL, GL, Webtoons, Siren (audiobook), and Airship imprints, with releases spread from late 2026 through 2027.

1. A Wide-Ranging Slate Across Genres

Seven Seas Entertainment has announced 18 new publishing licenses, first revealed at the company's industry panel at Anime NYC 2026. This round covers nearly every genre in the publisher's catalog: audiobook adaptations, Boys' Love (BL) manga, Girls' Love/yuri (GL) manga, manhwa, and Chinese danmei novels, underscoring the publisher's aggressive diversification strategy.

2. The Headliner: Faraway Wanderers Deluxe Hardcover

The standout announcement is Faraway Wanderers by priest, the wuxia danmei most requested by fans for years, from the same author behind Silent Reading and Guardian (both already available from Seven Seas). The novel will release as a premium deluxe hardcover edition in 2027. Alongside it comes First-Class Lawyer, a sci-fi mystery danmei novel from the author of Copper Coins and Panguan, slated for July 2027.

3. A Strong Wave of New BL/GL Titles

On the Boys' Love front, The Ghost's Nocturne (ANANAS & C.R Jade), a hit BL manhwa about a hedonistic prince of the underworld and the human whose heart he must win over, gets its first English print edition in June 2027. My Lips are Being Targeted by Karin Anzai, a rom-com set in a school of magic where a kiss can boost one's abilities, was also confirmed for the same month. On the Girls' Love side, I Wish it Had Just Been Love by Rui Obata, an emotional story of reuniting with a high school crush, is set for July 2027.

4. Notable Audiobooks And Novels

Seven Seas' Siren audiobook imprint also picked up three new titles: A Tale of the Secret Saint ZERO (a prequel to the light novel/manga series of the same name, releasing October 2026), Dinghai Fusheng Records (the hit danmei series previously adapted into a donghua, releasing November 2026), and There's Something Wrong with the Chief, narrated by Zion Jang, also releasing November 2026. Separately, the novel Till the End of the Moon: the source material behind a hit Chinese TV drama, gets its first English release in August 2027.
