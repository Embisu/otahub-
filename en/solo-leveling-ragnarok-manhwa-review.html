Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Review: An Ambitious Sequel

In-depth review of Solo Leveling: Ragnarok: Sung Suho rises to inherit his father's shadow mantle and protect humanity against galactic threats.

Home › Manga › Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Review: An Ambitious Sequel
Manga 2026-09-27 4 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
Solo Leveling: Ragnarok serves as the official continuation of the legendary manhwa series, following Sung Suho - son of Shadow Monarch Sung Jin-woo - as he awakens his dormant heritage against cosmic invaders. An in-depth review of REDICE Studio's dynamic artwork and its universe expansion.

Solo Leveling: Ragnarok is the official sequel to one of the most successful global manhwa, Solo Leveling. The story is written by Daul at REDICE Studio, with JIN, one of the disciples who finished the final chapters of the original Solo Leveling after author DUBU passed away in 2022, handling the art.

The series drew major attention from its announcement thanks to the original's reputation, but also faced disruption: it went on hiatus in early 2026 when illustrator JIN announced mandatory military service, and chapter 69 marked its return after about seven months, released on August 12, 2026.

Quick Facts

  • Author: Story: Daul; art: JIN; REDICE Studio
  • Publisher: Official sequel to Solo Leveling (DUBU/Chugong)
  • Reception: On hiatus from around January 7, 2026; returned with chapter 69 on August 12, 2026
  • Adaptations: Season 3 (following the hiatus) is expected to switch to a new artist, one of DUBU's disciples

Story

Ragnarok continues the Solo Leveling world after Sung Jin-Woo reached the peak of his power, introducing a new threat and cast of characters while staying connected to the concluded original story.

As the official sequel rather than a fan project, the series faces heavy pressure to both preserve the spirit of the original Solo Leveling and carve out its own direction.

Behind The Scenes

JIN, an artist tied to DUBU's legacy, stepping away to complete mandatory military service is a common occurrence for male Korean comic artists, but for a series with expectations as high as Ragnarok, it caused a significant disruption. Confirming a new artist for the next phase, described as one of DUBU's disciples, shows the studio wants to preserve visual continuity.

Experience

The pressure of an official sequel

Ragnarok faces enormous expectations from original Solo Leveling fans, and every story or visual decision is scrutinized far more than a typical new manhwa.

Disruption and fan trust

The seven-month hiatus and the likely artist change are a major test of the community's patience, though choosing a successor from DUBU's own team is an effort to minimize risk.

Strengths

  • Directly continues the beloved Solo Leveling world
  • The team has direct ties to DUBU's original crew
  • The return after hiatus shows commitment to finishing the series

Weaknesses

  • The seven-month hiatus hurt fan momentum
  • A mid-series artist change risks visual consistency
  • Heavy pressure from comparisons to the original

What Critics Say

As the official sequel to a global IP, Ragnarok drew major attention from its announcement, but the story around its hiatus and the artist's military service became nearly as big a talking point as the content itself. The confirmation of chapter 69's return on August 12, 2026 was welcomed positively by the community after a long wait.

Who Is It For

For Solo Leveling fans who want to keep following the world after the original ended. Newcomers should read the original Solo Leveling in full first to understand the context and characters.

Verdict

Solo Leveling: Ragnarok is an ambitious sequel with a solid foundation from the original team, but still has to prove itself through publishing disruptions. OtaHub scores it 8.2/10.
