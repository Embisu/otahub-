Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Manhwa Premiere Review: Sung Suho Inherits the Shadow Monarch Legacy

In-depth review of Solo Leveling: Ragnarok webtoon premiere, Sung Suho's awakening, Beru's return, and REDICE Studio artistry.

Home›Manga›Solo Leveling: Ragnarok Manhwa Premiere Review: Sung Suho Inherits the Shadow Monarch Legacy
Manga Aug 24, 2026 8 min read OtaHub Editorial
Webtoon Review Verdict
The highly anticipated sequel webtoon Solo Leveling: Ragnarok has officially debuted on KakaoPage. Illustrated by the talented team at REDICE Studio, Sung Suho steps up to defend Earth against Outer Gods.

Adapted from the hit web novel by Daul, Solo Leveling: Ragnarok continues the timeline protected by Sung Jinwoo\'s ultimate sacrifice. When Jinwoo travels to the cosmic frontier to battle the Outer Gods (Itarim), Earth once again faces existential threats.

1. Narrative: Sung Suho\'s Awakening & Father\'s Legacy

Having his powers sealed as a child to live a normal life, college student Suho is thrown back into danger when an S-rank dungeon gate opens on campus. The shadow ant king Beru crosses dimensions to awaken Suho\'s exclusive Hunter Leveling System.

2. Visual Excellence by REDICE Studio

Alleviating all fan concerns following the passing of artist DUBU, REDICE Studio\'s successor team delivers breathtaking glowing particle effects, ferocious combat choreography, and signature dark purple monarch auras.

3. OtaHub Score & Verdict: 9.2 / 10

With stellar action pacing and deeply engaging world expansion, Solo Leveling: Ragnarok proves itself as an essential sequel for all webtoon enthusiasts.
