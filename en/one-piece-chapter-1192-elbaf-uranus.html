Summary
The latest issue of Weekly Shonen Jump delivers a monumental shockwave as One Piece Chapter 1192 reveals the secrets of Ancient Weapon Uranus, the Mother Flame energy source, and the terrifying dark authority of Imu.

Weekly Shonen Jump made global headlines with the release of One Piece Chapter 1192, thrusting the Elbaf Arc into its most dramatic and consequential climax yet.

Ancient Weapon Uranus & The Mother Flame

Chapter 1192 ties directly into the destruction of Lulucia Kingdom. Ancient inscriptions upon Elbaf's Yggdrasil confirm that Uranus is an airborne weapon system powered by Dr. Vegapunk's perpetual Mother Flame.

Imu's Shadow Forms & The Stand at Elbaf

The chapter reveals explicit illustrations of Imu's metaphysical dark arts. Standing in opposition, Luffy in Gear 5 Nika form aligns with giant warriors Dorry, Brogy, and Prince Loki to defend Elbaf against the World Government's invasion.
