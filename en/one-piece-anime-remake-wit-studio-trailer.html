THE ONE PIECE: WIT Studio Drops First Teaser for 4K East Blue Remake on Netflix

WIT Studio and Netflix reveal the first official animation teaser for THE ONE PIECE remake, featuring reimagined East Blue cinematography and dynamic pacing.

Date: Aug 31, 2026
Read Time: 5 min read

A Legend Reborn in the East Blue
WIT Studio and Netflix have released the first animation teaser for THE ONE PIECE, reimagining Eiichiro Oda's epic saga with modern 4K cinematics.

1. Streamlined Pacing & Pure Manga Faithfulness

The remake restructures the iconic East Blue Saga into 25 high-octane episodes, eliminating filler and delivering cinematic storytelling from Romance Dawn to Arlong Park.

2. Signature WIT Studio Action Direction

Director Masashi Koizuka brings dynamic 3D camera sweeps and visceral choreography to Luffy's Gum-Gum attacks and Zoro's Three-Sword Style swordplay.
