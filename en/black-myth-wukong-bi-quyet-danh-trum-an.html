Black Myth: Wukong: Master Guide to 5 Hardest Secret Bosses & Erlang Shen True Ending

Detailed tactics to unlock and conquer the 5 toughest secret bosses in Black Myth: Wukong, Mount Mei access, and True Ending requirements.

Boss Tactics Summary
To unlock the True Ending before the Rebirth of the Great Sage DLC, hunters must conquer Erlang Shen at Mount Mei. This guide breaks down shield-breaking vessels and stance counters.

The secret boss encounters in Black Myth: Wukong are designed as ultimate endgame challenges, demanding mastery over all 3 combat stances (Smash, Pillar, Thrust) and transformation spells.

1. Unlocking the Secret Realm of Mount Mei (Mai Sơn)

To access Mount Mei and battle Erlang Shen, you must complete the hidden sub-areas across Chapters 1 through 4:

  1. Chapter 1 (Elder Jinchi): Ring all 3 ancient bells in the Wolf Forest and Bamboo Grove.
  2. Chapter 2 (Kingdom of Sahali): Defeat the Tiger Vanguard and help the Drunken Boar retrieve the Sobering Stone.
  3. Chapter 3 (Melon Field): Complete the Treasure Hunter side quest to meet Maitreya Buddha.
  4. Chapter 4 (Purple Cloud Mountain): Defeat Venom Daoist twice to enter the ancient mural realm.

2. Combat Strategies Against Erlang Shen\'s Armor

  • Break Shields with Vessels: When Erlang activates his celestial blue barrier, immediately deploy the Weaver\'s Needle or Wind Tamer to completely strip his armor for 5 seconds.
  • Howling Celestial Hound Counters: Use A Pluck of Many combined with Immobilize to pin down the boss while dodging the hound\'s lunges.

3. 5 Hardest Secret Bosses Ranked

From the Scorpionlord in Purple Cloud Mountain to Yellow Loong in Chapter 3, utilize Thrust Stance parries and high poison resistance gourds to secure victory.
