Ball x Pit Launches Final Free Update: The Naturalist

Ball x Pit released The Naturalist, its final free content update, adding two new characters, 11 new balls, and five new passives on August 6.

Home › Gaming › Ball x Pit Launches Final Free Update: The Naturalist
Gaming 2026-08-18 6 min read OtaHub Editorial
Key Takeaway
In short: Ball x Pit released The Naturalist, its final free content update, adding two new characters, 11 new balls, and five new passives on August 6.

Ball x Pit has officially released The Naturalist, the game's final free content update, on all platforms on August 6, 2026, adding two new characters, 11 new balls and five new passives.

The two new characters are The Ballbearer, who doubles the number of ball slots but gives up passive items entirely, and The Hoary Hoarder, who has only two ball slots but gets double the passive slots. They are two opposite playstyles, and they expand build-crafting options considerably.

New Weapons and Mechanics in The Naturalist

Among the new balls, the Armageddon ball summons a fierce meteor shower, while the Drill ball pierces enemies from behind. Among the new passives, the Remote Detonator makes defeated enemies spawn explosive balls, and the Platinum Dumbbell increases the damage of your balls until they next hit a wall.

Background for anyone new to the ball-throwing roguelite genre: Ball x Pit belongs to the roguelite family combined with a "vaporwave brick-breaker" mechanic. Players throw an ever-growing number of balls with different effects to destroy enemies, build defensive structures vertically and keep upgrading with every new run. The genre has risen thanks to the ability to combine hundreds of different balls and passives, producing a huge variety of builds each time you start, which is why the two contrasting characters added in The Naturalist make a real tactical difference.

Supported Platforms and Future Plans

Ball x Pit is now available on PC, PS5, Nintendo Switch 2 and mobile. The developer confirmed that The Naturalist is the game's final free update, meaning the official free content support cycle ends after this award-winning roguelite kept expanding continuously since launch.

OtaHub's Take

Openly announcing this as the "final" free update, instead of quietly ending support as many indie games do, is a trust-building move worth noting with the community. Players know the free content cycle has been closed out cleanly, avoiding the feeling that the game was "abandoned" midway. With two new characters whose building philosophies are completely opposed, one maximizing the number of balls and the other maximizing passives, this last update is also a smart way to end the free content with a tactical choice that has depth, rather than simply adding content for the sake of it.

Source: blog.playstation.com.
