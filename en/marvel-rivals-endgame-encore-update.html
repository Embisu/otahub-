Marvel Rivals Launches "Endgame Encore" Update: New Infinity Saga Tab, 20% Off Coupons

Version 20260827 brings a limited-time Infinity Saga event that gathers every previously released MCU-inspired costume bundle into one store tab, plus a stacking discount coupon timed to Avengers: Endgame Encore's theatrical return.

Author: OtaHub Editorial
Published: 2026-08-28
Reading time: 4 min read
Category: Gaming Update
Timed To Avengers: Endgame Encore
Marvel Rivals' August 27, 2026 patch introduces a limited-time Infinity Saga store tab collecting every previously released MCU-inspired costume bundle, alongside a 20%-off Encore Coupon tied to the theatrical re-release of Avengers: Endgame Encore.

1. Version 20260827 Is Live, No Server Downtime Required

NetEase Games and Marvel Games confirmed that Marvel Rivals update 20260827 went live at 09:00 UTC on August 27, 2026, with no server downtime — players simply patch and jump straight into matches. The update continues the steady cadence of balance passes and bug fixes the studio has kept up throughout Season 3.5.

2. The Infinity Saga Tab And A 20% Encore Coupon

The headline addition is a limited-time store tab called Infinity Saga, live from August 28 through September 11, 2026, which brings together every MCU-inspired costume bundle the game has released to date. Every player receives an Encore Coupon good for 20% off purchases in that tab — and using the coupon on an eligible purchase instantly grants an identical replacement coupon, letting the discount chain into the next buy.

The event is timed to ride the promotional wave of Avengers: Endgame Encore, Marvel Studios' expanded theatrical re-release of Avengers: Endgame, due in cinemas shortly after this patch went live.

3. New Costumes: Peni Parker, Angela, And Squirrel Girl

Alongside the Infinity Saga event, the store also welcomes a wave of new costumes: the Peni Parker Liquid Shell bundle (available August 28 – September 25), plus the Angela Queen of Hel, Squirrel Girl Acorn Divinity, and Squirrel Girl Acorn Divinity Emoji bundles, all live in the store starting 02:00 UTC on August 28, 2026.

4. Notable Fixes: Angela, Doctor Strange, Scarlet Witch

On the balance side, the patch fixes a visual bug where Angela's effects displayed incorrectly after she was defeated while Odin's "Unacknowledged" Team-Up ability was active, and addresses an issue that let Doctor Strange instantly refill his Shield of the Seraphim to maximum while the "Psionic Vortex" Team-Up's enhanced effect was active — a bug that could tilt the balance in ranked matches. The patch also fixes the Scarlet Witch–Doctor Strange Team-Up showcase video, jagged textures on Cloak & Dagger's Coastal Couple costume, and a VFX offset on Scarlet Witch's Floral Tide emote.
