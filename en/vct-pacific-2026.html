VCT Pacific 2026: Paper Rex Dominates the Season, Aiming for Champions

Paper Rex closed the VCT Pacific 2026 group stage with an absolutely unbeaten record, affirming its position as the region's top championship candidate before the playoffs.

OtaHub › Gaming › VCT Pacific 2026: Paper Rex Dominates the Season, Aiming for Champions
Gaming 2026-08-11 5 minutes to read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
Paper Rex is undefeated in the VCT Pacific 2026 group stage, considered the number one candidate for the regional championship and ticket to the Champions at the end of the year.

Undefeated. That's the only word that needs to be used to describe the group stage VCT Pacific 2026 of Paper Rex. While most other strong teams lost at least one match against unpredictable opponents, the Singapore team went through the group stage without losing to anyone, far ahead of the entire group of opponents competing directly for the year-end Champions ticket.

It's not luck, it's a proven formula

What makes this impressive winning streak possible isn't temporary luck, but rather the playstyle that has become Paper Rex's trademark since their rise to international prominence: flexibly adapting tactics to each map, instead of sticking to a fixed meta like many other teams. This unpredictability forces opponents to prepare for multiple scenarios simultaneously, often leaving them unable to react in time.

The duelist duo carries the burden, but not alone

On the court, the duelist duo remains the main offensive spearhead, consistently creating decisive opening plays. But what makes this lineup harder to counter lies in its support system behind it, flexible enough to handle even defensive maps where many strong offensive teams often struggle. The coaching staff also demonstrates an extremely keen ability to read the game and adjust tactics mid-game, small details that are often the deciding factor in the most closely contested matches.

There is still a fierce race behind

Although Paper Rex has almost secured their spot, the race for the remaining Champions League tickets is far from over. Many strong teams from South Korea and Japan are close behind, and the upcoming Pacific playoffs are likely to be the scene of some of the most nail-biting matches of the season, with the final ticket potentially separated by just one play.

With their dominant performance throughout the group stage, experts now almost unanimously consider Paper Rex the number one contender for the Pacific Championship 2026. The question now is no longer whether they will qualify for the Champions League, but whether any team is strong enough to stop them on that journey.
