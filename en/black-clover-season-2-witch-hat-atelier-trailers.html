Fall 2026 Anime Blockbusters: Black Clover Season 2 & Witch Hat Atelier Unveil Stunning New Trailers

Studio Pierrot and Bug Films showcase thrilling new trailers for Black Clover Season 2 and Witch Hat Atelier ahead of the highly anticipated Fall 2026 anime broadcast season.

Key Takeaway
Studio Pierrot and Bug Films showcase thrilling new trailers for Black Clover Season 2 and Witch Hat Atelier ahead of the highly anticipated Fall 2026 anime broadcast season.

The Fall 2026 anime broadcast season is gearing up for an unforgettable showdown as two major powerhouses — Black Clover Season 2 (Studio Pierrot) and Witch Hat Atelier (Bug Films) — reveal official high-octane trailers to massive fan acclaim.

1. Black Clover Season 2: Asta Reclaims the Battlefield in Spade Kingdom Arc

The newly dropped 2-minute preview highlights Asta's triumphant return wielding anti-magic demonic blades against the sinister Dark Triad. Pierrot confirmed enhanced cinematic production pipelines delivering fluid animation and orchestral battle scores.

2. Witch Hat Atelier: A Hand-Drawn Magical Masterpiece by Bug Films

Adapted from Kamome Shirahama's Eisner-winning manga, Witch Hat Atelier stands as one of 2026's most visually stunning anime adaptations. The trailer exhibits rich classical hand-drawn line art, delicate ink hatching, and captivating atmospheric world-building.
