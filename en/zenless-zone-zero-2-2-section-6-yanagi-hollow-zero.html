Zenless Zone Zero 2.2: Section 6 Yanagi Debut, Endless Hollow Zero Roguelike & New Codes

HoYoverse details Zenless Zone Zero Version 2.2 featuring S-Rank agent Yanagi of Section 6, an endless Hollow Zero roguelike mode, and 1600 free Polychromes.

🕒 07/09/2026 ✍️ Mambu (Duc Anh) ⏱️ 7 min read
⚡ Zenless Zone Zero 2.2 Key Highlights
  • S-Rank Agent Yanagi (Electric / Anomaly): Wielding high-tech naginata dual blades with rapid-fire off-field Shock detonation mechanics.
  • Endless Hollow Zero Mode: 50 floors of escalating roguelike challenges featuring new Resonium synergy cards and Tuning Calibrator rewards.
  • Combat QoL Upgrades: Enhanced Perfect Dodge response windows and native 120 FPS refresh rate support for PC and PS5.
  • Version Rewards: 7-day check-in event granting 10 Encrypted Master Tapes and 300 Polychrome redemption codes.

Following the roaring acclaim of the Outer Ring storylines, developer HoYoverse has officially broadcast the Version 2.2 Special Program for Zenless Zone Zero: Thunderous Midnight of Section 6.

New S-Rank Agent Tsukishiro Yanagi – Section 6 Vice-Captain

Tsukishiro Yanagi enters New Eridu as an S-Rank Electric / Anomaly specialist. Armed with a cybernetic Naginata blade, her dual-stance combat rhythm sets a new benchmark for team rotation fluidity:

  • EX Special Attack – Stance Shift Cleave: Executes five lightning-infused slashes creating a high-voltage zone that immediately triggers instantaneous Disorder procs against afflicted targets.
  • Chain Synergy: When teammates execute Chain Attacks, Yanagi dashes into combat off-field, granting a 45% Anomaly Buildup rate buff to the active squad for 15 seconds.

Explore more gacha updates in our analysis of Genshin Impact 7.0 Cryo Archon Tsaritsa and our Girls Frontline 2 Beginner & Squad Guide.

Infinite Hollow Zero Overhaul – Ultimate Roguelike Action

The centerpiece of Version 2.2 is the Endless Hollow Zero game mode. Agents brave 50 procedurally generated floors filled with dynamic Corruption events, randomized Boss encounters, and exclusive Resonium synergy cards that radically modify skill mechanics.

Launch Schedule & Celebration Rewards

Zenless Zone Zero Version 2.2 goes live on September 25, 2026. Players logging in during the launch period will receive 10 Encrypted Master Tapes, 1,600+ Polychromes, and valuable upgrade catalysts. Catch more guides in our Gaming Section and OtaHub Reviews.
