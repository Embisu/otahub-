Top 10 Best PC Games 2026: A Can't Miss List

2026 is a booming year for PC Game with a series of blockbusters coming at the same time. Here are 10 games you must definitely experience.

OtaHub › RankingsIn-depth › Top 10 Best PC Games 2026: A Can't Miss List
Rankings 2026-06-23 9 minutes read OtaHub Editorial
Tom Tat
The top 10 PC Game of 2026 include: GTA 6, Elden Ring DLC 2, Death Stranding 2, Monster Hunter Wilds DLC, Alan Wake 3, and many groundbreaking indies. Judged by gameplay, story, graphics and value.

2026 was one of the richest PC game years of the decade, not in terms of quantity, but in terms of consistent quality. There were no "dry years" as each quarter had at least one blockbuster worth playing. Below are the 10 best PC Game of 2026, ranked based on community reviews and actual value.

1. GTA 6 (Rockstar Games)

There's no arguing about the number one spot; GTA 6 is the most anticipated game in history, and it lived up to that expectation. The open world of Leonida is so vast that GTA 5 looks small, the dual protagonist story between Jason and Lucia is the best Rockstar has ever written, and the level of detail in every street corner surpasses any other open-world game to date. 10/10.

2. Elden Ring: Fractured Abyss (FromSoftware)

Elden Ring's second DLC continues the tradition of Shadow of the Erdtree, being much bigger than many thought, much harder than many wanted, and more satisfying than anything. Fractured Abyss leads players into a land beneath the earth, the final lore about the origins of the Greater Will is revealed. 9.5/10.

3. Death Stranding 2: On The Beach (Kojima Productions)

Hideo Kojima has once again created something no one else has: a strange, emotional, and truly human connection delivery simulator experience. DS2 expands beyond America and takes Sam to new lands with new philosophies, and Woodkid's soundtrack continues to be a pinnacle of game music. 9.3/10.

4. Monster Hunter Wilds: Titan Rift (Capcom)

Monster Hunter Wilds' first DLC brings to Titan Rift with 8 completely new monsters and 3 returning monsters redesigned from the ground up. Endgame loop is significantly improved, and the MH community is rating this as the best MH DLC since Iceborne. 9.1/10.

5-10: Gems You Can't Miss

Positions 5 to 10 in the top PC Game 2026 include: Alan Wake 3 (Remedy continues meta-fiction horror with new peak, 9.0/10), Hollow Knight: Silksong (finally released and worth all the wait, 9.4/10), Hades II 1.0 full release (Supergiant continues the perfect roguelike formula, 9.5/10), Avowed (Obsidian finally returns to RPG heights, 8.8/10), Crimson Desert (biggest surprise of the year, 8.5/10) and Stellar Blade PC (perfect port with DLC content, 8.8/10).

Conclusion: 2026 Is The Year Of PC Games

If you could only play three games in 2026, choose GTA 6, Elden Ring Fractured Abyss, and Hollow Knight Silksong, three titles representing three different design styles but all reaching the absolute pinnacle of the genre. 2026 will be remembered as one of the greatest gaming years in PC gaming history.
