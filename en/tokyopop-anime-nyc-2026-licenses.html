Tokyopop Announces 7 New Manga Licenses at Anime NYC 2026

At Anime NYC 2026, Tokyopop announced seven new North American manga licenses plus two additional manhwa titles, headlined by the surreal Garagarapon and the psychological horror thriller And Then, She Became Mina.

At Anime NYC 2026, Tokyopop announced seven new North American manga licenses plus two additional manhwa titles, headlined by the surreal Garagarapon and the psychological horror thriller And Then, She Became Mina.
A Licensing Wave Out Of Anime NYC 2026
At Anime NYC 2026, publisher Tokyopop announced seven new manga licenses for the North American market, alongside two additional manhwa titles, with most releases planned for 2027.

1. Garagarapon, A War Between Reality And Imagination

Leading the list is Garagarapon, centered on two government officials, Daichi Hongou and Hisui Tenma, tasked with resolving and mediating conflicts that arise between the real world and the worlds and objects born from imagination, daydreams, and idle thoughts, a surreal premise rarely seen in licensed manga.

2. "And Then, She Became Mina", The Standout Psychological Horror Pick

The most talked-about title in this announcement is And Then, She Became Mina, following high schooler Mina, a survivor of a random daylight mass murder who lives on after receiving a heart transplant. The real tragedy begins when Mina discovers the heart transplanted into her body belonged to Shimizu Misako, the very perpetrator of the murders.

3. SIX: Paths of Horror And The Rest Of The Lineup

The remaining licenses include SIX: Paths of Horror – The Manga, What Goes On Behind the Scenes, The Commander's Proposal Is Out of the Question!, Breathe Out, Look at Me, and First Love Begins with a Second Kiss. Alongside these, Tokyopop also confirmed two new manhwa, Lily of the Valley and Magic Soup, bringing the total announced titles to nine.

4. Part Of A Larger Licensing Wave At Anime NYC 2026

Tokyopop's announcement is just one part of a broader licensing wave at Anime NYC 2026, the event also saw Seven Seas Entertainment reveal 18 new licenses, Yen Press announce a fresh batch of light novels and manga, and numerous other publishers expand their North American catalogs. All seven Tokyopop manga and two manhwa are slated for a 2027 release; OtaHub will update this page with exact dates as they're confirmed.
