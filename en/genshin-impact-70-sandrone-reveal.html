Genshin Impact 7.0: HoYoverse Introduces 7th Fatui Harbinger Sandrone & Cryo Marionette Combat

The Snezhnaya update unveils giant autonomous mech mechanics in battle and explores Sandrone pivotal role in the uprising against Celestia.

Author: OtaHub Editorial
Published: 2026-08-25
Reading time: 5 min read
Category: Mobile & Gacha
Special Program Highlights
HoYoverse has officially unveiled the Snezhnaya expansion, headlined by the debut of 5-star Cryo playable character Sandrone (The Marionette).

1. Marionette Mech Summon & Persistent Cryo Field

Sandrone introduces an innovative pet-combat archetype, commanding an imposing mechanical automaton that delivers devastating heavy-impact Cryo sweeps while shredding enemy elemental resistances. Her Elemental Burst conjures a lasting blizzard zone, cutting enemy elemental resistance by 40% and amplifying Melt reaction damage.

2. The Snezhnaya Storyline Unfolds

The Traveler's journey through the frozen nation will center on the Zapolyarny Palace, where the Tsaritsa is gathering the remaining Gnosis needed for her long-awaited confrontation with the Heavenly Principles.

3. Sandrone's Place Within The Fatui

One of the most reclusive Harbingers, Sandrone rarely appears in person, communicating almost exclusively through the mechanical puppets she engineers herself. Version 7.0's story hints at a connection between her engineering genius and lost technology from the fallen civilization of Khaenri'ah — a thread that could prove significant for the franchise's overarching plot.

4. Recommended Teams & Release Window

As a support-leaning Cryo damage dealer, Sandrone is expected to shine alongside Pyro units for Melt reactions or Hydro units for Freeze compositions. HoYoverse has confirmed her limited banner will go live in the first half of Version 7.0, alongside a brand-new Snezhnaya region map.
