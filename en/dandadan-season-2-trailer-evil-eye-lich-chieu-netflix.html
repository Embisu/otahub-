Dandadan Season 2: Science SARU Drops Official Evil Eye Arc Trailer & Global Netflix Date

Science SARU officially releases the mind-bending trailer for Dandadan Season 2, kicking off the Evil Eye Arc alongside a worldwide Netflix premiere schedule.

🕒 07/09/2026 ✍️ Tik (Khanh Quynh) ⏱️ 6 min read
⚡ Dandadan Season 2: Evil Eye Arc Key Highlights
  • Official Season 2 Trailer: Science SARU debuts the terrifying visual aesthetic of the cursed Evil Eye demon and hyper-kinetic chase sequences.
  • Electrifying Soundtrack: Creepy Nuts and composer Kensuke Ushio return to produce adrenaline-pumping opening tracks and OST beats.
  • Character Dynamics: Deeper emotional evolution between Momo Ayase, Okarun, and childhood friend Jiji in the cursed Kitano estate.
  • Global Simulcast: Premiering simultaneously worldwide on Netflix and Crunchyroll in October 2026.

Following its monumental worldwide success during its debut season, Yukinobu Tatsu's smash-hit supernatural anime Dandadan has officially returned with an electrifying trailer for Dandadan Season 2, animated by the visionary studio Science SARU.

Entering the Terrifying Cursed Manor: The Evil Eye Arc

The Season 2 trailer transports viewers to a remote hot-spring town and the unsettling haunted estate of Jiji (Jin Enjoji), Momo Ayase’s childhood friend. There, the trio uncovers ancient dark secrets and awakens one of the series' most terrifying spiritual threats: The Evil Eye.

Science SARU’s signature psychedelic visuals, ink-wash distortion effects, and fluid neon palette bring the Evil Eye to life with visceral horror. Okarun’s high-velocity Turbo Granny transformations combine with Momo’s psychokinetic grasp in breathtaking battle sequences.

Catch more top anime news in our coverage of Bleach: TYBW Part 4 Trailer and Frieren Season 2 Aureole Arc.

Legendary Production Team & Creepy Nuts Opening Theme

Director Fuga Yamashiro returns alongside acclaimed composer Kensuke Ushio (Chainsaw Man, Devilman Crybaby). Chart-topping hip-hop duo Creepy Nuts is confirmed once again to deliver an addictive, high-tempo opening theme following the viral success of Otonoke.

Global Premiere Date & Streaming Platforms

Dandadan Season 2 will launch globally in October 2026, streaming with official multi-language subtitles and dubs on Netflix and Crunchyroll. Browse more in-depth anime coverage at our Anime Hub and read our latest OtaHub Reviews.
